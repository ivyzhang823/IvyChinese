第十一课 · Lesson 11

看来我没办法解决这个问题

Kànlái wǒ méi bànfǎ jiějué zhège wèntí

It seems I can't solve this problem

Text 1 · 在办公室里
In the office — Wang Yixue & Yang Tongle discuss the upcoming meeting
同乐
会议定了几点开始？ Huìyì dìng le jǐ diǎn kāishǐ? When has the meeting been set to start?
一雪
后天上午十点，还在第一会议室。不过经理说，今天下午先开个小会。 Hòutiān shàngwǔ shí diǎn, hái zài dì yī huìyìshì. Búguò jīnglǐ shuō, jīntiān xiàwǔ xiān kāi gè xiǎo huì. Day after tomorrow at 10 a.m., still in the first meeting room. But the manager says we'll have a short meeting this afternoon first.
同乐
开会的时候，咱们用会议室的电脑还是自己的笔记本电脑？ Kāihuì de shíhou, zánmen yòng huìyìshì de diànnǎo háishi zìjǐ de bǐjìběn diànnǎo? During the meeting, should we use the meeting room's computer or our own laptops?
一雪
用会议室的吧，这样可以互相发文件，或者一起看资料。 Yòng huìyìshì de ba, zhèyàng kěyǐ hùxiāng fā wénjiàn, huòzhě yīqǐ kàn zīliào. Let's use the meeting room's—that way we can share files with each other, or look at materials together.
同乐
好，那我需要带什么？ Hǎo, nà wǒ xūyào dài shénme? Okay, what do I need to bring?
一雪
你帮我做会议记录就好，材料我已经发到大家邮箱了。 Nǐ bāng wǒ zuò huìyì jìlù jiù hǎo, cáiliào wǒ yǐjīng fā dào dàjiā yóuxiāng le. Just help me take the meeting notes—I've already sent the materials to everyone's email.
Text 2 · 在会议室里
In the meeting room — the computer has no sound
同乐
电脑好了吗？声音有了吗？ Diànnǎo hǎo le ma? Shēngyīn yǒu le ma? Is the computer working? Is there sound now?
一雪
我试了好几次，还是没有声音。 Wǒ shì le hǎo jǐ cì, háishi méiyǒu shēngyīn. I've tried many times, but there's still no sound.
同乐
让我看看……你说几句话试试？ Ràng wǒ kànkan…… Nǐ shuō jǐ jù huà shìshi? Let me have a look... Say a few words to test it?
一雪
喂，喂，能听见吗？ Wèi, wèi, néng tīngjiàn ma? Hello, hello, can you hear me?
同乐
声音有点儿小，听不清楚。 Shēngyīn yǒudiǎnr xiǎo, tīng bù qīngchǔ. The sound is a bit quiet; I can't hear it clearly.
一雪
看来我没办法解决这个问题，只能找别人来帮忙了。 Kànlái wǒ méi bànfǎ jiějué zhège wèntí, zhǐ néng zhǎo biéren lái bāngmáng le. It seems I can't solve this problem—I'll have to find someone else to help.
Text 3 · 在办公室里
In the office — colleagues away, heavy workload
一雪
同乐，你看见老张了吗？ Tónglè, nǐ kànjiàn Lǎo Zhāng le ma? Tongle, have you seen Old Zhang?
同乐
老张没来，他说家里有点儿事，请假了。 Lǎo Zhāng méi lái, tā shuō jiālǐ yǒudiǎnr shì, qǐngjià le. Old Zhang isn't in. He said something came up at home and asked for the day off.
一雪
好几个同事都休假了，这么多工作我一个人忙不完。 Hǎo jǐ gè tóngshì dōu xiūjià le, zhème duō gōngzuò wǒ yī gè rén máng bù wán. Several colleagues are on vacation. I can't handle all this work by myself.
同乐
都十二点多了，咱们先去吃饭，下午我帮你把工作做完。 Dōu shí'èr diǎn duō le, zánmen xiān qù chīfàn, xiàwǔ wǒ bāng nǐ bǎ gōngzuò zuò wán. It's already past noon. Let's eat first, and I'll help you finish the work this afternoon.
一雪
不行，我很怕来不及。今天还要把报告发到公司邮箱。 Bùxíng, wǒ hěn pà lái bù jí. Jīntiān hái yào bǎ bàogào fā dào gōngsī yóuxiāng. That won't do—I'm worried about running out of time. I still need to send the report to the company inbox today.
同乐
你没看到邮件吗？经理还没回来，下周才能开会。 Nǐ méi kàndào yóujiàn ma? Jīnglǐ hái méi huílái, xià zhōu cái néng kāihuì. Didn't you see the email? The manager hasn't returned yet—the meeting can only happen next week.
一雪
那不着急了，走，吃饭去！ Nà bù zháojí le, zǒu, chīfàn qù! Oh, no need to rush then! Come on, let's go eat!
Text 4 · 在家里·日记
At home — Wang Yixue's diary: work vs. family
一雪
前几天，上海的同事来北京了。吃饭的时候，他们告诉我，公司有不少同事去上海工作了，问我愿不愿意也去上海。我是这样想的：父母和弟弟都在北京，刘明的工作很忙，孩子们也还小。如果我去别的城市工作，就要离开家人了。虽然去上海可能会有更好的机会，或者工资也更高，但是对我来说，生活比工作更重要，我不愿意为了工作离开家人。 Qián jǐ tiān, Shànghǎi de tóngshì lái Běijīng le. Chīfàn de shíhou, tāmen gàosù wǒ, gōngsī yǒu bù shǎo tóngshì qù Shànghǎi gōngzuò le, wèn wǒ yuàn bú yuànyì yě qù Shànghǎi. Wǒ shì zhèyàng xiǎng de: fùmǔ hé dìdi dōu zài Běijīng, Liú Míng de gōngzuò hěn máng, háizimen yě hái xiǎo. Rúguǒ wǒ qù bié de chéngshì gōngzuò, jiù yào líkāi jiārén le. Suīrán qù Shànghǎi kěnéng huì yǒu gèng hǎo de jīhuì, huò gōngzī yě gèng gāo, dànshì duì wǒ lái shuō, shēnghuó bǐ gōngzuò gèng zhòngyào, wǒ bú yuànyì wèile gōngzuò líkāi jiārén. A few days ago, colleagues from Shanghai came to Beijing. While we were having dinner, they told me that quite a few colleagues had moved to Shanghai to work, and asked whether I'd be willing to go too. Here is how I think about it: my parents and brother are all in Beijing, Liu Ming's work keeps him very busy, and the kids are still young. If I went to work in another city, I'd have to leave my family. Although going to Shanghai might offer better opportunities, or perhaps a higher salary, for me life is more important than work—I'm not willing to leave my family for the sake of work.

1还是 vs 或者 — Which "or"?
Both translate as "or," but they belong in different sentence types: 还是 goes in questions (offering a choice), while 或者 goes in statements (listing alternatives). Using one where the other belongs sounds unnatural.
疑问句 (question) → 还是
陈述句 (statement) → 或者
开会时咱们用会议室的电脑还是笔记本电脑？
During the meeting, should we use the meeting room's computer or our laptops? (choice question → 还是)
你可以发邮件或者打电话联系我。
You can contact me by email or phone. (statement listing options → 或者)
你去上海还是留在北京？
Are you going to Shanghai or staying in Beijing? (question → 还是)
去上海可能有更好的机会，或者工资也更高。
Going to Shanghai might offer better opportunities, or a higher salary too. (statement → 或者)
2看来 — Drawing a Conclusion
The fixed phrase 看来 introduces a conclusion or inference that the speaker draws from observation or reasoning. It typically opens a sentence or clause. It conveys "from what I can see / given the situation, it seems that…"
【看来】＋ conclusion
S ＋ 【看来】＋ conclusion
看来我没办法解决这个问题，只能找别人帮忙了。
It seems I can't solve this problem—I'll have to find someone else to help.
这么晚了，看来经理今天不回来了。
It's so late; apparently the manager isn't coming back today.
你都把材料发好了，看来准备得很充分。
You've already sent all the materials — it seems you're well prepared.
3把字句 (三) — Result Complement
A 把 sentence can show that an action produces a result. After the verb, add a result complement — a verb or adjective that tells you what happened to the object as a consequence of the action.
S ＋ 把 ＋ O ＋ V ＋ 结果补语 (result complement)
下午我帮你把工作做完。
This afternoon I'll help you get the work finished.
今天还要把报告发到公司邮箱。
I still need to send the report to the company inbox today.
请你把材料准备好。
Please get the materials ready.
他把开会时间改了。
He changed the meeting time.
4对……来说 — "For … it …"
The fixed pattern 对……来说 sets the frame of reference — it indicates the person or group for whom a statement applies. Think of it as "as far as [X] is concerned" or "for [X]."
对 ＋ [person / group] ＋ 来说，＋ [statement]
对我来说，生活比工作更重要。
For me, life is more important than work.
对他来说，学中文不难。
For him, learning Chinese isn't hard.
对年轻人来说，有更好的机会很重要。
For young people, having better opportunities is very important.
对公司来说，开好每一次会议都很重要。
For the company, running every meeting well is very important.

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国的职场文化 Chinese Workplace Culture
在中国，职场文化（zhíchǎng wénhuà）有几个明显的特点。首先，会议（huìyì）非常重要，开会前通常要提前准备好材料（cáiliào）。其次，上下级关系（shàngxiàjí guānxi）很受重视——下级通常先听上级的意见。此外，请假（qǐngjià）需要提前向上级申请，直接告知原因，并写好请假条。在许多中国公司，员工还有"加班文化"，常常自愿留在公司工作到很晚。随着时代的变化，越来越多的年轻人开始重视工作与生活的平衡（pínghéng）。
Chinese workplace culture has several distinctive characteristics. First, meetings are very important — materials must be prepared in advance. Second, hierarchical relationships are highly valued: subordinates typically listen to their superiors' opinions first. Additionally, taking leave requires advance approval from a manager, with a clear reason stated and a leave application submitted. In many Chinese companies, there is also a culture of "overtime" — employees often voluntarily stay late. As times change, more and more young people are beginning to value work-life balance.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
还是 vs 或者 vs 或 Three Ways to Say "Or"
① 还是 (háishi)：用于疑问句，表示两者之间选择一个。
例：你喝茶还是咖啡？（你必须选一个）

② 或者 (huòzhě)：用于陈述句，表示两者均可，不强迫选择。
例：周末我们可以去爬山，或者去游泳。（两个都可以）

③ 或 (huò)：书面语，常见于正式文件、条款或书面表达，功能与"或者"相似但更简洁。
例：请带身份证或护照。（书面文件风格）

总结：问句用还是，陈述句用或者，书面语用或。
① 还是 (háishi): Used in questions — choosing between two options (the listener must pick one).
E.g., Would you like tea or coffee? (you must choose)

② 或者 (huòzhě): Used in statements — both options are acceptable, no forced choice.
E.g., On the weekend we can go hiking or swimming. (either is fine)

③ 或 (huò): Written/formal register — found in official documents, rules, and formal writing. Functions like 或者 but more concise.
E.g., Please bring your ID card or passport. (formal written style)

Summary: Use 还是 in questions, 或者 in statements, 或 in formal/written contexts.
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
看来 vs 好像 vs 似乎 Three Ways to Express Inference
三个词都表示"based on what I see/know, I conclude that…"，但用法有细微差别：

看来 (kànlái)：常位于句首，说话人根据观察作出有把握的推断，语气较肯定。
例：电脑一直没有声音，看来是哪里出了问题。

好像 (hǎoxiàng)：语气较软，表示"看起来像是……"，可以是主观感觉，不太确定。
例：她好像不太高兴。（也许是，也许不是）

似乎 (sìhū)：书面语，语气介于两者之间，正式场合或写作中较常用。
例：他似乎知道这件事，但没有说出来。

口诀：肯定用看来，委婉用好像，书面用似乎。
All three express "based on what I observe/know, I infer that…" but with subtle differences:

看来 (kànlái): Often sentence-initial; the speaker makes a fairly confident inference from observation.
E.g., The computer has had no sound all along — it seems something is wrong with it.

好像 (hǎoxiàng): Softer tone; "looks like / seems as if" — more subjective and uncertain.
E.g., She seems not very happy. (maybe, maybe not)

似乎 (sìhū): Written register; tone between the other two; more common in formal writing.
E.g., He seemed to know about it but didn't say anything.

Rule of thumb: Use 看来 for confident inferences, 好像 for tentative ones, 似乎 in written/formal contexts.

北京的四季 Beijing's Four Seasons
北京位于中国北方，属于典型的温带大陆性气候，四季分明。春天（三月至五月）天气变化快，早晚温差大，沙尘暴（shāchén bào）偶尔出现，但樱花（yīnghuā）和玉兰花（yùlán huā）盛开，非常漂亮。夏天（六月至八月）炎热多雨，七八月常有"雷阵雨（léizhènyǔ）"。秋天（九月至十一月）被很多人认为是北京最美的季节，天高云淡，香山（Xiāng Shān）的红叶特别有名。冬天（十二月至二月）寒冷干燥，下雪时景色很美，但雾霾（wùmái）也是困扰北京的一个问题。
Beijing, in northern China, has a typical temperate continental climate with four distinct seasons. Spring (March–May) brings rapid temperature swings and occasional sandstorms, but cherry blossoms and magnolias make it beautiful. Summer (June–August) is hot and rainy, with frequent thunderstorms in July and August. Autumn (September–November) is considered by many to be Beijing's most beautiful season — the sky is crisp and clear, and the red leaves at Fragrant Hills Park are especially famous. Winter (December–February) is cold and dry; snowfall creates gorgeous scenery, though smog (haze) remains a challenge for the city.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
起来 vs 下来 vs 下去 Three Direction Complements
三个趋向补语的引申用法经常让学习者感到困惑：

① V + 起来：动作开始发生，强调"开始"。
例：下起雨来了。（It started raining.）| 唱起歌来了。（started singing）

② V + 下来：动作完成，强调经过努力、克服困难"坚持到底"。
例：这本书我终于看下来了。（managed to finish the book）| 住下来了。（settled in）

③ V + 下去：动作继续进行，强调"持续下去"（不一定结束）。
例：继续说下去。（keep talking）| 这样下去不行。（can't keep going like this）

口诀：起来=开始，下来=完成，下去=继续。
Three extended directional complements that often confuse learners:

① V + 起来: An action is beginning to happen — emphasis on starting.
E.g., It started raining. | They started singing.

② V + 下来: An action has been completed — emphasis on persisting through difficulty to finish.
E.g., I finally managed to read through the whole book. | She settled in.

③ V + 下去: An action continues — emphasis on keeping going (not necessarily finishing).
E.g., Keep talking. | We can't go on like this.

Rule of thumb: 起来 = start, 下来 = complete, 下去 = continue.

Chinese weather expressions follow fixed patterns:

下雨/下雪: rain/snow "falls" — 下 (down) is the verb used, not 落.

打雷 / 闪电: thunder / lightning

1 · 请客 vs 做客
These two look similar but mean the opposite! 请客 (qǐng kè) means to be the host — you invite someone and pay the bill. 做客 (zuò kè) means to be the guest — you visit someone else's home. Remember: 请 = invite (you're in charge); 做 = act as (you're the visitor).
今天我请客，大家随便点！Today I'm treating everyone — order whatever you like!
我打算去邻居家做客。I plan to visit the neighbors' home as a guest.
下次你来北京，我一定请你吃北京烤鸭。Next time you come to Beijing, I'll definitely treat you to Peking duck.
2 · 如果……的话 — "If…"
如果 (rúguǒ) introduces a condition. 的话 (de huà) is an optional marker at the end of the conditional clause that softens the tone. The main clause often uses 就 (jiù) to signal the result. You can use 如果 alone or with 的话, but the two always go together if both appear.
如果 ＋ [condition] ＋ （的话），（就）＋ [result]
如果你有时间的话，我们就一起去爬山吧。If you have time, let's go hiking together.
如果他们喜欢中国菜的话，你就多做几道。If they like Chinese food, just make more dishes.
如果明天下雨的话，我们就不去了。If it rains tomorrow, we won't go.
3 · 不同 vs 一样 — "Different" vs "the Same"
不同 (bùtóng) means "different / not the same" and is used as an adjective or predicate. 一样 (yīyàng) means "the same" and often appears in the pattern A 跟 B 一样 or A 和 B 不一样. Note: 不同 is slightly more formal than 不一样.
南方菜和北方菜的做法不同，味道也不一样。Southern and northern cooking methods are different, and so are the flavors.
他们两个人的想法不一样。The two of them have different ideas.
我跟你的意见完全一样。My opinion is exactly the same as yours.
4 · 一边……一边…… — Simultaneous Actions
一边……一边…… (yībiān … yībiān …) means doing two things at the same time. Both verbs should be actions that can realistically happen together. The subject does not need to be repeated. Use this with two verb phrases, not two nouns.
[Subject] ＋ 一边 ＋ V₁ ＋ 一边 ＋ V₂
他一边听音乐，一边画画儿。He listens to music while drawing.
我们一边吃饭，一边聊天。We chat while eating.
她一边走路，一边看手机。She looks at her phone while walking.
5 · 来自 vs 从……来 — Two Ways to Express Origin
Both express where someone or something comes from, but they differ in structure and register. 来自 (láizì) is a single verb meaning "come from / originate from" — more formal, used for permanent origin or background. 从……来 (cóng … lái) is a prepositional phrase meaning "come from [a place]" — focuses on movement and is more colloquial.
他来自英国。He comes from the UK. (his origin/background)
他是从英国来的。He came here from the UK. (he traveled here)
这种茶来自中国云南。This tea comes from Yunnan, China. (origin)
6 · 文化 — Culture
文化 (wénhuà) literally combines 文 (writing, civilization) and 化 (transformation, becoming). It covers culture, civilization, and also educational level in informal usage (e.g., 他没有文化 = "he has little education"). In everyday speech, 文化 is broad — from art and traditions to way of life. Common collocations include 中国文化, 传统文化 (traditional culture), 文化差异 (cultural difference).
他对东方文化很感兴趣。He is very interested in Eastern culture.
中西方文化差异很大。The cultural differences between China and the West are significant.
学语言的时候，了解那个国家的文化也很重要。When learning a language, understanding that country's culture is also very important.
1如果……的话，就…… — Hypothetical Condition
如果 (rúguǒ) introduces a hypothetical "if" condition. 的话 (de huà) at the end of the conditional clause is optional but adds a softer, more conversational tone. The result clause typically uses 就 (jiù, "then") to connect. This is one of the most common conditional patterns in Mandarin.
如果 ＋ [Condition] ＋ （的话），[Subject] ＋ 就 ＋ [Result]
如果你有时间的话，就来我家玩儿吧。
If you have time, come hang out at my place.
如果他们喜欢中国菜的话，你就多做几道。
If they like Chinese food, just make a few extra dishes.
如果明天天气好的话，我们就去公园。
If the weather is nice tomorrow, we'll go to the park.
如果你不明白的话，就再看一遍。
If you don't understand, read it again.
2把字句 (4) — with Directional / Resultative Complements
The 把 (bǎ) construction emphasizes what happens to a definite object as a result of the action. In this lesson, 把 is used with directional complements (e.g., 放到…上/里/下) and resultative complements (e.g., 放好, 搬来, 拿出来), showing where the object ends up or what state it reaches.
[Subject] ＋ 把 ＋ [Object] ＋ V ＋ 到/在 ＋ [Location]
[Subject] ＋ 把 ＋ [Object] ＋ V ＋ [Result Complement]
把酒放到桌子上。
Put the wine on the table.
把盘子放好，一会儿要用。
Set the plates neatly — we'll need them soon.
把蛋糕放到冰箱里。
Put the cake in the fridge.
请把那张桌子搬到客厅中间。
Please move that table to the middle of the living room.
3前缀/后缀 — 老/小 and 家/师/员
Chinese uses prefixes and suffixes to modify nouns, especially for people. 老 (lǎo) and 小 (xiǎo) as prefixes before surnames show familiarity or seniority. As suffixes, 家 (jiā = expert), 师 (shī = teacher/specialist), and 员 (yuán = member/worker) form professional nouns.
老/小 ＋ Surname → informal address by seniority
Noun ＋ 家 → expert in a field
Noun ＋ 师 → specialist / teacher
Noun ＋ 员 → member / staff
老张今天不来了。/ 小李，你来一下。
Old Zhang isn't coming today. / Young Li, come here a moment.
他是一位有名的画家。
He is a famous painter. (画 + 家)
她是我们学校的音乐老师。
She is our school's music teacher. (老 + 师)
他在银行做工作人员。
He works as a bank staff member. (人 + 员)
4一边……一边…… — Simultaneous Actions
一边……一边…… (yībiān … yībiān …) describes two actions happening at the same time by the same subject. Both slots take verb phrases. The two actions should be physically compatible (e.g., walking and talking). This pattern only works for deliberate, ongoing actions — not instantaneous ones.
[Subject] ＋ 一边 ＋ V₁ (+ Obj₁) ＋ 一边 ＋ V₂ (+ Obj₂)
小明一边听音乐，一边画画儿。
Xiǎo Míng listens to music while drawing.
我们一边吃饭，一边聊天。
We chat while eating.
她总是喜欢一边走路一边看手机。
She always likes looking at her phone while walking.
他们一边喝红茶，一边聊东方文化。
They chatted about Eastern culture while drinking black tea.

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国的请客文化 Chinese Hosting Culture
在中国，"请客"（qǐng kè）是一种重要的社交活动。主人通常会事先准备很多菜，热情招待客人。去别人家做客时，带礼物（lǐwù）是基本礼貌，常见的礼物有水果、茶叶、酒或者小点心。吃饭时，主人会不断给客人夹菜（jiā cài），表示热情。客人一般不会第一个动筷子，要等主人先邀请。在中国，饭桌是联系感情、增进友谊的重要场所。与朋友一起吃饭，不仅仅是为了填饱肚子，更是为了增进感情。
In China, "qǐng kè" (treating guests) is an important social activity. Hosts typically prepare many dishes in advance and welcome guests warmly. When visiting someone's home as a guest, bringing a gift is basic courtesy — common gifts include fruit, tea, wine, or small snacks. During the meal, the host will keep offering food to guests as a sign of warmth. Guests generally don't pick up their chopsticks first — they wait for the host's invitation. In China, the dining table is an important place for bonding and building friendships. Eating together with friends is not just about satisfying hunger — it is about deepening relationships.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
红茶 vs 绿茶 vs 茉莉花茶 Three Types of Chinese Tea
① 绿茶 (lǜchá)：未经发酵，保留了茶叶的天然色泽和清爽香气。代表：龙井茶（Lóngjǐng chá）、碧螺春（Bìluóchūn）。

② 红茶 (hóngchá)：经过完全发酵，颜色深红，味道浓郁，在西方也很受欢迎。英语叫"black tea"（黑茶），但中文叫"红茶"（red tea）。

③ 茉莉花茶 (mòlì huāchá)：绿茶与茉莉花混合，香气迷人，在中国北方特别流行。

注意：英语的"black tea"翻译成中文是"红茶"，不是"黑茶"！
① Green tea (绿茶): Unfermented, retaining the natural color and fresh aroma of the leaves. Notable examples: Longjing (Dragon Well), Biluochun.

② Black tea (红茶): Fully fermented, deep red in color, rich in flavor, and popular in the West. In English it's called "black tea," but in Chinese it's called 红茶 (red tea).

③ Jasmine tea (茉莉花茶): A blend of green tea and jasmine flowers, with a captivating fragrance, especially popular in northern China.

Note: The English "black tea" translates to 红茶 in Chinese — not 黑茶!
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
来自 vs 是……人 vs 从……来 Three Ways to Express Origin
三种方式都可以表示"来自哪里"，但用法有细微区别：

来自 (láizì)：正式一些，强调出身或背景。
例：他来自英国。（他的背景/国籍是英国）

是……人 (shì … rén)：最口语的方式，直接说国籍或地区身份。
例：他是英国人。（他的国籍是英国）

从……来 (cóng … lái)：强调动作，"从某地来到这里"。
例：他从英国来的。（他从英国来了，来到了中国）

总结：身份/背景用"来自/是…人"，移动/行程用"从…来"。
All three express "where someone is from," but with subtle differences:

来自 (láizì): Slightly more formal, emphasizes origin or background.
E.g.: He comes from the UK. (his nationality/background)

是……人 (shì … rén): Most colloquial, directly states nationality or regional identity.
E.g.: He is British. (his nationality is British)

从……来 (cóng … lái): Emphasizes movement — "came here from somewhere."
E.g.: He came from the UK. (he traveled here from the UK)

Summary: Use 来自/是…人 for identity/background; use 从…来 for travel/movement.
