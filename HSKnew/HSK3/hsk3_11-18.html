第十一课 · Lesson 11

看来我没办法解决这个问题

Kànlái wǒ méi bànfǎ jiějué zhège wèntí

It seems I can't solve this problem

Text 1 · 在办公室里
In the office — Wang Yixue & Yang Tongle discuss the upcoming meeting
同乐
会议定了几点开始？ Huìyì dìng le jǐ diǎn kāishǐ? When has the meeting been set to start?
一雪
后天上午十点，还在第一会议室。不过经理说，今天下午先开个小会。 Hòutiān shàngwǔ shí diǎn, hái zài dì yī huìyìshì. Búguò jīnglǐ shuō, jīntiān xiàwǔ xiān kāi gè xiǎo huì. Day after tomorrow at 10 a.m., still in the first meeting room. But the manager says we'll have a short meeting this afternoon first.
同乐
开会的时候，咱们用会议室的电脑还是自己的笔记本电脑？ Kāihuì de shíhou, zánmen yòng huìyìshì de diànnǎo háishi zìjǐ de bǐjìběn diànnǎo? During the meeting, should we use the meeting room's computer or our own laptops?
一雪
用会议室的吧，这样可以互相发文件，或者一起看资料。 Yòng huìyìshì de ba, zhèyàng kěyǐ hùxiāng fā wénjiàn, huòzhě yīqǐ kàn zīliào. Let's use the meeting room's—that way we can share files with each other, or look at materials together.
同乐
好，那我需要带什么？ Hǎo, nà wǒ xūyào dài shénme? Okay, what do I need to bring?
一雪
你帮我做会议记录就好，材料我已经发到大家邮箱了。 Nǐ bāng wǒ zuò huìyì jìlù jiù hǎo, cáiliào wǒ yǐjīng fā dào dàjiā yóuxiāng le. Just help me take the meeting notes—I've already sent the materials to everyone's email.
Text 2 · 在会议室里
In the meeting room — the computer has no sound
同乐
电脑好了吗？声音有了吗？ Diànnǎo hǎo le ma? Shēngyīn yǒu le ma? Is the computer working? Is there sound now?
一雪
我试了好几次，还是没有声音。 Wǒ shì le hǎo jǐ cì, háishi méiyǒu shēngyīn. I've tried many times, but there's still no sound.
同乐
让我看看……你说几句话试试？ Ràng wǒ kànkan…… Nǐ shuō jǐ jù huà shìshi? Let me have a look... Say a few words to test it?
一雪
喂，喂，能听见吗？ Wèi, wèi, néng tīngjiàn ma? Hello, hello, can you hear me?
同乐
声音有点儿小，听不清楚。 Shēngyīn yǒudiǎnr xiǎo, tīng bù qīngchǔ. The sound is a bit quiet; I can't hear it clearly.
一雪
看来我没办法解决这个问题，只能找别人来帮忙了。 Kànlái wǒ méi bànfǎ jiějué zhège wèntí, zhǐ néng zhǎo biéren lái bāngmáng le. It seems I can't solve this problem—I'll have to find someone else to help.
Text 3 · 在办公室里
In the office — colleagues away, heavy workload
一雪
同乐，你看见老张了吗？ Tónglè, nǐ kànjiàn Lǎo Zhāng le ma? Tongle, have you seen Old Zhang?
同乐
老张没来，他说家里有点儿事，请假了。 Lǎo Zhāng méi lái, tā shuō jiālǐ yǒudiǎnr shì, qǐngjià le. Old Zhang isn't in. He said something came up at home and asked for the day off.
一雪
好几个同事都休假了，这么多工作我一个人忙不完。 Hǎo jǐ gè tóngshì dōu xiūjià le, zhème duō gōngzuò wǒ yī gè rén máng bù wán. Several colleagues are on vacation. I can't handle all this work by myself.
同乐
都十二点多了，咱们先去吃饭，下午我帮你把工作做完。 Dōu shí'èr diǎn duō le, zánmen xiān qù chīfàn, xiàwǔ wǒ bāng nǐ bǎ gōngzuò zuò wán. It's already past noon. Let's eat first, and I'll help you finish the work this afternoon.
一雪
不行，我很怕来不及。今天还要把报告发到公司邮箱。 Bùxíng, wǒ hěn pà lái bù jí. Jīntiān hái yào bǎ bàogào fā dào gōngsī yóuxiāng. That won't do—I'm worried about running out of time. I still need to send the report to the company inbox today.
同乐
你没看到邮件吗？经理还没回来，下周才能开会。 Nǐ méi kàndào yóujiàn ma? Jīnglǐ hái méi huílái, xià zhōu cái néng kāihuì. Didn't you see the email? The manager hasn't returned yet—the meeting can only happen next week.
一雪
那不着急了，走，吃饭去！ Nà bù zháojí le, zǒu, chīfàn qù! Oh, no need to rush then! Come on, let's go eat!
Text 4 · 在家里·日记
At home — Wang Yixue's diary: work vs. family
一雪
前几天，上海的同事来北京了。吃饭的时候，他们告诉我，公司有不少同事去上海工作了，问我愿不愿意也去上海。我是这样想的：父母和弟弟都在北京，刘明的工作很忙，孩子们也还小。如果我去别的城市工作，就要离开家人了。虽然去上海可能会有更好的机会，或者工资也更高，但是对我来说，生活比工作更重要，我不愿意为了工作离开家人。 Qián jǐ tiān, Shànghǎi de tóngshì lái Běijīng le. Chīfàn de shíhou, tāmen gàosù wǒ, gōngsī yǒu bù shǎo tóngshì qù Shànghǎi gōngzuò le, wèn wǒ yuàn bú yuànyì yě qù Shànghǎi. Wǒ shì zhèyàng xiǎng de: fùmǔ hé dìdi dōu zài Běijīng, Liú Míng de gōngzuò hěn máng, háizimen yě hái xiǎo. Rúguǒ wǒ qù bié de chéngshì gōngzuò, jiù yào líkāi jiārén le. Suīrán qù Shànghǎi kěnéng huì yǒu gèng hǎo de jīhuì, huò gōngzī yě gèng gāo, dànshì duì wǒ lái shuō, shēnghuó bǐ gōngzuò gèng zhòngyào, wǒ bú yuànyì wèile gōngzuò líkāi jiārén. A few days ago, colleagues from Shanghai came to Beijing. While we were having dinner, they told me that quite a few colleagues had moved to Shanghai to work, and asked whether I'd be willing to go too. Here is how I think about it: my parents and brother are all in Beijing, Liu Ming's work keeps him very busy, and the kids are still young. If I went to work in another city, I'd have to leave my family. Although going to Shanghai might offer better opportunities, or perhaps a higher salary, for me life is more important than work—I'm not willing to leave my family for the sake of work.

1还是 vs 或者 — Which "or"?
Both translate as "or," but they belong in different sentence types: 还是 goes in questions (offering a choice), while 或者 goes in statements (listing alternatives). Using one where the other belongs sounds unnatural.
疑问句 (question) → 还是
陈述句 (statement) → 或者
开会时咱们用会议室的电脑还是笔记本电脑？
During the meeting, should we use the meeting room's computer or our laptops? (choice question → 还是)
你可以发邮件或者打电话联系我。
You can contact me by email or phone. (statement listing options → 或者)
你去上海还是留在北京？
Are you going to Shanghai or staying in Beijing? (question → 还是)
去上海可能有更好的机会，或者工资也更高。
Going to Shanghai might offer better opportunities, or a higher salary too. (statement → 或者)
2看来 — Drawing a Conclusion
The fixed phrase 看来 introduces a conclusion or inference that the speaker draws from observation or reasoning. It typically opens a sentence or clause. It conveys "from what I can see / given the situation, it seems that…"
【看来】＋ conclusion
S ＋ 【看来】＋ conclusion
看来我没办法解决这个问题，只能找别人帮忙了。
It seems I can't solve this problem—I'll have to find someone else to help.
这么晚了，看来经理今天不回来了。
It's so late; apparently the manager isn't coming back today.
你都把材料发好了，看来准备得很充分。
You've already sent all the materials — it seems you're well prepared.
3把字句 (三) — Result Complement
A 把 sentence can show that an action produces a result. After the verb, add a result complement — a verb or adjective that tells you what happened to the object as a consequence of the action.
S ＋ 把 ＋ O ＋ V ＋ 结果补语 (result complement)
下午我帮你把工作做完。
This afternoon I'll help you get the work finished.
今天还要把报告发到公司邮箱。
I still need to send the report to the company inbox today.
请你把材料准备好。
Please get the materials ready.
他把开会时间改了。
He changed the meeting time.
4对……来说 — "For … it …"
The fixed pattern 对……来说 sets the frame of reference — it indicates the person or group for whom a statement applies. Think of it as "as far as [X] is concerned" or "for [X]."
对 ＋ [person / group] ＋ 来说，＋ [statement]
对我来说，生活比工作更重要。
For me, life is more important than work.
对他来说，学中文不难。
For him, learning Chinese isn't hard.
对年轻人来说，有更好的机会很重要。
For young people, having better opportunities is very important.
对公司来说，开好每一次会议都很重要。
For the company, running every meeting well is very important.

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国的职场文化 Chinese Workplace Culture
在中国，职场文化（zhíchǎng wénhuà）有几个明显的特点。首先，会议（huìyì）非常重要，开会前通常要提前准备好材料（cáiliào）。其次，上下级关系（shàngxiàjí guānxi）很受重视——下级通常先听上级的意见。此外，请假（qǐngjià）需要提前向上级申请，直接告知原因，并写好请假条。在许多中国公司，员工还有"加班文化"，常常自愿留在公司工作到很晚。随着时代的变化，越来越多的年轻人开始重视工作与生活的平衡（pínghéng）。
Chinese workplace culture has several distinctive characteristics. First, meetings are very important — materials must be prepared in advance. Second, hierarchical relationships are highly valued: subordinates typically listen to their superiors' opinions first. Additionally, taking leave requires advance approval from a manager, with a clear reason stated and a leave application submitted. In many Chinese companies, there is also a culture of "overtime" — employees often voluntarily stay late. As times change, more and more young people are beginning to value work-life balance.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
还是 vs 或者 vs 或 Three Ways to Say "Or"
① 还是 (háishi)：用于疑问句，表示两者之间选择一个。
例：你喝茶还是咖啡？（你必须选一个）

② 或者 (huòzhě)：用于陈述句，表示两者均可，不强迫选择。
例：周末我们可以去爬山，或者去游泳。（两个都可以）

③ 或 (huò)：书面语，常见于正式文件、条款或书面表达，功能与"或者"相似但更简洁。
例：请带身份证或护照。（书面文件风格）

总结：问句用还是，陈述句用或者，书面语用或。
① 还是 (háishi): Used in questions — choosing between two options (the listener must pick one).
E.g., Would you like tea or coffee? (you must choose)

② 或者 (huòzhě): Used in statements — both options are acceptable, no forced choice.
E.g., On the weekend we can go hiking or swimming. (either is fine)

③ 或 (huò): Written/formal register — found in official documents, rules, and formal writing. Functions like 或者 but more concise.
E.g., Please bring your ID card or passport. (formal written style)

Summary: Use 还是 in questions, 或者 in statements, 或 in formal/written contexts.
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
看来 vs 好像 vs 似乎 Three Ways to Express Inference
三个词都表示"based on what I see/know, I conclude that…"，但用法有细微差别：

看来 (kànlái)：常位于句首，说话人根据观察作出有把握的推断，语气较肯定。
例：电脑一直没有声音，看来是哪里出了问题。

好像 (hǎoxiàng)：语气较软，表示"看起来像是……"，可以是主观感觉，不太确定。
例：她好像不太高兴。（也许是，也许不是）

似乎 (sìhū)：书面语，语气介于两者之间，正式场合或写作中较常用。
例：他似乎知道这件事，但没有说出来。

口诀：肯定用看来，委婉用好像，书面用似乎。
All three express "based on what I observe/know, I infer that…" but with subtle differences:

看来 (kànlái): Often sentence-initial; the speaker makes a fairly confident inference from observation.
E.g., The computer has had no sound all along — it seems something is wrong with it.

好像 (hǎoxiàng): Softer tone; "looks like / seems as if" — more subjective and uncertain.
E.g., She seems not very happy. (maybe, maybe not)

似乎 (sìhū): Written register; tone between the other two; more common in formal writing.
E.g., He seemed to know about it but didn't say anything.

Rule of thumb: Use 看来 for confident inferences, 好像 for tentative ones, 似乎 in written/formal contexts.

北京的四季 Beijing's Four Seasons
北京位于中国北方，属于典型的温带大陆性气候，四季分明。春天（三月至五月）天气变化快，早晚温差大，沙尘暴（shāchén bào）偶尔出现，但樱花（yīnghuā）和玉兰花（yùlán huā）盛开，非常漂亮。夏天（六月至八月）炎热多雨，七八月常有"雷阵雨（léizhènyǔ）"。秋天（九月至十一月）被很多人认为是北京最美的季节，天高云淡，香山（Xiāng Shān）的红叶特别有名。冬天（十二月至二月）寒冷干燥，下雪时景色很美，但雾霾（wùmái）也是困扰北京的一个问题。
Beijing, in northern China, has a typical temperate continental climate with four distinct seasons. Spring (March–May) brings rapid temperature swings and occasional sandstorms, but cherry blossoms and magnolias make it beautiful. Summer (June–August) is hot and rainy, with frequent thunderstorms in July and August. Autumn (September–November) is considered by many to be Beijing's most beautiful season — the sky is crisp and clear, and the red leaves at Fragrant Hills Park are especially famous. Winter (December–February) is cold and dry; snowfall creates gorgeous scenery, though smog (haze) remains a challenge for the city.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
起来 vs 下来 vs 下去 Three Direction Complements
三个趋向补语的引申用法经常让学习者感到困惑：

① V + 起来：动作开始发生，强调"开始"。
例：下起雨来了。（It started raining.）| 唱起歌来了。（started singing）

② V + 下来：动作完成，强调经过努力、克服困难"坚持到底"。
例：这本书我终于看下来了。（managed to finish the book）| 住下来了。（settled in）

③ V + 下去：动作继续进行，强调"持续下去"（不一定结束）。
例：继续说下去。（keep talking）| 这样下去不行。（can't keep going like this）

口诀：起来=开始，下来=完成，下去=继续。
Three extended directional complements that often confuse learners:

① V + 起来: An action is beginning to happen — emphasis on starting.
E.g., It started raining. | They started singing.

② V + 下来: An action has been completed — emphasis on persisting through difficulty to finish.
E.g., I finally managed to read through the whole book. | She settled in.

③ V + 下去: An action continues — emphasis on keeping going (not necessarily finishing).
E.g., Keep talking. | We can't go on like this.

Rule of thumb: 起来 = start, 下来 = complete, 下去 = continue.

Chinese weather expressions follow fixed patterns:

下雨/下雪: rain/snow "falls" — 下 (down) is the verb used, not 落.

打雷 / 闪电: thunder / lightning

1 · 请客 vs 做客
These two look similar but mean the opposite! 请客 (qǐng kè) means to be the host — you invite someone and pay the bill. 做客 (zuò kè) means to be the guest — you visit someone else's home. Remember: 请 = invite (you're in charge); 做 = act as (you're the visitor).
今天我请客，大家随便点！Today I'm treating everyone — order whatever you like!
我打算去邻居家做客。I plan to visit the neighbors' home as a guest.
下次你来北京，我一定请你吃北京烤鸭。Next time you come to Beijing, I'll definitely treat you to Peking duck.
2 · 如果……的话 — "If…"
如果 (rúguǒ) introduces a condition. 的话 (de huà) is an optional marker at the end of the conditional clause that softens the tone. The main clause often uses 就 (jiù) to signal the result. You can use 如果 alone or with 的话, but the two always go together if both appear.
如果 ＋ [condition] ＋ （的话），（就）＋ [result]
如果你有时间的话，我们就一起去爬山吧。If you have time, let's go hiking together.
如果他们喜欢中国菜的话，你就多做几道。If they like Chinese food, just make more dishes.
如果明天下雨的话，我们就不去了。If it rains tomorrow, we won't go.
3 · 不同 vs 一样 — "Different" vs "the Same"
不同 (bùtóng) means "different / not the same" and is used as an adjective or predicate. 一样 (yīyàng) means "the same" and often appears in the pattern A 跟 B 一样 or A 和 B 不一样. Note: 不同 is slightly more formal than 不一样.
南方菜和北方菜的做法不同，味道也不一样。Southern and northern cooking methods are different, and so are the flavors.
他们两个人的想法不一样。The two of them have different ideas.
我跟你的意见完全一样。My opinion is exactly the same as yours.
4 · 一边……一边…… — Simultaneous Actions
一边……一边…… (yībiān … yībiān …) means doing two things at the same time. Both verbs should be actions that can realistically happen together. The subject does not need to be repeated. Use this with two verb phrases, not two nouns.
[Subject] ＋ 一边 ＋ V₁ ＋ 一边 ＋ V₂
他一边听音乐，一边画画儿。He listens to music while drawing.
我们一边吃饭，一边聊天。We chat while eating.
她一边走路，一边看手机。She looks at her phone while walking.
5 · 来自 vs 从……来 — Two Ways to Express Origin
Both express where someone or something comes from, but they differ in structure and register. 来自 (láizì) is a single verb meaning "come from / originate from" — more formal, used for permanent origin or background. 从……来 (cóng … lái) is a prepositional phrase meaning "come from [a place]" — focuses on movement and is more colloquial.
他来自英国。He comes from the UK. (his origin/background)
他是从英国来的。He came here from the UK. (he traveled here)
这种茶来自中国云南。This tea comes from Yunnan, China. (origin)
6 · 文化 — Culture
文化 (wénhuà) literally combines 文 (writing, civilization) and 化 (transformation, becoming). It covers culture, civilization, and also educational level in informal usage (e.g., 他没有文化 = "he has little education"). In everyday speech, 文化 is broad — from art and traditions to way of life. Common collocations include 中国文化, 传统文化 (traditional culture), 文化差异 (cultural difference).
他对东方文化很感兴趣。He is very interested in Eastern culture.
中西方文化差异很大。The cultural differences between China and the West are significant.
学语言的时候，了解那个国家的文化也很重要。When learning a language, understanding that country's culture is also very important.
1如果……的话，就…… — Hypothetical Condition
如果 (rúguǒ) introduces a hypothetical "if" condition. 的话 (de huà) at the end of the conditional clause is optional but adds a softer, more conversational tone. The result clause typically uses 就 (jiù, "then") to connect. This is one of the most common conditional patterns in Mandarin.
如果 ＋ [Condition] ＋ （的话），[Subject] ＋ 就 ＋ [Result]
如果你有时间的话，就来我家玩儿吧。
If you have time, come hang out at my place.
如果他们喜欢中国菜的话，你就多做几道。
If they like Chinese food, just make a few extra dishes.
如果明天天气好的话，我们就去公园。
If the weather is nice tomorrow, we'll go to the park.
如果你不明白的话，就再看一遍。
If you don't understand, read it again.
2把字句 (4) — with Directional / Resultative Complements
The 把 (bǎ) construction emphasizes what happens to a definite object as a result of the action. In this lesson, 把 is used with directional complements (e.g., 放到…上/里/下) and resultative complements (e.g., 放好, 搬来, 拿出来), showing where the object ends up or what state it reaches.
[Subject] ＋ 把 ＋ [Object] ＋ V ＋ 到/在 ＋ [Location]
[Subject] ＋ 把 ＋ [Object] ＋ V ＋ [Result Complement]
把酒放到桌子上。
Put the wine on the table.
把盘子放好，一会儿要用。
Set the plates neatly — we'll need them soon.
把蛋糕放到冰箱里。
Put the cake in the fridge.
请把那张桌子搬到客厅中间。
Please move that table to the middle of the living room.
3前缀/后缀 — 老/小 and 家/师/员
Chinese uses prefixes and suffixes to modify nouns, especially for people. 老 (lǎo) and 小 (xiǎo) as prefixes before surnames show familiarity or seniority. As suffixes, 家 (jiā = expert), 师 (shī = teacher/specialist), and 员 (yuán = member/worker) form professional nouns.
老/小 ＋ Surname → informal address by seniority
Noun ＋ 家 → expert in a field
Noun ＋ 师 → specialist / teacher
Noun ＋ 员 → member / staff
老张今天不来了。/ 小李，你来一下。
Old Zhang isn't coming today. / Young Li, come here a moment.
他是一位有名的画家。
He is a famous painter. (画 + 家)
她是我们学校的音乐老师。
She is our school's music teacher. (老 + 师)
他在银行做工作人员。
He works as a bank staff member. (人 + 员)
4一边……一边…… — Simultaneous Actions
一边……一边…… (yībiān … yībiān …) describes two actions happening at the same time by the same subject. Both slots take verb phrases. The two actions should be physically compatible (e.g., walking and talking). This pattern only works for deliberate, ongoing actions — not instantaneous ones.
[Subject] ＋ 一边 ＋ V₁ (+ Obj₁) ＋ 一边 ＋ V₂ (+ Obj₂)
小明一边听音乐，一边画画儿。
Xiǎo Míng listens to music while drawing.
我们一边吃饭，一边聊天。
We chat while eating.
她总是喜欢一边走路一边看手机。
She always likes looking at her phone while walking.
他们一边喝红茶，一边聊东方文化。
They chatted about Eastern culture while drinking black tea.

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国的请客文化 Chinese Hosting Culture
在中国，"请客"（qǐng kè）是一种重要的社交活动。主人通常会事先准备很多菜，热情招待客人。去别人家做客时，带礼物（lǐwù）是基本礼貌，常见的礼物有水果、茶叶、酒或者小点心。吃饭时，主人会不断给客人夹菜（jiā cài），表示热情。客人一般不会第一个动筷子，要等主人先邀请。在中国，饭桌是联系感情、增进友谊的重要场所。与朋友一起吃饭，不仅仅是为了填饱肚子，更是为了增进感情。
In China, "qǐng kè" (treating guests) is an important social activity. Hosts typically prepare many dishes in advance and welcome guests warmly. When visiting someone's home as a guest, bringing a gift is basic courtesy — common gifts include fruit, tea, wine, or small snacks. During the meal, the host will keep offering food to guests as a sign of warmth. Guests generally don't pick up their chopsticks first — they wait for the host's invitation. In China, the dining table is an important place for bonding and building friendships. Eating together with friends is not just about satisfying hunger — it is about deepening relationships.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
红茶 vs 绿茶 vs 茉莉花茶 Three Types of Chinese Tea
① 绿茶 (lǜchá)：未经发酵，保留了茶叶的天然色泽和清爽香气。代表：龙井茶（Lóngjǐng chá）、碧螺春（Bìluóchūn）。

② 红茶 (hóngchá)：经过完全发酵，颜色深红，味道浓郁，在西方也很受欢迎。英语叫"black tea"（黑茶），但中文叫"红茶"（red tea）。

③ 茉莉花茶 (mòlì huāchá)：绿茶与茉莉花混合，香气迷人，在中国北方特别流行。

注意：英语的"black tea"翻译成中文是"红茶"，不是"黑茶"！
① Green tea (绿茶): Unfermented, retaining the natural color and fresh aroma of the leaves. Notable examples: Longjing (Dragon Well), Biluochun.

② Black tea (红茶): Fully fermented, deep red in color, rich in flavor, and popular in the West. In English it's called "black tea," but in Chinese it's called 红茶 (red tea).

③ Jasmine tea (茉莉花茶): A blend of green tea and jasmine flowers, with a captivating fragrance, especially popular in northern China.

Note: The English "black tea" translates to 红茶 in Chinese — not 黑茶!
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
来自 vs 是……人 vs 从……来 Three Ways to Express Origin
三种方式都可以表示"来自哪里"，但用法有细微区别：

来自 (láizì)：正式一些，强调出身或背景。
例：他来自英国。（他的背景/国籍是英国）

是……人 (shì … rén)：最口语的方式，直接说国籍或地区身份。
例：他是英国人。（他的国籍是英国）

从……来 (cóng … lái)：强调动作，"从某地来到这里"。
例：他从英国来的。（他从英国来了，来到了中国）

总结：身份/背景用"来自/是…人"，移动/行程用"从…来"。
All three express "where someone is from," but with subtle differences:

来自 (láizì): Slightly more formal, emphasizes origin or background.
E.g.: He comes from the UK. (his nationality/background)

是……人 (shì … rén): Most colloquial, directly states nationality or regional identity.
E.g.: He is British. (his nationality is British)

从……来 (cóng … lái): Emphasizes movement — "came here from somewhere."
E.g.: He came from the UK. (he traveled here from the UK)

Summary: Use 来自/是…人 for identity/background; use 从…来 for travel/movement.

1 · 词典 vs 字典 — Two Kinds of Dictionary
词典 (cídiǎn) is a word dictionary — it explains the meanings of words and phrases, often bilingual (Chinese-English, etc.). 字典 (zìdiǎn) is a character dictionary — it explains individual Chinese characters, their radicals, pronunciations, and basic meanings. Learners often use 词典; students writing essays use 字典 to look up characters.
我买了一本汉英词典。I bought a Chinese-English dictionary.
不认识这个字，可以查字典。If you don't know this character, you can look it up in the character dictionary.
现在很多人用手机里的电子词典。Now many people use electronic dictionaries on their phones.
2 · 最好 — "Had Better / It's Best To"
最好 (zuìhǎo) is an adverb meaning "it would be best to / had better." It expresses a strong recommendation or advice, often implying that not following the advice could lead to a problem. It is placed directly before the verb. Do not confuse it with 最好 as an adjective meaning "the best (quality)."
[Subject] ＋ 最好 ＋ V (advice/recommendation)
那本书最好今天还。You'd better return that book today.
出门最好带一把伞。It's best to bring an umbrella when you go out.
比赛前最好多练习几次。You'd better practice a few more times before the competition.
3 · 被 vs 让 vs 叫 — Passive Voice Markers
All three can mark the passive voice (something is done to the subject by an agent), but they differ in tone and usage. 被 (bèi) is the most standard and neutral passive marker; it is used in both written and spoken Chinese. 让 (ràng) and 叫 (jiào) are more colloquial alternatives. Importantly, 让/叫 require an agent — you can't omit it. 被 can be used without stating the agent.
这本书被别人借走了。This book was checked out by someone else. (standard)
我的钱包被人偷了。My wallet was stolen. (agent omitted, still OK)
我的校园卡可能被我放在家里了。My campus card was probably left at home by me.
4 · 然后 vs 以后 — "Then" vs "After"
然后 (ránhòu) means "and then / after that" — it connects two sequential actions in the same sentence or between sentences. It always implies the first action has just been completed. 以后 (yǐhòu) means "after / in the future" — it indicates a point or period in time after a reference event, often at the start of a clause.
我先去图书馆，然后去游泳。I'll go to the library first, then go swimming.
下课以后，我们去打球吧。After class, let's go play ball.
吃完饭，然后我们出去走走。After eating, then we'll go for a walk.
5 · 有些 vs 有点儿 — Both Mean "Somewhat / A Bit"
有些 (yǒuxiē) and 有点儿 (yǒu diǎnr) both mean "somewhat / a little / a bit" before adjectives or stative verbs, but they have different ranges. 有点儿 is more colloquial and often implies a mild negative or unwanted quality. 有些 can be more neutral and slightly more formal, and it can also mean "some" before nouns (有些人 = "some people").
我有些紧张。/ 我有点儿紧张。I'm a bit nervous. (both work; 有点儿 is more colloquial)
有些人喜欢看书，有些人喜欢看视频。Some people like reading; some like watching videos. (有些 = some)
这道题有点儿难。This question is a little hard. (mild negative: difficulty)
6 · 相信 vs 信任 — Believe vs Trust
相信 (xiāngxìn) means to believe (that something is true) or to believe in oneself / someone's abilities. It targets a proposition or a person's capacity. 信任 (xìnrèn) means to trust (someone) — it's about reliance and confidence in a person's character or reliability. 相信 can take a clause as its object; 信任 typically takes a person.
你要相信自己！You have to believe in yourself!
我相信他说的是真的。I believe what he said is true.

In a 被 (bèi) sentence, the subject is the receiver of the action, not the doer. The agent (the doer) follows 被. Adverbs and modal verbs are placed before 被. 被动句：被 / 让 / 叫 Three Passive Markers Compared
① 被 (bèi)：最正式、最常用，书面语和口语都可以，不一定需要施动者。
例：书被借走了。/ 书被他借走了。

② 让 (ràng)：比较口语化，必须有施动者（不能省略），带有"允许或导致"的含义。
例：书让他借走了。（他借走了，有点儿暗示被允许）

③ 叫 (jiào)：最口语，北方方言色彩更浓，意思与"让"相近，也必须有施动者。
例：书叫他借走了。（北方口语）

总结：写作用被，口语用让/叫；让/叫不可省略施动者。
① 被 (bèi): Most formal and most common; used in both written and spoken Chinese; the agent can be omitted.
E.g.: The book was checked out. / The book was checked out by him.

② 让 (ràng): More colloquial; the agent CANNOT be omitted; carries a slight sense of "allowed to happen."
E.g.: The book was taken by him. (implies it was allowed/happened)

③ 叫 (jiào): Most colloquial; more common in northern dialects; similar to 让; agent also cannot be omitted.
E.g.: The book was taken by him. (northern colloquial)

Summary: Use 被 in writing; use 让/叫 in casual speech; never omit the agent with 让/叫.
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
最好 vs 应该 vs 必须 Three Ways to Give Advice or Express Obligation
三个词都可以表示"建议或义务"，但语气不同：

最好 (zuìhǎo)：最温和，意思是"我建议你……会更好"，属于建议而非命令。
例：你最好早点睡。（建议，友好）

应该 (yīnggāi)：表示"按理来说应当……"，语气比最好强，带有道德或逻辑上的期待。
例：你应该多锻炼身体。（应当，有期待）

必须 (bìxū)：最强，表示"一定要……不能不……"，没有选择余地，语气最命令化。
例：明天必须交作业。（强制要求）

总结：建议→最好，期待→应该，强制→必须。
All three can express advice or obligation, but with different degrees of force:

最好 (zuìhǎo): Softest — "I suggest it would be better if you…" — a recommendation, not a command.
E.g.: You'd better go to sleep early. (friendly suggestion)

应该 (yīnggāi): Medium — "you should / ought to…" — carries a moral or logical expectation.
E.g.: You should exercise more. (expectation)

必须 (bìxū): Strongest — "you must / have to…" — no choice, close to an order.
E.g.: The homework must be handed in tomorrow. (compulsory)

Summary: suggestion→最好, expectation→应该, compulsion→必须.

1 · 半 — Half, Semi-, Partly
半 (bàn) means "half" and can combine with nouns, measure words, and time expressions. It can appear before a noun (半个南京人 = half a Nanjing local), before a time word (半年 = half a year, 一个半 = one and a half), or in set expressions like 半天 (a long time; half a day). Note: 一个半 = 1.5, 两个半 = 2.5.

2 · 平时 vs 有时 vs 以前 — Three Time Expressions
平时 (píngshí) means "in ordinary times / usually / on a regular day" — it's about habitual, everyday situations, not right now. 有时 (yǒushí) means "sometimes / occasionally" — less frequent. 以前 (yǐqián) means "before / in the past" — refers to a time before now, often contrasted with 现在.
平时老人们在小区里下棋、聊天儿。The elderly residents usually play chess and chat in the community.
我有时骑车，有时坐地铁去上班。I sometimes cycle and sometimes take the subway to work.
以前我住在北京，现在住在上海。Before, I lived in Beijing — now I live in Shanghai.
3 · 根据 vs 按照 — Both Mean "According to"
Both are prepositions meaning "according to / based on," but with subtle differences. 根据 (gēnjù) emphasizes using something as a basis for inference or judgment — often used with facts, data, or observations. 按照 (ànzhào) emphasizes following rules, instructions, or standards in doing something. Both come before the reference point, followed by the action.
根据我的了解，这里的环境非常好。Based on what I know, the environment here is excellent. (inference)
按照规定，大家都要准时到。According to the rules, everyone must arrive on time. (following rules)
根据天气预报，明天会下雨。According to the weather forecast, it will rain tomorrow.
4 · 了解 vs 知道 — Depth of Knowing
Both mean "to know," but at different levels. 知道 (zhīdào) means to know a fact — simple awareness that something is true. 了解 (liǎojiě) means to understand in depth / be familiar with — it implies more thorough knowledge, often gained through experience or research. 了解 can also be used as a verb meaning "to find out about" (actively learning).
我知道黄河是中国的一条大河。I know the Yellow River is one of China's great rivers. (basic fact)
我对南京不太了解，你能介绍一下吗？I don't know much about Nanjing — could you tell me more? (deep knowledge)
我想了解一下这里的历史。I'd like to learn more about the history of this place.
5 · 著名 vs 有名 vs 出名 — Three Ways to Say "Famous"
All three mean "famous," but with differences in register and usage. 著名 (zhùmíng) is the most formal and literary — used for cities, historical figures, works of art. 有名 (yǒumíng) is neutral and everyday — used for people, places, and things. 出名 (chūmíng) means "to become famous" or "famous" with a connotation of rising to prominence — often carries a sense of earned reputation.
南京是中国一座非常著名的城市。Nanjing is a very famous city in China. (formal)
这家餐厅很有名，常常坐满了人。This restaurant is very well-known — it's always full. (everyday)
他唱歌以后，一下子就出名了。After performing, he became famous overnight. (rose to fame)
6 · 另外 — In Addition / Besides / Another
另外 (lìngwài) has two main uses: ① as a conjunction / sentence adverb meaning "in addition; besides; moreover" — introducing extra information; ② as a determiner before nouns meaning "another / a different." It is more formal than 还 and 也, and signals a new separate point rather than continuation of the same point.
另外，我还知道一些当地人才知道的景点。In addition, I also know some spots only locals know about.
这个问题很有意思，另外还有一个更有趣的问题。This question is interesting — besides, there is an even more interesting one.
我去了一家店，另外一家还没去。I went to one shop — I haven't been to the other one yet.
1介词"根据" — Preposition "According to / Based on"
The preposition 根据 (gēnjù) introduces the basis or source for an action, judgment, or conclusion. It places what you're relying on before the main verb. It answers the question "on what basis?" Unlike 按照 (which follows rules), 根据 is used for facts, observations, data, or personal knowledge as a foundation for reasoning.
根据 ＋ [Basis/Source] ，[Subject] ＋ [Action / Conclusion]
根据我的了解，这里的居民都很喜欢这个小区的环境。
Based on what I know, all the residents love the environment of this community.
根据要求，我们应该参加明天的会议。
According to the requirements, we should attend tomorrow's meeting.
老师根据每个学生的兴趣，选择了不一样的书。
The teacher chose different books for each student based on their interests.
根据天气预报，明天有大雨。
According to the weather forecast, there will be heavy rain tomorrow.
2数量词重叠 — Reduplication of Numeral-Measure Word Phrases
Repeating a numeral + measure word combination creates an adverbial that typically means one by one / bit by bit / gradually. It describes the manner of an action vividly — often suggesting slow, incremental, or methodical progress. The structure can optionally be followed by 地 before the verb.
数词 ＋ 量词 ＋ 数词 ＋ 量词 ＋（地）＋ 动词
e.g. 一点儿一点儿（地）, 一段一段（地）, 一个一个（地）
我的汉语水平一天一天地提高了。
My Chinese level improved day by day.
他们两个两个地走进教室。
They walked into the classroom two by two.
你可以一点儿一点儿地练习，不用着急。
You can practice little by little — no need to rush.
老师给我们一个一个地讲解了这道题。
The teacher explained this problem to us one by one.
3固定短语 "在……看来" — "In sb.'s View / From sb.'s Perspective"
The set phrase 在……看来 (zài … kànlái) expresses the viewpoint of a specific person or group. The subject who holds the opinion goes between 在 and 看来. It is often used sentence-initially to frame the rest of the sentence as a subjective opinion rather than objective fact. Unlike 看来 alone (which signals the speaker's own inference), 在……看来 can attribute the view to any person or group.
在 ＋ [Person/Group] ＋ 看来，[Opinion / Conclusion]
在我看来，时间比钱更重要。
In my view, time is more important than money.
在老人们看来，住在这里非常舒服。
From the elderly residents' perspective, living here is very comfortable.
在很多游客看来，南京是中国最美的城市之一。
In the eyes of many tourists, Nanjing is one of China's most beautiful cities.
在他看来，每天运动对身体很好。
In his opinion, exercising every day is very good for one's health.
4固定短语 "不一会儿" — "In No Time / Very Soon / Before Long"
不一会儿 (bù yīhuìr) is a colloquial set phrase meaning "in a very short time / in no time at all." It functions as a time adverbial in sentences and is used to emphasize that something happened or will happen quickly — often with an element of surprise. It typically precedes the verb or appears at the start of the clause.
[Context]，[Subject] ＋ 不一会儿 ＋ 就 ＋ V + 了
难过的事情不一会儿就忘了。
Any sadness faded away in no time.
我收拾了不一会儿，已经收拾完十多本书了。
I had only been tidying for a short while and had already sorted over ten books.
你稍等，他不一会儿就回来了。
Wait a moment — he'll be back very soon.
她不一会儿就学会了这首歌。
She learned this song in no time at all.

🏮 Culture · 文化
南京：历史古城 Nanjing: An Ancient Capital
南京（Nánjīng）位于中国东部，是江苏省的省会，有超过两千年的历史。南京曾经是多个朝代的都城（dūchéng），包括明朝和中华民国时期。南京的著名景点很多，包括中山陵（Zhōngshān Líng，孙中山陵墓）、南京长江大桥、秦淮河（Qínhuái Hé）夜游，以及南京博物馆等。南京也是中国著名的"火炉城市"之一，夏天非常热。南京的美食也很有特色，著名的有盐水鸭（yánshuǐ yā）、小笼包和各种汤类食物。"我是半个南京人"这句话常常被在南京住过一段时间的外地人用来表示对这座城市的感情和认同感。
Nanjing, located in eastern China, is the capital of Jiangsu Province with over 2,000 years of history. Nanjing served as the capital of several dynasties, including the Ming Dynasty and the Republic of China period. Famous attractions include the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, the Nanjing Yangtze River Bridge, night boat tours on the Qinhuai River, and the Nanjing Museum. Nanjing is also one of China's famously hot "furnace cities" in summer. The local cuisine is distinctive, with salted duck, xiaolongbao dumplings, and various soups among the highlights. The phrase "I'm half a Nanjing local" is commonly used by people who lived there for a period to express their attachment and identification with the city.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
长江 vs 黄河 The Yangtze vs The Yellow River
长江 (Cháng Jiāng)：中国最长的河，全长约6,300公里，也是亚洲最长的河流。长江流经中国中部，是中国重要的经济通道，沿岸城市包括重庆、武汉、南京和上海。

黄河 (Huáng Hé)：中国第二长的河，全长约5,464公里。因河水含大量黄色泥沙而得名。黄河被称为"中华民族的母亲河"，是中华文明的发源地之一。

文化意义：长江代表经济繁荣和商业发展；黄河代表中国文明的根源和历史传承。两条河流都在中国历史和文化中占有极为重要的地位。
The Yangtze River: China's longest river at approximately 6,300 km — also Asia's longest. It flows through central China and is a vital economic artery. Major cities along its banks include Chongqing, Wuhan, Nanjing, and Shanghai.

The Yellow River: China's second longest river at approximately 5,464 km. It gets its name from the large amounts of yellow silt it carries. Known as "the mother river of the Chinese nation," it is one of the cradles of Chinese civilization.

Cultural significance: The Yangtze represents economic prosperity and commerce; the Yellow River represents the roots of Chinese civilization and historical heritage. Both rivers hold a place of enormous importance in Chinese history and culture.
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
了解 vs 知道 vs 认识 Three Ways to "Know"
三个词都与"知晓"有关，但侧重点不同：

知道 (zhīdào)：最基础，意思是知道某个事实或信息，不需要深度理解。
例：我知道他叫什么名字。

了解 (liǎojiě)：比知道深，意思是对某事有较全面或深入的了解，常常需要经历或研究。
例：我对中国历史有一定的了解。

认识 (rènshi)：主要用于"认识人"或"认识字"，表示知晓并能辨认。
例：我认识他，他是我同学。/ 我不认识这个字。

总结：事实用知道，深度理解用了解，人/字用认识。
All three relate to "knowing," but with different emphases:

知道 (zhīdào): Most basic — knowing a fact or piece of information, no deep understanding required.
E.g.: I know what his name is.

了解 (liǎojiě): Deeper than 知道 — having a thorough or comprehensive understanding, often requiring experience or research.
E.g.: I have a certain understanding of Chinese history.

认识 (rènshi): Primarily used for recognizing people or characters — knowing and being able to identify.
E.g.: I know him — he's my classmate. / I don't recognize this character.

Summary: Use 知道 for facts, 了解 for deep understanding, 认识 for people/characters.

1一会儿…一会儿… — Alternating Actions
Repeating 一会儿 before two different verbs (or verb phrases) expresses that someone (or something) alternates rapidly between two actions. It creates a vivid sense of restlessness or constant change. Think of it as "one moment … the next moment …"
S ＋ 一会儿 ＋ V₁，一会儿 ＋ V₂
那只猫一会儿跑，一会儿跳，停不下来。
That cat keeps running one moment and jumping the next — it won't stop.
她一会儿哭，一会儿笑，我不知道她怎么了。
She's crying one moment and laughing the next — I don't know what's wrong with her.
他一会儿说想去，一会儿又说不去，真烦人！
One moment he says he wants to go, the next he says he doesn't — how annoying!
这天的天气一会儿晴，一会儿下雨，变化很快。
The weather today is sunny one moment and rainy the next — it changes very quickly.
2介词"关于" — The Preposition 关于
The preposition 关于 introduces the topic or subject of a statement, meaning "about / regarding / concerning." It always appears before the noun or noun phrase that is the topic. 关于 can open a sentence (then a comma follows) or appear after the subject.
【关于 + topic】＋ , ＋ [comment]
S ＋ 读过 / 知道 / 听说 / 写了 ＋ 【关于…的…】
关于大熊猫出国的事，这是中国友好外交的方式。
Regarding pandas going abroad, this is China's way of friendly diplomacy.
我读过一本关于中国历史的书。
I've read a book about Chinese history.
关于野生动物的保护，政府做了很多努力。
Regarding wild animal protection, the government has made a lot of effort.

Today I learned a lot of knowledge about giant pandas.
3固定短语"一般来说" — Generally Speaking
The fixed phrase 一般来说 (yībān lái shuō) means "generally speaking / in general / as a rule." It introduces a generalisation or typical situation. It usually appears at the beginning of a sentence or after the subject, followed by a comma.
【一般来说】，＋ [general statement]
S + 【一般来说】，＋ [general statement]
一般来说，大熊猫每天要吃十几公斤的竹子。
Generally speaking, giant pandas eat over ten kilograms of bamboo every day.
一般来说，竹子就是它们最主要的食物。
In general, bamboo is their main food source.
一般来说，周末动物园的游客比平时多很多。

一般来说，野生动物不容易被人照顾，需要专业人员帮忙。
As a rule, wild animals are not easy for people to look after — they need professional help.
4比较句(11) — A比B + 多/少/早/晚 + V + 数量短语
This comparative pattern expresses a specific quantity difference between two things. After stating that A exceeds (or falls short of) B by a certain amount, the measure of the difference comes at the end.
A ＋ 比 ＋ B ＋ 多/少/早/晚 ＋ V ＋ 数量短语
我们比平时早出发了一个小时。
We set off one hour earlier than usual.
我们比昨天早睡了两个小时。
We went to bed two hours earlier than yesterday.
那只大熊猫比旁边的多吃了不少竹子。
That giant panda ate quite a bit more bamboo than the one beside it.
我们在熊猫馆待的时间比其他地方多了将近一个小时。
We spent nearly an hour longer in the panda house than anywhere else.
今天他比我少吃了两碗饭。

🏮 Culture · 文化
大熊猫——中国的国宝 Giant Pandas — China's National Treasure
大熊猫（dàxióngmāo）是中国最有名的国宝（guóbǎo），也是全世界最受喜爱的动物之一。野生（yěshēng）大熊猫主要生活在中国四川、陕西和甘肃的山区，数量非常少，因此受到严格保护。大熊猫的主要食物是竹子（zhúzi），一般来说每天要吃十几公斤。大熊猫出国是著名的"熊猫外交"——中国会把大熊猫租借给其他国家，作为两国友好的象征。全世界很多大型动物园都曾经接待过来自中国的大熊猫，大人和小孩都非常喜爱它们。
The giant panda is China's most famous national treasure and one of the most beloved animals in the world. Wild giant pandas mainly live in the mountain regions of Sichuan, Shaanxi, and Gansu in China, and their numbers are extremely low — so they are strictly protected. Their main food is bamboo, and generally speaking they eat over ten kilograms a day. Pandas going abroad is the famous "panda diplomacy" — China loans pandas to other countries as a symbol of friendship between the two nations. Many major zoos worldwide have hosted pandas from China, and people of all ages adore them.

1副词 "越来越" — "More and More / Increasingly"
越来越 (yuèláiyuè) is placed directly before an adjective or verb to express that something is continuously increasing or intensifying over time. It implies an ongoing, progressive change — not a one-time shift. The adjective/verb that follows describes the quality that is increasing.
Subject ＋ 越来越 ＋ Adj / V (+ 了)
窗外的风景越来越好看了。
The scenery outside the window is getting more and more beautiful.
他的汉语说得越来越流利了。
His spoken Chinese is getting more and more fluent.
天气越来越冷，要多穿衣服。
The weather is getting colder and colder — you should wear more clothes.
我越来越喜欢中国的饮食文化了。
I like Chinese food culture more and more.
⚠️ Note: 越来越 always comes before the adj/v, never after. Do NOT say ✗ 好看越来越.
2关联结构 "越A越B" — "The More A, the More B"
越A越B expresses a proportional relationship between two qualities or actions: as A increases or happens, B also increases or happens. A and B can each be an adjective or a verb (phrase). The subject can be the same for both halves or different.
[Subject₁] ＋ 越 ＋ A ＋ [Subject₂] ＋ 越 ＋ B
行李越少越方便。
The fewer the bags, the more convenient.
我越看越喜欢这里的风景。
The more I look, the more I love the scenery here.
这首歌越听越好听。
The more you listen to this song, the better it sounds.
她学汉语学得越多，越有兴趣。
The more she studies Chinese, the more interested she becomes.
💡 Tip: 越来越 = progressive increase over time; 越A越B = proportional relationship between two things.
3关联结构 "连…都/也…" — "Even … Also …"
连…都/也… is used to express that even an extreme or unexpected case is included. It adds emphasis by highlighting the most unlikely item: "not only the expected, but even X." 连 introduces the surprising element; 都 or 也 reinforces the inclusive statement. The pattern emphasises the breadth or thoroughness of a condition.
连 ＋ [even this / surprising element] ＋ 都/也 ＋ V/Adj
连我这个当地人也会被这片海感动。
Even I, a local, am moved by this sea.
连平时的工作压力都忘了。
Even the usual work stress was forgotten.
这道题太难了，连老师都觉得不容易。
This problem is so hard that even the teacher finds it difficult.
他忙得连饭都没时间吃。
He's so busy he doesn't even have time to eat.
⚠️ Note: 都 and 也 are interchangeable here. 连 must come before the noun/pronoun being highlighted.
4关联结构 "一…就…" — "As Soon As … / Once … Then …"
一…就… expresses that as soon as the first event or condition is met, the second immediately follows. It implies a tight, automatic, or natural connection between the two actions. The subject can be the same or different in each half.
[Subject₁] ＋ 一 ＋ V₁ ＋ [Subject₂] ＋ 就 ＋ V₂
一到青岛，你就会喜欢上那里的。
As soon as you arrive in Qingdao, you'll fall in love with the place.
他一看到海，就跑了过去。
The moment he saw the sea, he ran over.
我一回到家，就开始做作业。
As soon as I get home, I start doing homework.
她一听音乐，就心情好了。
Once she listens to music, her mood lifts immediately.
💡 Tip: 一就 can also express a habit: 他一吃辣就出汗 — "He sweats the moment he eats spicy food."

✏️ Grammar · 语言点
越来越 vs 越…越… Two "yuè" Patterns Compared
这两个结构都含有"越"，但用法不同：

越来越 + adj/v：表示某一特征或状态随时间推移不断增加。只有一个主语，描述一种持续变化。
例：天气越来越冷了。（随时间变化）

越A越B：表示两件事之间的比例关系——A越多/大，B也越多/大。可以有一个或两个主语。
例：越吃越想吃。（两件事互相影响）

关键区别："越来越"是时间上的渐变；"越A越B"是两个变量之间的相互关系。
Both structures contain 越, but they are used differently:

越来越 + adj/v: expresses that a quality or state is continuously increasing over time. There is only one subject, describing an ongoing change.
E.g.: The weather is getting colder and colder. (change over time)

越A越B: expresses a proportional relationship between two things — the more A increases, the more B increases. Can have one or two subjects.
E.g.: The more you eat, the more you want to eat. (two things influencing each other)

Key difference: 越来越 = gradual change over time; 越A越B = proportional relationship between two variables.

进步 vs 提高

to improve/progress vs to raise/improve
compare
进步 (jìnbù)：focuses on the person making progress — often used with 很快, 很大, or as a noun. It means personal advancement or improvement over time. Common in education and skill development.
提高 (tígāo)：to raise or improve a level — often used with a specific skill (提高汉语水平). It implies a deliberate increase in a measurable standard. Can be transitive: 提高效率 (improve efficiency).

compare
感谢 (gǎnxiè)：more formal, heartfelt "I'm grateful for / I wish to thank." Used in speeches, letters, and situations where you want to express genuine appreciation. Can be followed by a person or an action.
谢谢 (xièxie)：everyday "thank you." Casual and universal — used in any situation, but less formal in tone than 感谢.
例句 · Examples

盼望 (pànwàng)：emotional longing or eager anticipation — often used when someone is eagerly waiting for something heartfelt, like seeing a loved one return.
1关联结构 "虽然…但是/可是…" — "Although … But …"
虽然…但是/可是… is a concessive structure used to acknowledge one fact while introducing a contrasting or surprising conclusion. 虽然 introduces the concession; 但是 or 可是 introduces the contrast. Both conjunctions must appear — you cannot use one without the other in Chinese (unlike English, where you can say "although X" as a standalone clause).
虽然 ＋ [clause 1] ，但是/可是 ＋ [clause 2 — the contrast]
虽然有时候学汉语很困难，但是每次进步都让我很高兴。
Although learning Chinese is sometimes difficult, every bit of progress makes me very happy.
虽然刚开始不太习惯，但现在越来越喜欢了。
Although I wasn't very used to it at first, I like it more and more now.
她虽然很忙，可是还是来帮了我。
Although she was very busy, she still came to help me.
虽然这道菜很辣，但是非常好吃。
Although this dish is very spicy, it's absolutely delicious.
⚠️ Note: Never say ✗ 虽然…但是…可是… — pick one of 但是 / 可是 / 却 for the second clause, not multiple.
2关联结构 "不但…而且/还…" — "Not Only … But Also …"
不但…而且/还… is a progressive additive structure: the second clause (while且/还) builds on or surpasses the first clause (不但), adding something more, stronger, or more surprising. It is the additive counterpart of 虽然…但是 (concessive). The subject can be stated once at the start or repeated in both clauses.
[Subject] ＋ 不但 ＋ [clause 1] ，而且/还 ＋ [clause 2 — adds more]
这一年不但让我学到了汉语，而且让我认识了很多好朋友。
This year has not only taught me Chinese, but also introduced me to many good friends.
她不但汉语说得好，还会写很漂亮的汉字。
She not only speaks Chinese well, but can also write beautiful characters.
这家餐厅不但菜好吃，而且服务也很好。
This restaurant is not only delicious, but also has great service.
他不但来了，而且还带来了很多礼物。
He not only came, but also brought a lot of gifts.
💡 Compare: 虽然…但是 = concession (A is true, but B is the key point); 不但…而且 = addition (A is true, and B makes it even better/stronger).
3关联结构 "只有…才…" — "Only If / Only When … Then …"
只有…才… expresses an exclusive condition: only by meeting the condition in the first clause can the result in the second clause be achieved. It implies that no other way is possible — the condition is both necessary and sufficient. The structure is stronger and more exclusive than 如果…就… (general condition).
只有 ＋ [necessary condition] ，[Subject] ＋ 才 ＋ [result/conclusion]
只有不断努力，才能实现梦想。
Only through constant effort can one realise one's dreams.
只有多交流，才能真正了解彼此。
Only through more communication can we truly understand each other.
只有你来了，我才能放心。
I can only feel reassured if you come.
只有坚持，才能成功。
Only by persisting can you succeed.
💡 Compare: 如果…就… = general "if … then …" (one possible path); 只有…才… = exclusive "only if … then …" (no other path is possible).
4固定结构 "一点儿也不…" — "Not … at All / Not in the Least"
一点儿也不… is used to completely negate a quality or state. Literally it means "not even a little" — expressing absolute denial. It is placed before the adjective or verb being negated. A common variant is 一点儿都不… (也 and 都 are interchangeable here).
Subject ＋ 一点儿也/都不 ＋ Adj/V
这道题一点儿也不难！
This question is not difficult at all!
我昨天太累了，一点儿都不想说话。
I was so tired yesterday — I didn't want to talk at all.
他说汉语一点儿也不像外国人。
He speaks Chinese without any foreign accent whatsoever.
虽然外面很冷，她一点儿都不觉得。
Even though it was cold outside, she didn't feel it at all.
⚠️ Note: For past actions, use 一点儿也没/都没 + V (e.g. 我一点儿也没吃 — "I didn't eat anything at all").

外国人在中国学汉语 Foreigners Learning Chinese in China
每年有数以万计的外国学生来到中国学习汉语，参加汉语进修项目（hànyǔ jìnxiū，Chinese language programme）或攻读学位。学习汉语对很多人来说是一大挑战，原因在于汉语有四个声调（sìgè shēngdiào）、大量的汉字（hànzì）以及与西方语言完全不同的语法结构。尽管如此，越来越多的外国人把说一口流利的普通话作为自己的职业目标或个人梦想。著名的汉语水平考试HSK（汉语水平考试）是专门为外国学习者设计的，分为1至9级。通过HSK考试不但能证明语言能力，而且在中国找工作或申请学校时也很有帮助。
Every year, tens of thousands of foreign students come to China to study Chinese, either in language programmes or degree courses. Learning Chinese is a major challenge for many, due to its four tones, large number of characters, and grammar structure completely different from Western languages. Despite this, more and more foreigners are making fluency in Mandarin their career goal or personal dream. The well-known HSK (Chinese Proficiency Test) is designed specifically for foreign learners and is divided into levels 1–9. Passing the HSK not only proves language ability but is also very helpful when looking for work in China or applying to universities.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
虽然…但是 vs 不但…而且 Concession vs Addition
这两个结构经常被混淆，但用法完全不同：

虽然…但是/可是（转折）：前半部分承认一个事实，后半部分提出相反的或更重要的结论。重点在"但是"之后。
例：虽然很辣，但是很好吃。（承认辣，但强调好吃）

不但…而且/还（递进）：前半部分说一件事，后半部分加上另一件更强的事，加强语气。两件事都是正面的（或都是负面的）。
例：不但好吃，而且很便宜。（好吃，加上便宜，两件好事）

简单记忆：虽然…但是 = "A，但是B（转一个方向）"；不但…而且 = "A，加上B（同方向加强）"。
These two structures are often confused, but they work in completely different ways:

虽然…但是/可是 (concession): the first clause acknowledges a fact; the second introduces a contrasting or more important conclusion. The emphasis is on what comes after 但是.
E.g.: Although it's spicy, it's delicious. (acknowledges spicy, emphasises delicious)

不但…而且/还 (addition): the first clause states one thing; the second adds something stronger, building on it. Both clauses point in the same direction (both positive or both negative).
E.g.: Not only is it delicious, but it's also cheap. (two good things)

Simple memory aid: 虽然…但是 = "A, but B (turns in a new direction)"; 不但…而且 = "A, and even more B (same direction, amplified)".

如果…就… vs 只有…才… Two Conditional Structures
两个结构都表示条件，但强调不同：

如果…就…（一般条件）：表示"假如A，那么B"。条件不唯一，只是其中一种可能。语气比较温和。
例：如果你努力，就会成功。（努力是成功的一种途径）

只有…才…（唯一条件）：表示"只有A，才能B"。强调A是实现B的唯一方法，没有其他路。语气更强，更有说服力。
例：只有努力，才能成功。（努力是成功的唯一途径）

关键区别：如果 = 一个可能的条件；只有 = 唯一必要条件。
Both structures express conditions, but with a different emphasis:

如果…就… (general condition): "if A, then B" — the condition is one of several possible ways. The tone is relatively mild.
E.g.: If you work hard, you'll succeed. (hard work is one path to success)

只有…才… (exclusive condition): "only if A, then B" — emphasises that A is the ONLY way to achieve B. Stronger, more persuasive tone.
E.g.: Only by working hard can you succeed. (hard work is the only path)

Key difference: 如果 = one possible condition; 只有 = the one and only necessary condition.
