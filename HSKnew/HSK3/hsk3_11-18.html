第十一课 · Lesson 11

看来我没办法解决这个问题

Kànlái wǒ méi bànfǎ jiějué zhège wèntí

It seems I can't solve this problem

Text 1 · 在办公室里
In the office — Wang Yixue & Yang Tongle discuss the upcoming meeting
同乐
会议定了几点开始？ Huìyì dìng le jǐ diǎn kāishǐ? When has the meeting been set to start?
一雪
后天上午十点，还在第一会议室。不过经理说，今天下午先开个小会。 Hòutiān shàngwǔ shí diǎn, hái zài dì yī huìyìshì. Búguò jīnglǐ shuō, jīntiān xiàwǔ xiān kāi gè xiǎo huì. Day after tomorrow at 10 a.m., still in the first meeting room. But the manager says we'll have a short meeting this afternoon first.
同乐
开会的时候，咱们用会议室的电脑还是自己的笔记本电脑？ Kāihuì de shíhou, zánmen yòng huìyìshì de diànnǎo háishi zìjǐ de bǐjìběn diànnǎo? During the meeting, should we use the meeting room's computer or our own laptops?
一雪
用会议室的吧，这样可以互相发文件，或者一起看资料。 Yòng huìyìshì de ba, zhèyàng kěyǐ hùxiāng fā wénjiàn, huòzhě yīqǐ kàn zīliào. Let's use the meeting room's—that way we can share files with each other, or look at materials together.
同乐
好，那我需要带什么？ Hǎo, nà wǒ xūyào dài shénme? Okay, what do I need to bring?
一雪
你帮我做会议记录就好，材料我已经发到大家邮箱了。 Nǐ bāng wǒ zuò huìyì jìlù jiù hǎo, cáiliào wǒ yǐjīng fā dào dàjiā yóuxiāng le. Just help me take the meeting notes—I've already sent the materials to everyone's email.
Text 2 · 在会议室里
In the meeting room — the computer has no sound
同乐
电脑好了吗？声音有了吗？ Diànnǎo hǎo le ma? Shēngyīn yǒu le ma? Is the computer working? Is there sound now?
一雪
我试了好几次，还是没有声音。 Wǒ shì le hǎo jǐ cì, háishi méiyǒu shēngyīn. I've tried many times, but there's still no sound.
同乐
让我看看……你说几句话试试？ Ràng wǒ kànkan…… Nǐ shuō jǐ jù huà shìshi? Let me have a look... Say a few words to test it?
一雪
喂，喂，能听见吗？ Wèi, wèi, néng tīngjiàn ma? Hello, hello, can you hear me?
同乐
声音有点儿小，听不清楚。 Shēngyīn yǒudiǎnr xiǎo, tīng bù qīngchǔ. The sound is a bit quiet; I can't hear it clearly.
一雪
看来我没办法解决这个问题，只能找别人来帮忙了。 Kànlái wǒ méi bànfǎ jiějué zhège wèntí, zhǐ néng zhǎo biéren lái bāngmáng le. It seems I can't solve this problem—I'll have to find someone else to help.
Text 3 · 在办公室里
In the office — colleagues away, heavy workload
一雪
同乐，你看见老张了吗？ Tónglè, nǐ kànjiàn Lǎo Zhāng le ma? Tongle, have you seen Old Zhang?
同乐
老张没来，他说家里有点儿事，请假了。 Lǎo Zhāng méi lái, tā shuō jiālǐ yǒudiǎnr shì, qǐngjià le. Old Zhang isn't in. He said something came up at home and asked for the day off.
一雪
好几个同事都休假了，这么多工作我一个人忙不完。 Hǎo jǐ gè tóngshì dōu xiūjià le, zhème duō gōngzuò wǒ yī gè rén máng bù wán. Several colleagues are on vacation. I can't handle all this work by myself.
同乐
都十二点多了，咱们先去吃饭，下午我帮你把工作做完。 Dōu shí'èr diǎn duō le, zánmen xiān qù chīfàn, xiàwǔ wǒ bāng nǐ bǎ gōngzuò zuò wán. It's already past noon. Let's eat first, and I'll help you finish the work this afternoon.
一雪
不行，我很怕来不及。今天还要把报告发到公司邮箱。 Bùxíng, wǒ hěn pà lái bù jí. Jīntiān hái yào bǎ bàogào fā dào gōngsī yóuxiāng. That won't do—I'm worried about running out of time. I still need to send the report to the company inbox today.
同乐
你没看到邮件吗？经理还没回来，下周才能开会。 Nǐ méi kàndào yóujiàn ma? Jīnglǐ hái méi huílái, xià zhōu cái néng kāihuì. Didn't you see the email? The manager hasn't returned yet—the meeting can only happen next week.
一雪
那不着急了，走，吃饭去！ Nà bù zháojí le, zǒu, chīfàn qù! Oh, no need to rush then! Come on, let's go eat!
Text 4 · 在家里·日记
At home — Wang Yixue's diary: work vs. family
一雪
前几天，上海的同事来北京了。吃饭的时候，他们告诉我，公司有不少同事去上海工作了，问我愿不愿意也去上海。我是这样想的：父母和弟弟都在北京，刘明的工作很忙，孩子们也还小。如果我去别的城市工作，就要离开家人了。虽然去上海可能会有更好的机会，或者工资也更高，但是对我来说，生活比工作更重要，我不愿意为了工作离开家人。 Qián jǐ tiān, Shànghǎi de tóngshì lái Běijīng le. Chīfàn de shíhou, tāmen gàosù wǒ, gōngsī yǒu bù shǎo tóngshì qù Shànghǎi gōngzuò le, wèn wǒ yuàn bú yuànyì yě qù Shànghǎi. Wǒ shì zhèyàng xiǎng de: fùmǔ hé dìdi dōu zài Běijīng, Liú Míng de gōngzuò hěn máng, háizimen yě hái xiǎo. Rúguǒ wǒ qù bié de chéngshì gōngzuò, jiù yào líkāi jiārén le. Suīrán qù Shànghǎi kěnéng huì yǒu gèng hǎo de jīhuì, huò gōngzī yě gèng gāo, dànshì duì wǒ lái shuō, shēnghuó bǐ gōngzuò gèng zhòngyào, wǒ bú yuànyì wèile gōngzuò líkāi jiārén. A few days ago, colleagues from Shanghai came to Beijing. While we were having dinner, they told me that quite a few colleagues had moved to Shanghai to work, and asked whether I'd be willing to go too. Here is how I think about it: my parents and brother are all in Beijing, Liu Ming's work keeps him very busy, and the kids are still young. If I went to work in another city, I'd have to leave my family. Although going to Shanghai might offer better opportunities, or perhaps a higher salary, for me life is more important than work—I'm not willing to leave my family for the sake of work.

Three ways to say "or"
compare
还是 (háishi)：used in questions offering a choice between two options.
或者 (huòzhě)：used in statements listing alternatives (not questions).
或 (huò)：the short, written/formal form of 或者. Common in text, less common in speech.
例句 · Examples
你喝茶还是咖啡？ (question → 还是)

你可以喝茶或者咖啡。 (statement → 或者)

虽然工资更高，或者机会更好……(written listing → 或者/或)
Although the salary is higher, or the opportunities are better…
1还是 vs 或者 — Which "or"?
Both translate as "or," but they belong in different sentence types: 还是 goes in questions (offering a choice), while 或者 goes in statements (listing alternatives). Using one where the other belongs sounds unnatural.
疑问句 (question) → 还是
陈述句 (statement) → 或者
开会时咱们用会议室的电脑还是笔记本电脑？
During the meeting, should we use the meeting room's computer or our laptops? (choice question → 还是)
你可以发邮件或者打电话联系我。
You can contact me by email or phone. (statement listing options → 或者)
你去上海还是留在北京？
Are you going to Shanghai or staying in Beijing? (question → 还是)
去上海可能有更好的机会，或者工资也更高。
Going to Shanghai might offer better opportunities, or a higher salary too. (statement → 或者)
2看来 — Drawing a Conclusion
The fixed phrase 看来 introduces a conclusion or inference that the speaker draws from observation or reasoning. It typically opens a sentence or clause. It conveys "from what I can see / given the situation, it seems that…"
【看来】＋ conclusion
S ＋ 【看来】＋ conclusion
看来我没办法解决这个问题，只能找别人帮忙了。
It seems I can't solve this problem—I'll have to find someone else to help.
这么晚了，看来经理今天不回来了。
It's so late; apparently the manager isn't coming back today.
你都把材料发好了，看来准备得很充分。
You've already sent all the materials — it seems you're well prepared.
3把字句 (三) — Result Complement
A 把 sentence can show that an action produces a result. After the verb, add a result complement — a verb or adjective that tells you what happened to the object as a consequence of the action.
S ＋ 把 ＋ O ＋ V ＋ 结果补语 (result complement)
下午我帮你把工作做完。
This afternoon I'll help you get the work finished.
今天还要把报告发到公司邮箱。
I still need to send the report to the company inbox today.
请你把材料准备好。
Please get the materials ready.
他把开会时间改了。
He changed the meeting time.
4对……来说 — "For … it …"
The fixed pattern 对……来说 sets the frame of reference — it indicates the person or group for whom a statement applies. Think of it as "as far as [X] is concerned" or "for [X]."
对 ＋ [person / group] ＋ 来说，＋ [statement]
对我来说，生活比工作更重要。
For me, life is more important than work.
对他来说，学中文不难。
For him, learning Chinese isn't hard.
对年轻人来说，有更好的机会很重要。
For young people, having better opportunities is very important.
对公司来说，开好每一次会议都很重要。
For the company, running every meeting well is very important.

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国的职场文化 Chinese Workplace Culture
在中国，职场文化（zhíchǎng wénhuà）有几个明显的特点。首先，会议（huìyì）非常重要，开会前通常要提前准备好材料（cáiliào）。其次，上下级关系（shàngxiàjí guānxi）很受重视——下级通常先听上级的意见。此外，请假（qǐngjià）需要提前向上级申请，直接告知原因，并写好请假条。在许多中国公司，员工还有"加班文化"，常常自愿留在公司工作到很晚。随着时代的变化，越来越多的年轻人开始重视工作与生活的平衡（pínghéng）。
Chinese workplace culture has several distinctive characteristics. First, meetings are very important — materials must be prepared in advance. Second, hierarchical relationships are highly valued: subordinates typically listen to their superiors' opinions first. Additionally, taking leave requires advance approval from a manager, with a clear reason stated and a leave application submitted. In many Chinese companies, there is also a culture of "overtime" — employees often voluntarily stay late. As times change, more and more young people are beginning to value work-life balance.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
还是 vs 或者 vs 或 Three Ways to Say "Or"
① 还是 (háishi)：用于疑问句，表示两者之间选择一个。
例：你喝茶还是咖啡？（你必须选一个）

② 或者 (huòzhě)：用于陈述句，表示两者均可，不强迫选择。
例：周末我们可以去爬山，或者去游泳。（两个都可以）

③ 或 (huò)：书面语，常见于正式文件、条款或书面表达，功能与"或者"相似但更简洁。
例：请带身份证或护照。（书面文件风格）

总结：问句用还是，陈述句用或者，书面语用或。
① 还是 (háishi): Used in questions — choosing between two options (the listener must pick one).
E.g., Would you like tea or coffee? (you must choose)

② 或者 (huòzhě): Used in statements — both options are acceptable, no forced choice.
E.g., On the weekend we can go hiking or swimming. (either is fine)

③ 或 (huò): Written/formal register — found in official documents, rules, and formal writing. Functions like 或者 but more concise.
E.g., Please bring your ID card or passport. (formal written style)

Summary: Use 还是 in questions, 或者 in statements, 或 in formal/written contexts.

看来 vs 好像 vs 似乎 Three Ways to Express Inference
三个词都表示"based on what I see/know, I conclude that…"，但用法有细微差别：

看来 (kànlái)：常位于句首，说话人根据观察作出有把握的推断，语气较肯定。
例：电脑一直没有声音，看来是哪里出了问题。

好像 (hǎoxiàng)：语气较软，表示"看起来像是……"，可以是主观感觉，不太确定。
例：她好像不太高兴。（也许是，也许不是）

似乎 (sìhū)：书面语，语气介于两者之间，正式场合或写作中较常用。
例：他似乎知道这件事，但没有说出来。

口诀：肯定用看来，委婉用好像，书面用似乎。
All three express "based on what I observe/know, I infer that…" but with subtle differences:

看来 (kànlái): Often sentence-initial; the speaker makes a fairly confident inference from observation.
E.g., The computer has had no sound all along — it seems something is wrong with it.

好像 (hǎoxiàng): Softer tone; "looks like / seems as if" — more subjective and uncertain.
E.g., She seems not very happy. (maybe, maybe not)

似乎 (sìhū): Written register; tone between the other two; more common in formal writing.
E.g., He seemed to know about it but didn't say anything.

Rule of thumb: Use 看来 for confident inferences, 好像 for tentative ones, 似乎 in written/formal contexts.
