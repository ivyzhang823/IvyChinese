第一课 · Lesson 01

我们去机场接你们

Wǒmen qù jīchǎng jiē nǐmen

We will pick you up at the airport

Text 1 · 在家里
At home — Liu Ming and Wang Yixue chat on the sofa
刘明
这是杨同乐吗？他怎么跟白家月在一起？
Zhè shì Yáng Tónglè ma? Tā zěnme gēn Bái Jiāyuè zài yīqǐ?
Is this Yang Tongle? How come he's with Bai Jiayue?
王一雪
他不是杨同乐，他叫李文，是白家月的好朋友。
Tā bú shì Yáng Tónglè, tā jiào Lǐ Wén, shì Bái Jiāyuè de hǎo péngyou.
He's not Yang Tongle — his name is Li Wen, and he's Bai Jiayue's good friend.
刘明
我还以为是同乐呢，他们看上去有点儿像。
Wǒ hái yǐwéi shì Tónglè ne, tāmen kàn shàngqù yǒudiǎnr xiàng.
I thought it was Tongle — they look a bit alike.
王一雪
是长得有点儿像，但是他比同乐高，身高有一米八。
Shì zhǎng de yǒudiǎnr xiàng, dànshì tā bǐ Tónglè gāo, shēngāo yǒu yī mǐ bā.
They do look a bit similar, but he's taller than Tongle — he's 1.8 metres tall.
刘明
看起来也比同乐瘦一点儿。
Kàn qǐlai yě bǐ Tónglè shòu yīdiǎnr.
He also looks a bit thinner than Tongle.
王一雪
下个星期家月和李文要来北京，我去机场接他们。
Xià ge xīngqī Jiāyuè hé Lǐ Wén yào lái Běijīng, wǒ qù jīchǎng jiē tāmen.
Next week Jiayue and Li Wen are coming to Beijing — I'll go pick them up at the airport.
刘明
他们哪天到北京？我跟你一起去机场接他们吧。
Tāmen nǎ tiān dào Běijīng? Wǒ gēn nǐ yīqǐ qù jīchǎng jiē tāmen ba.
Which day are they arriving in Beijing? I'll go to the airport with you to meet them.
Text 2 · 在机场行李领取处
At the airport baggage claim — Li Wen and Bai Jiayue
白家月
我的行李怎么还没出来？是不是丢了？
Wǒ de xíngli zěnme hái méi chūlai? Shì bú shì diū le?
Why hasn't my luggage come out yet? Could it be lost?
李文
你的行李箱是什么样的？
Nǐ de xínglixiāng shì shénme yàng de?
What does your suitcase look like?
白家月
是一个黑色的箱子，上面写着我的名字和电话号码。
Shì yī ge hēisè de xiāngzi, shàngmiàn xiězhe wǒ de míngzi hé diànhuà hàomǎ.
It's a black suitcase with my name and phone number written on it.
李文
我好像在哪儿看到过这个箱子，是不是有人拿错了？
Wǒ hǎoxiàng zài nǎr kàn dào guò zhège xiāngzi, shì bú shì yǒu rén ná cuò le?
I think I've seen this suitcase somewhere — could someone have taken the wrong one?
白家月
我们快找谁问一下吧，箱子里有不少重要的东西。
Wǒmen kuài zhǎo shéi wèn yīxià ba, xiāngzi lǐ yǒu bù shǎo zhòngyào de dōngxi.
Let's quickly find someone to ask — there are quite a few important things in that suitcase.
李文
别着急，我们拿着护照和机票，去服务台问问吧。
Bié zháojí, wǒmen názhe hùzhào hé jīpiào, qù fúwùtái wènwen ba.
Don't worry — let's take our passports and tickets and go ask at the service desk.
Text 3 · 在机场大厅
In the airport arrival hall — Liu Ming and Wang Yixue waiting
刘明
飞机早就到了，你看见白家月他们了吗？
Fēijī zǎo jiù dào le, nǐ kànjiàn Bái Jiāyuè tāmen le ma?
The plane landed a while ago — have you spotted Bai Jiayue and the others?
王一雪
没有，他们应该快出来了。
Méiyǒu, tāmen yīnggāi kuài chūlai le.
Not yet — they should be coming out soon.
刘明
我们站到中间去吧，这样他们好找一些。
Wǒmen zhàn dào zhōngjiān qù ba, zhèyàng tāmen hǎo zhǎo yīxiē.
Let's move to the middle — that way they'll be able to spot us more easily.
王一雪
你看那个高个子的人是李文吗？
Nǐ kàn nàge gāo gèzi de rén shì Lǐ Wén ma?
Do you think that tall person over there is Li Wen?
刘明
你说的是哪个？那个穿着黑衣服的短头发的年轻人？
Nǐ shuō de shì nǎge? Nàge chuānzhe hēi yīfu de duǎn tóufa de niánqīng rén?
Which one do you mean? The young person with short hair wearing black clothes?
王一雪
对，那个人就是李文！你看，家月在他后面呢。
Duì, nàge rén jiùshì Lǐ Wén! Nǐ kàn, Jiāyuè zài tā hòumiàn ne.
Yes, that's Li Wen! Look — Jiayue is right behind him.
Text 4 · 李文的日记
Li Wen's diary — in the hotel room
昨天我到北京了。虽然路上花了十几个小时，但是跟家月坐在一起，我觉得时间过得很快。到机场的时候，家月发现行李箱不见了，我带她去服务台，帮助她找到了行李箱。走出机场的时候，已经八点多了，我们一出来就看见了王老师的姐姐，她看起来比照片上年轻。晚上，一雪姐带我们去吃了一家很好吃的中国菜。
Zuótiān wǒ dào Běijīng le. Suīrán lùshang huā le shí jǐ ge xiǎoshí, dànshì gēn Jiāyuè zuò zài yīqǐ, wǒ juéde shíjiān guò de hěn kuài. Dào jīchǎng de shíhou, Jiāyuè fāxiàn xínglixiāng bú jiàn le, wǒ dài tā qù fúwùtái, bāngzhù tā zhǎodào le xínglixiāng. Zǒuchū jīchǎng de shíhou, yǐjīng bā diǎn duō le, wǒmen yī chūlai jiù kànjiàn le Wáng lǎoshī de jiějie, tā kàn qǐlai bǐ zhàopiàn shàng niánqīng. Wǎnshang, Yīxuě jiě dài wǒmen qù chī le yī jiā hěn hǎochī de Zhōngguó cài.
Yesterday I arrived in Beijing. Although the journey took over ten hours, sitting with Jiayue made the time pass quickly. At the airport, Jiayue discovered her suitcase was missing. I took her to the service desk and helped her find it. By the time we walked out of the airport it was already past eight o'clock. The moment we came out we spotted Teacher Wang's older sister — she looks younger in person than in photos. In the evening, Sister Yixue took us out for a delicious Chinese meal.

1固定短语"看上去／看起来" · Set Phrases "看上去 / 看起来"

"看上去"与"看起来"都可用来描述某人或某物的外观或状态，意思相近，可以互换。
Both phrases describe the appearance or condition of a person or object and are largely interchangeable.

看上去 / 看起来 + Adj / Sentence
他们看上去有点儿像。
They look a bit alike.
这些菜看起来都很好吃。
This food looks delicious.
那个新来的老师看上去很年轻。
The new teacher looks very young.
她看起来比照片上年轻。
She looks younger than in her photo.
2疑问代词的非疑问用法 · Non-Interrogative Use of Question Words

疑问代词"哪儿、什么、谁、哪"等用在陈述句中，表示不知道或不需要指明的地点、事物或人等。
Question words like 哪儿, 什么, 谁, 哪 can appear in statements to indicate unspecified places, things, or people — similar to "somewhere", "something", "someone" in English.

疑问代词（哪儿 / 什么 / 谁 / 哪）used in declarative sentences
我好像在哪儿看到过这个箱子。
I think I've seen this suitcase somewhere.
你吃点儿什么再走吧。

我们快找谁问一下吧。

我们哪天一起去踢足球吧。
Let's go play football together some day.
3多项定语 · Multiple Attributives

名词性中心语的前面可以同时有多个定语，一般按照时间/处所、动作/状态、特点的顺序排列。数量短语或指量短语位置比较灵活。
A noun can be modified by multiple attributives simultaneously, generally ordered: time/location → action/state → characteristic.

（时间/处所）+（动作/状态）+（特点）+ 的 + 名词
那个穿着黑衣服的短头发的年轻人是李文。
That young person wearing black clothes with short hair is Li Wen.
昨天和家月一起看电影的两个人是我的朋友。
The two people who watched a film with Jiayue yesterday are my friends.
我那件新买的白色的衣服在哪儿？
Where is my newly-bought white clothing?
那本你昨天借给我的红色的书放在桌子上。
That red book you lent me yesterday is on the table.

在中国坐飞机 Flying in China

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
以为 vs 认为 Similarities and Differences
相同点：都表示"认为"，都是动词。

不同点：
① 以为：常用于表示误解或主观判断，后来发现是错的。强调"原来以为……（但实际上不是）"。
例：我以为他是李文，没想到不是。

② 认为：表示经过思考后的客观判断或观点，不含"误解"的意思，可以是正确的。
例：我认为学中文很有意思。
Similarity: Both mean "to think/consider" and are verbs.

Differences:
① 以为: Often implies a mistaken belief or subjective assumption that turned out to be wrong. Emphasizes "I thought (but was wrong)."
E.g., I thought he was Li Wen — turns out he wasn't.

② 认为: Expresses a considered opinion or judgment based on reasoning. Does not imply error; can be a correct view.
E.g., I think studying Chinese is very interesting.

身高的表达 Expressing Height in Chinese
中文表达身高的方式：身高 + 数字 + 米（mǐ），或直接说数字。

例：他身高有一米八。（= 1.80m）
她大概一米六五。（= 1.65m）

注意：中文用"米"和"厘米（límǐ）"，不用英尺或英寸。一米八大约等于5英尺11英寸。
In Chinese, height is expressed as: 身高 + number + 米, or just the numbers directly.

E.g., 他身高有一米八 = He is 1.80 m tall.
她大概一米六五 = She's about 1.65 m.

Note: Chinese uses metres (米) and centimetres (厘米 límǐ), not feet or inches. 一米八 ≈ 5 ft 11 in.

1并列复句"又…又…" · Coordinate Sentence "又…又…"

"又…又…"用在形容词前，表示一个人或事物同时具有两种特点；也可用在动词前，表示同时进行的两种动作或状态。
"又…又…" appears before adjectives (or verbs) to indicate that a person or thing has two qualities — or is performing two actions — at the same time.

又 + Adj₁ + 又 + Adj₂　／　又 + V₁ + 又 + V₂
现在还真是又饿又渴。
Right now I'm really both hungry and thirsty.
这里的饭菜又便宜又好吃。
The food here is both cheap and delicious.
那个女孩儿又高又漂亮。
That girl is both tall and beautiful.
快要考试的时候又忙又累。
Around exam time, I'm both busy and tired.
2疑问代词的非疑问用法（2）· Non-Interrogative Use of Question Words (2)

疑问代词"哪、哪儿、什么、谁、怎么"等可以用来表示"任指"——指任何一个人、东西、地方、方式或时间。句中常用副词"都"与之呼应。
Question words like 哪, 哪儿, 什么, 谁, 怎么 can express general / arbitrary reference — "any person / anything / anywhere / any way." The adverb 都 is usually used to echo them.

疑问代词 + …… + 都 + V / Adj
哪个菜都好吃。
Every dish is delicious.
这次旅游，我去哪儿都可以。
For this trip, I'm fine going anywhere.
我下午有时间，你什么时候来都没问题。
I'm free in the afternoon — you can come any time.
家里什么吃的都没有。
There's nothing at all to eat at home.
3"想…就…" / 两个相同疑问代词的呼应 · Echoing Question Words

两个相同的疑问代词前后呼应，表示任指——指同一个人、同一件事、同一个地点、同一种方式等。当主语相同时，第二个主语可省略；不同时，第二个主语放在"就"前面。
Two identical question words echo each other to refer to the same person / thing / place / manner. If the two clauses share a subject, the second is dropped; otherwise, place the second subject before 就.

疑问代词 + V₁ + ……，就 + V₂ + 同一疑问代词
你们想吃什么就点什么。
Order whatever you feel like.
你觉得哪个好看就买哪个。

你想去哪儿，我们就去哪儿。

你什么时候来都行，我什么时候都在家。

🏮 Culture · 文化
筷子 Chopsticks
筷子是中国人最常用的餐具，已经有三千多年的历史。中式筷子一般比较长，上方下圆，多用木头或竹子做成。中国人用筷子有不少"礼貌规矩"：不能把筷子直直地插在饭里（很像祭拜的样子），也不能用筷子敲碗、用筷子指着别人。在饭桌上，主人通常会很热情地给客人夹菜，说"多吃点儿，别客气"，这是中国饮食文化里非常常见的表达。
Chopsticks have been the main eating utensil in China for over three thousand years. Chinese chopsticks are typically longer than Japanese or Korean ones, square at the top and round at the bottom, and most often made of wood or bamboo. There are several etiquette rules: never stick chopsticks straight up in a bowl of rice (it resembles incense at funerals), never tap the bowl with chopsticks, and never point at people with them. At the table, hosts often warmly serve food to guests, saying things like "eat more, don't be shy" — a hallmark of Chinese dining culture.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
又 vs 再 "Both/Again" — Different Roles
相同点：都和"重复、添加"有关，但用法完全不同。

不同点：
① 又：常用于"又…又…"结构，表示两种特点或两种动作同时存在；也可以表示一个动作"已经"重复发生了。
例：又饿又渴。 / 他又来了。

② 再：表示动作"将要"重复，常和未来动作连用。
例：我要再来吃一次。 / 请再说一遍。
Similarity: Both relate to repetition or addition, but their use is very different.

Differences:
① 又: Used in the "又…又…" pattern to express two simultaneous features/actions; can also indicate a repetition that has already happened.
E.g., 又饿又渴 (both hungry and thirsty); 他又来了 (he came again).

② 再: Marks a repetition that is going to happen — used with future actions.
E.g., 我要再来吃一次 (I want to come back and eat again); 请再说一遍 (please say it again).

"上／下 + 量词"主要用来表示时间顺序："上"表示过去，"下"表示将来。

3副词"就"和"才"的对比 · Comparing "就" and "才"

副词"就"和"才"用在动词前作状语。"就"表示说话人认为动作发生得早、进行得快、顺利；"才"则相反——表示动作发生得晚、进行得慢、不顺利。
Both "就" and "才" come before a verb. "就" expresses that the speaker views the action as early, fast, or smooth; "才" expresses the opposite — late, slow, or not smooth.

🏮 Culture · 文化
在中国租房 Renting an Apartment in China
在中国大城市，"租房"是很常见的选择。中国的住宅一般在"小区"里——一个有围墙、保安和绿化（lǜhuà, landscaping）的住宅区，里面有花园、停车场，有时候还有超市和健身房。租房的时候，房东（fángdōng, landlord）一般要求"押一付三"（押一个月房租做押金，一次付三个月房租）。除了房租，租客还要付水电费、煤气费、网费等。中国年轻人换房子很常见，因为工作变动或家里情况变化都可能让人搬家。
In big Chinese cities, renting is the most common housing choice. Chinese apartments are usually inside a "小区" — a residential complex with walls, security guards, landscaping, and amenities like parking, sometimes a supermarket or a gym. Landlords typically require "押一付三" (a one-month deposit plus three months' rent up front). On top of rent, tenants also pay water, electricity, gas, and internet bills. Young people in China move quite often, as job changes or family situations frequently prompt a move.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
咱们 vs 我们 "Inclusive We" vs. Plain "We"
相同点：都表示"我们"，都是代词。

不同点：
① 咱们：一定包括听话的人——"你和我"。语气亲切、口语化，常见于北方话。
例：咱们换一个房子吧。（你和我一起）

② 我们：可以包括听话人，也可以不包括，要看上下文。
例：明天我们去打扫新家，你也来吗？（"我们"可能不包括"你"）
Similarity: Both are pronouns meaning "we".

Differences:
① 咱们: Always includes the listener — "you and I". It's friendly, colloquial, and especially common in northern Chinese.
E.g., 咱们换一个房子吧 = Let's switch homes (you and I together).

② 我们: May or may not include the listener — depends on context.
E.g., 明天我们去打扫新家 = Tomorrow we'll go clean the new place (the listener may or may not be included).
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
"V + 着"表示状态持续 "V + 着" — Ongoing State
动词后面加"着"，表示动作或状态正在持续。常用于描写场景或对话中。

1固定格式"一…也/都+不/没…" · Fixed Pattern for Complete Negation

"一…也／都+不／没…"表示完全否定——"一点也不／没…"。"一+量词" 也可换成"一点儿"。当谓语是形容词时，常用"一点儿也／都+不／没+形容词"。
The pattern "一 + Measure Word + 也/都 + 不/没 + Verb" expresses complete negation ("not at all"). The "一 + measure word" can be replaced by "一点儿". With an adjective predicate, "一点儿也／都 + 不/没 + Adj" is the typical form.

一 + M.W. + 也/都 + 不/没 + V　／　一点儿 + 也/都 + 不/没 + Adj
草原一点儿也不冷。
The grassland is not cold at all.
司机小李一点儿也没着急。
Driver Xiao Li wasn't impatient at all.
她一件衣服都不想买。
She doesn't want to buy a single piece of clothing.
我一个中国电影也没看过。
I haven't seen a single Chinese movie.
2比较句"A 跟 B 一样" · Comparative Sentences with 一样

"A 跟 B 一样"表示比较后两者相同。后面可加形容词说明比较的方面。否定形式在"一样"前加"不"，即"A 跟 B 不一样"。
"A 跟 B 一样" expresses that A and B are the same. An adjective can follow "一样" to specify the dimension being compared. Negation: place 不 before 一样 ("A 跟 B 不一样" = A is different from B).

A + 跟 + B + (不)一样 + (Adj)
这家宾馆跟别的都不一样。

他买的新手机跟我的一样。

3"除了…(以外)，…都／还／也…" · Exclusion vs. Addition

"除了…(以外)，…都…"表示在某个范围内排除一部分，其他都一样。"除了…(以外)，…还／也…"表示排除一部分，再补充其他。"以外"可省略。
"除了…(以外)，…都…" excludes one part and notes that all others share the same situation ("everyone except…"). "除了…(以外)，…还/也…" excludes one part and adds another ("besides X, also Y"). The 以外 is optional.

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国的草原旅游 Grassland Tourism in China
中国北方有非常美丽的大草原，最有名的是内蒙古（Nèi Měnggǔ, Inner Mongolia）的呼伦贝尔（Hūlúnbèi'ěr）和锡林郭勒（Xīlínguōlè）草原。每年六到九月是草原旅游的最好时间——天气不冷不热，草绿花开。游客可以骑马、住"蒙古包"（Měnggǔbāo, yurt）、吃羊肉、喝奶茶、晚上看星星和月亮。这种"草原游"已经成了中国人最受欢迎的国内旅游路线之一。
Northern China is home to vast and beautiful grasslands. The most famous are in Inner Mongolia — Hulunbuir and Xilingol. The best season for grassland tourism is June through September: weather is mild, the grass is green, and flowers bloom. Travelers can ride horses, sleep in yurts (蒙古包), eat lamb, drink milk tea, and watch the stars and the moon at night. "草原游" (grassland trips) has become one of the most popular domestic travel options in China.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
晚点 vs 迟到 "Delayed" vs "Late"
相同点：都和"比预定时间晚"有关。

不同点：
① 晚点：用于交通工具（飞机、火车、轮船等）比预定的时间晚。
例：飞机晚点了三十分钟。

② 迟到：用于人比预定时间晚到达约会、上课、上班等。
例：他今天上课迟到了。
Similarity: Both relate to being later than scheduled.

Differences:
① 晚点: Used for transport (planes, trains, boats) running behind schedule.
E.g., 飞机晚点了三十分钟 — the flight is 30 minutes late.

② 迟到: Used for a person arriving late to an appointment, class, or work.
E.g., 他今天上课迟到了 — he was late to class today.

1程度补语"得很" · Complement of Degree "得很"

"得很"用在形容词后面，表示程度高，相当于"很…"。这种说法常见于口语，比"很+形容词"语气更自然、强烈。

2量词重叠 · Reduplication of Measure Words

汉语中很多量词可以重叠，用来强调"在某个范围内每一个成员都具有某种特征"，相当于"每一+量词"，后面常用"都"呼应。
Many Chinese measure words can be reduplicated to mean "every single one within the scope". Equivalent to "每一+M.W."; usually paired with 都. E.g. 张张 = 每张 = every (sheet/photo).

M.W. + M.W. + 都 + V/Adj　≈　每一 + M.W. + 都 …
这些照片张张都非常好看。

3存现句（3）· Existential Sentences (3)

存现句用来描述某个处所有人或事物的"出现"或"消失"。基本结构是：处所词语 + 动词 + 趋向／结果补语 +（动态助词"了"）+ 数量短语 + 人/物。
Existential sentences describe people or things appearing or disappearing at a location. Pattern: Place + Verb + Directional/Result Complement + (了) + Numeral-M.W. + Person/Thing. Note: the "subject" (the new person/thing) comes last.

Place + V + Comp + (了) + Number-M.W. + N
后边走过去两个人。
Two people just walked past behind.
树上飞来了几只鸟。
A few birds flew into the tree.
前面开过来很多车。

🏮 Culture · 文化
爬山在中国 Hiking Culture in China
"爬山"是中国人非常喜欢的一种休闲活动，也是常见的锻炼方式。中国有很多名山——五岳（Wǔ Yuè, the Five Great Mountains）：东岳泰山、西岳华山、南岳衡山、北岳恒山、中岳嵩山。"登泰山而小天下"是孔子说过的一句有名的话。除了名山，每个城市附近一般也有"小山"，周末很多人会一家人或者跟朋友一起去爬。爬山时常见的活动有：拍照、野餐、看日出（rìchū, sunrise）、运动锻炼。
Hiking ("爬山") is a beloved leisure activity in China and a common form of exercise. China has many famous mountains — the "Five Great Mountains" (五岳): Mount Tai in the east, Hua in the west, Heng (Hunan) in the south, Heng (Shanxi) in the north, and Song in the center. Confucius is famously quoted as saying "Climb Mount Tai and the world feels small". Beyond the famous peaks, most cities have nearby smaller hills where families and friends hike on weekends. Typical hiking activities: taking photos, picnics, watching sunrises, and getting exercise.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
难看 vs 难过 vs 难听 "Bad" + Different Senses
共同点：都是"难+感官／心情"的结构，表示"令人不愉快"。

不同点：
① 难看（nánkàn）：用眼睛看，丑、不漂亮。
例：这张照片有点儿难看。

② 难听（nántīng）：用耳朵听，声音不好听。
例：他唱歌真难听。

③ 难过（nánguò）：心情不好、伤心。
例：听到这个消息我很难过。
Shared: All follow the pattern "难 + sense / feeling" — "hard / unpleasant to ___".

Differences:
① 难看 = ugly, unflattering (visually).
E.g., 这张照片有点儿难看 — this photo is a bit unflattering.

② 难听 = bad-sounding (auditorily).
E.g., 他唱歌真难听 — his singing is really bad.

③ 难过 = sad, distressed (emotionally).
E.g., 听到这个消息我很难过 — I felt sad hearing the news.

2假设复句"如果…，就…" · Hypothetical Sentences

"如果"引出假设的情况，"就"引出在这种假设下产生的结果。要注意：第二个分句的主语必须放在"就"的前面，不能放在"就"的后面。

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国的高铁 High-Speed Rail in China
中国的高铁（高速铁路 gāosù tiělù）是世界上最大的高速铁路网，总长超过四万公里。最快的高铁时速可以达到350公里。从北京到上海一千多公里，坐高铁只需要四个半小时左右。买票可以用12306（官方App）或者别的旅行App，外国人可以用护照买票和检票进站。高铁站一般在城市的边上，但是有地铁、公交车等可以方便地到达。高铁车厢（chēxiāng, train carriage）里又安静又干净，还可以点外卖——只要选择停靠的车站，到站时服务员就会把饭送到你的座位上。
China's high-speed rail (高速铁路) is the world's largest network, with over 40,000 km of track. The fastest trains reach 350 km/h. From Beijing to Shanghai, more than 1,000 km, the high-speed train takes about 4½ hours. Tickets are bought via the official 12306 app or other travel apps; foreigners can use passports for booking and gate entry. Stations are usually on city outskirts but are well-connected by metro and bus. Train carriages are quiet and clean, and you can even order takeout — pick a station stop and your food is delivered to your seat when the train arrives.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
以前 vs 后来 "Before" vs "Afterward"
共同点：都是表示时间的名词。

不同点：
① 以前（yǐqián）：表示某个时间点之前。可以指过去，也可以指某个具体时间之前。
例：二十分钟以前就到了。 / 我以前不喜欢坐火车。

② 后来（hòulái）：表示某件过去的事情之后又发生了什么。只用于过去，不能指将来。
例：这条路我走过一次，后来再也不走了。

注意：表示"将来"的"以后"是以后（yǐhòu），不是"后来"。
Shared: Both are time nouns.

Differences:
① 以前: "Before" — points in time before now or before another moment. Used for both past and reference points.
E.g., 二十分钟以前就到了 — we'd have arrived 20 minutes ago. / 我以前不喜欢坐火车 — I used to dislike trains.

② 后来: "Afterward" — what happened after something in the past. Past only, never future.
E.g., 这条路我走过一次，后来再也不走了 — I took this road once and never again afterward.

Note: For future "after / later", use 以后 yǐhòu, NOT 后来.
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
"V + 上"表示实现目标 "V + 上" — Achieving a Goal
除了第5课学过的"穿上、拿上"（动作的方向），动词后的"上"还可以表示实现某个目标或愿望。

常用搭配：
坐上高铁（finally got to ride the high-speed train）
买上票（managed to buy a ticket）
住上新房（got to live in a new home）
用上新手机（finally using the new phone）

例：早就听说过高铁，终于可以坐上了。
Beyond the directional "V + 上" from Lesson 5 (穿上, 拿上), 上 after a verb can mean achieving a long-awaited goal or desire — "finally getting to V".

Common pairs:
坐上 — finally got to ride
买上 — managed to buy
住上 — got to live in (a new place)
用上 — finally using

E.g., 早就听说过高铁，终于可以坐上了 — I've heard about high-speed rail forever; I finally get to ride it.

中国人的购物方式 Shopping in China
中国人的购物方式这些年变化非常大。以前大家都去商场、菜市场（càishìchǎng, wet market）买东西；现在网购（wǎnggòu, online shopping）已经成了大部分人的主要方式。淘宝、京东、拼多多等是最大的购物平台（píngtái, platform）。每年的"双十一"（11月11日）和"618"（6月18日）是中国最大的购物节，很多商品打折非常厉害。在水果店、菜市场买东西时还可以"砍价"，但商场和网店的价格一般是固定的。手机支付（zhīfù, payment）非常方便——用微信或支付宝扫码就能付钱，几乎不用带现金。
Chinese shopping habits have changed enormously. People once shopped mostly at malls and wet markets; now online shopping is the main mode for most. Taobao, JD.com, and Pinduoduo are the largest platforms. The biggest shopping festivals are "Double 11" (Nov 11) and "618" (June 18) — huge discounts. 比较句的家族 The 比 / 没有 / 不比 / 跟…一样 Family
① A 比 B + Adj：A 大于 B（A>B）。
例：裙子比短裤贵。（Skirt > shorts）

② A 比 B + 更/还 + Adj：同样大于，但程度更深。
例：那条裙子比短裤更好看。

③ A 没有 B + Adj：A 小于 B（A<B）。
例：我没有他高。（I < him）

④ A 不比 B + Adj：A 和 B 差不多（强调"不见得多"）。
例：他的中文不比你好。

⑤ A 跟 B 一样 (+ Adj)：A 等于 B。
例：这家宾馆跟别的不一样。
① A 比 B + Adj — A is more (A > B). Skirt is more expensive than shorts.

② A 比 B 更/还 + Adj — same direction, even more so. That skirt is even prettier than the shorts.

③ A 没有 B + Adj — A is less (A < B). I'm not as tall as him.

④ A 不比 B + Adj — A and B are about the same (push-back / "not really more"). His Chinese isn't really better than yours.

⑤ A 跟 B 一样 (+ Adj) — A equals B. This hotel is unlike any other (i.e. NOT the same).

1趋向补语的引申用法（1）"V/Adj + 下去" · "Keep …ing"

趋向补语"下去"用在动词或形容词后面，表示已经开始的动作或状态还要继续。带有"持续到将来"的意思。
"下去" after a verb or adjective means "to keep doing / keep being …" — the action or state has already begun and is to continue into the future.

V / Adj + 下去
我今年胖了十多斤，不能再胖下去了。
I've gained over 10 jin this year — can't keep getting heavier.
她读完了第一个问题，后面的你读下去。
She finished question 1; you keep reading from there.
这几天太热了，如果再热下去就不能出门了。
It's been so hot these days — if it keeps being this hot we can't go out.
这本书太有意思了，我想一直看下去。
This book is so interesting — I want to keep reading.
2离合词（2）补语的位置 · Separable Verbs + Complement

离合词（如：游泳、住院、结婚、跑步、感冒）一般不能直接加宾语。如果句中有补语（结果补语、时量补语、动量补语等），补语要放在离合词的中间。
Separable verbs (e.g. 游-泳, 住-院, 结-婚, 跑-步) cannot directly take an object. Complements (result, duration, count) go between the two parts, not after.

V₁ + Complement + V₂　(where V₁V₂ = a separable verb)
昨天游完泳以后，耳朵一直有点儿疼。
After finishing swimming yesterday, my ear has been hurting. (游 + 完 + 泳)
今年夏天只下了两次雨。
It only rained twice this summer. (下 + 两次 + 雨)
小张下个月跟他女朋友结婚。
Next month Little Zhang is getting married to his girlfriend.
他住了一个星期院。
He spent a week in the hospital. (住 + 一个星期 + 院)
3时量补语（2）"动作完成后的时长" · Time Elapsed Since Completion

时量补语用在动词后面，表示动作或状态持续的时长。如果动词表示不能持续的动作（来、去、出院、毕业…），动词后的时量补语表示动作完成到说话时已过去多久。
For non-durative verbs (come, go, leave hospital, graduate…), the duration after the verb tells you how long ago the action was completed — i.e. how much time has passed since.

V + (object) + Time Phrase + 了
我上次来医院已经过去差不多两年了。
It's been almost 2 years since I last came to the hospital.
她回家两个月了。
She went home two months ago. (= 2 months have passed since she went home)
开学已经三个多星期了。
It's been over three weeks since school started.
你来中国多长时间了？
How long has it been since you came to China?
4固定格式"…以前/以后/前/后" · Before / After

"时间/事件 + 以前／前"表示某个时间或事件之前；"时间/事件 + 以后／后"表示某个时间或事件之后。短形式（前/后）多用于固定搭配，如"睡前、饭后、出院前、下班后"。
Time/Event + 以前/前 = before; Time/Event + 以后/后 = after. The short forms (前/后) are common in fixed expressions like 睡前 (before bed), 饭后 (after a meal), 出院前 (before discharge).

Time/Event + 以前 / 前　|　Time/Event + 以后 / 后
这种药需要每天睡前吃一次。
This medicine needs to be taken once a day before bed.
其他几种每天吃三次，饭后吃。
The others, three times a day, after meals.
我们决定下班后一起去看个电影。
We decided to go see a movie together after work.
考完试以后，咱们一起去旅游吧。

共同点：都和"关注某人/某事"有关。

✏️ Grammar · 语言点
三种"得"的读音 Three Readings of 得
汉字"得"有三种读音，意思和用法都不同：

① de（轻声）：结构助词，连接动词和补语。
例：他跑得很快。 / 她说得太好了。

② dé：动词"得到、获得"。
例：他得了第一名。 / 我没得到答案。

③ děi（本课）：助动词"应该、必须"。
例：你病了，得多休息。 / 还得做完检查才能知道。
The character 得 has three different readings, each with a different meaning and use:

① de (neutral tone): structural particle linking verb and complement.
E.g., 他跑得很快 (he runs fast); 她说得太好了 (she speaks beautifully).

② dé: verb "to obtain, to get".
E.g., 他得了第一名 (he got first place); 我没得到答案 (I didn't get the answer).

③ děi (this lesson): modal "should, must, have to".
E.g., 你病了，得多休息 — you're sick, you need to rest more. / 还得做完检查才能知道 — we have to finish tests first.

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
得分 vs 成绩 Live score vs. achieved result
两个词都与比赛结果有关，但用法不同：

① 得分（défēn）：动词/名词，表示在比赛中得到的分数，常用于进行中的比赛。
例：你们告诉我得分吧。（Tell me the score.）/ 今天的比赛他得了20分。

② 成绩（chéngjì）：名词，表示最终取得的结果或成就，可以用于比赛、考试、学业等。
例：虽然他没有得到好成绩，但是他让世界认识了中国。

简单来说：得分 = the score during / after a game；成绩 = the achievement / result overall.
Both relate to competition results, but they're used differently:

① 得分 (défēn): verb/noun — the points scored during a match or game, often used in live situations.
E.g., 你们告诉我得分吧 (Tell me the score). / 今天他得了20分 (He scored 20 points today).

② 成绩 (chéngjì): noun only — the overall result or achievement, used in sports, exams, academics, etc.
E.g., 虽然他没有得到好成绩，但是他让世界认识了中国 (Although he didn't achieve a good result, he introduced China to the world).

Quick rule: 得分 = live/final score; 成绩 = overall achievement.
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
可能补语 vs 程度补语 Potentiality vs Degree Complement
两种补语结构相似，但含义完全不同：

① 程度补语（Degree Complement）：V + 得 + Adj → 描述动作做得怎么样（已实现）。
例：他打得很好。（He plays well — describes the quality of his playing.）

② 可能补语（Potentiality Complement）：V + 得/不 + Result → 表示能否实现某种结果。
例：他打得好吗？（Can he play well? — asks about ability or conditions.）
例：打不好没关系。（It doesn't matter if you can't play well.）

关键区别：程度补语描述已完成动作的质量；可能补语表达能力或可能性。
Two complement types look similar but mean very different things:

① Degree Complement: V + 得 + Adj → describes how an action was performed (result already achieved).
E.g., 他打得很好 (He plays well — describes quality).

② Potentiality Complement: V + 得/不 + Result → asks or states whether a result is achievable.
E.g., 他打得好吗? (Can he play well? — ability/conditions); 打不好没关系 (It doesn't matter if you can't play well).

Key rule: degree complements describe a completed quality; potentiality complements express ability or possibility.

qīngchu / míngbai
clear / understand
adj. / v.
用法说明 · Usage Note

Both relate to clarity, but differ in usage. 清楚: as adj = clear, distinct (记得很清楚); as verb = to be clear about / understand a fact (我清楚这件事). 明白: as verb = to understand / comprehend (我明白了); as adj = clear, reasonable. 明白 is more about comprehending something; 清楚 is more about it being distinct or factually known.

例句 · Example Sentences
我看过你的笔记，记得非常清楚。
I've seen your notes — they're recorded very clearly.
我终于明白了。
I finally understand.
数学考试我没看清楚要求。
I didn't read the requirements clearly in the maths exam.
差
chà
poor; be short of; differ from
adj. / v.
用法说明 · Usage Note

"差" (chà) has two main uses: ① as an adjective meaning "poor quality / below standard" (考得很差, 成绩差); ② as a verb meaning "to be short of / lack" (差一点儿). Note: 差 has another reading 差 (chā) meaning "difference / discrepancy" (误差, 差别). Different tone, different meaning.

常见搭配 · Collocations
考得很差成绩差差得多差一点儿
例句 · Example Sentences

他的成绩越来越差了。
His grades are getting worse and worse.
遍 vs 次
biàn / cì
two measure words for actions
m.
用法说明 · Usage Note

Both count occurrences of actions, but differently. 遍 (biàn): one complete run-through from start to finish — reading a passage once through, watching a film, listening to audio. 次 (cì): one occurrence or instance — can be brief (went once, asked once). 看一遍 = read it all the way through; 看一次 = watched it once (could be partial).

例句 · Example Sentences

Before doing homework, I always read through my notes once (start to finish).
我去过北京两次。
I've been to Beijing twice. (two visits)
再给你们讲一遍。
I'll go through it for you once more.
努力
nǔlì

adj. / v.
用法说明 · Usage Note

As an adjective: 很努力 (very hard-working), 非常努力 (extremely diligent). As a verb (+ optional direction): 努力学习 (study hard), 努力工作 (work hard). The object of effort goes after: 为了考试努力 (work hard for the exam). Unlike 认真 (careful approach), 努力 emphasises the degree of effort expended.

1"把"字句（1）— 位置的改变 · The "把" Sentence (1): Change of Position

"把"字句表示对确定的人或事物做出相应的动作，使其发生位置上的改变。否定副词"不/没"和能愿动词应该放在"把"字的前面。
The "把" sentence indicates performing an action on a definite person or thing that results in a change of position. Negation (不/没) and modals go before 把.

Subject + (不/没/能愿动词) + 把 + Object + Verb + 在/到 + Location
我会把这些题都记在本子上。
I'll write all these problems down in my notebook.
老师把作业本放到桌子上了。
The teacher put the homework books on the desk.
你不能把名字写在这里。
You can't write your name here.
我好像把校园卡忘在宿舍里了。
I think I left my campus card in the dorm.

⚠️ The object must be definite (a specific thing, not "some object"). The verb cannot stand alone — it needs a complement (在/到 + place, 了, 结果补语, etc.).

2固定格式"在……上/中/下" · Fixed Pattern "在……上/中/下"

介词"在"和方位词"上""中""下"组成固定格式，分别表示范围/方面、环境/时间、条件。
The preposition 在 combines with positional words 上, 中, 下 to indicate scope/aspect, environment/time, and condition respectively.

在+N+上（scope）　｜　在+N+中（environment/time）　｜　在+VP+下（condition）
在学习上，遇到什么问题都可以问我。
When it comes to studying, you can ask me anything.
在工作上，他总是能给我很多帮助。
At work, he's always able to help me a great deal.
在比赛中，他得分最高。
During the match, he scored the highest.
在你的影响下，我也开始喜欢拍照了。
Under your influence, I've started to enjoy photography too.

💡 在+N+下 requires a disyllabic verb with an attributive: 在老师的帮助下, 在她的影响下. The noun describes who or what is providing the condition.

3"把"字句（2）— 关系的转移 · The "把" Sentence (2): Relational Transfer

"把"字句还可以表示通过动作使确定的事物发生关系上的转移——即把某物给、送、带、还等给另一方。基本结构：主语+把+宾语₁+动词+给+宾语₂。
A second use of the 把 sentence shows the relational transfer of a thing through an action — giving, sending, bringing, returning something to someone. Structure: S + 把 + O₁ + V + 给 + O₂.

Subject + 把 + Object₁ + Verb + 给 + Object₂
你明天再把书还给我。
You can return the book to me tomorrow.
我把礼物送给她了。
I gave the gift to her.
请你把这本书带给老师。
Please bring this book to the teacher.
我给你看看我拍的照片。
Let me show you the photos I took.

📌 Common transfer verbs with 把…给: 还给（return to）, 送给（give as gift to）, 带给（bring to）, 交给（hand in to）, 借给（lend to）.

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国的教育体系 China's Education System
中国的学校教育一般分为三个阶段：小学（xiǎoxué, 6年）、初中（chūzhōng, 3年）和高中（gāozhōng, 3年）。小学和初中是"义务教育"阶段（9年），国家要求所有孩子必须完成。高中教育不是义务教育，但是绝大多数学生都会继续读高中。高中学习结束后，学生要参加"高考"（gāokǎo，全国统一高等学校招生考试），根据高考成绩申请大学。高考是中国最重要的考试，对学生的未来影响非常大。
Chinese school education is generally divided into three stages: primary school (小学, 6 years), middle school (初中, 3 years), and high school (高中, 3 years). Primary and middle school are "compulsory education" (义务教育, 9 years) — the state requires all children to complete them. High school is not compulsory, but the vast majority of students continue. After high school, students sit the 高考 (Gaokao — the National College Entrance Examination), and apply to universities based on their scores. The Gaokao is the most important exam in China and has a huge influence on students' futures.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
把字句的两种用法 Two uses of the 把 sentence
本课学习了两种"把"字句，它们都要求宾语是特定的事物：

① 位置的改变（把…放在/到/写在…）：强调某物被移到一个新的位置。
例：我会把这些题都记在本子上。（题→本子）
例：老师把作业本放到桌子上了。（作业本→桌子）

② 关系的转移（把…给/还给/送给…）：强调某物通过动作从一方转移到另一方。
例：你明天再把书还给我。（书：学生→老师）
例：我把礼物送给她了。（礼物：我→她）

记住：两种用法都不能用于不确定的事物（不能说"把一本书给我"）。
This lesson introduced two uses of the 把 sentence — both require the object to be a specific, definite thing:

① Change of position (把…放在/到/写在…): highlights that something is moved to a new location.
E.g., 我会把这些题都记在本子上 (problems → notebook); 老师把作业本放到桌子上了 (books → desk).

② Relational transfer (把…给/还给/送给…): highlights that something passes from one party to another through an action.
E.g., 你明天再把书还给我 (book: student → teacher); 我把礼物送给她了 (gift: me → her).

Remember: neither type works with indefinite objects — you cannot say 把一本书给我 (give me a book) using 把.
✏️ Grammar · 语言点
什么时候用"把"字句？ When to use a 把 sentence?
"把"字句不是随时都能用的，需要满足三个条件：

① 宾语必须是特定的（不能用"一个/一些"等不确定的表达）。
✓ 把这本书还给我。（this book = specific）
✗ 把一本书还给我。（a book = indefinite）

② 动词不能单独出现，必须有补语（在/到+地点、结果补语、趋向补语）、"了"或重叠形式。
✓ 把作业本放到桌子上。（V+到+地点）
✗ 把作业本放。（V alone — wrong）

③ 否定词和能愿动词放在"把"前面：不把/没把/可以把/应该把。
✓ 你不能把名字写在这里。
✗ 你把名字不能写在这里。
The 把 sentence has three conditions that must all be met:

① The object must be definite (specific — no 一个/一些 etc.).
✓ 把这本书还给我 (this book — specific). ✗ 把一本书还给我 (a book — indefinite).

② The verb cannot stand alone — it needs a complement (在/到+place, result complement, directional complement), 了, or reduplication.
✓ 把作业本放到桌子上 (V+到+place). ✗ 把作业本放 (V alone).

③ Negation and modals go before 把: 不把, 没把, 可以把, 应该把.
✓ 你不能把名字写在这里. ✗ 你把名字不能写在这里.
