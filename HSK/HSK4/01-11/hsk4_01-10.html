Text 1 · 孙月和王静聊王静的男朋友
Sun Yue and Wang Jing chat about Wang Jing's boyfriend
孙月
听说你男朋友李进跟你是一个学校的，是你同学吗？
Tīngshuō nǐ nánpéngyou Lǐ Jìn gēn nǐ shì yí ge xuéxiào de, shì nǐ tóngxué ma?
I heard your boyfriend Li Jin is from the same school as you — is he your classmate?
王静
是的，他学的是新闻，我学的是法律，我和他不是一个班。
Shì de, tā xué de shì xīnwén, wǒ xué de shì fǎlǜ, wǒ hé tā bú shì yí ge bān.
Yes. He studies journalism and I study law — we're not in the same class.
孙月
那你们俩是怎么认识的？
Nà nǐmen liǎ shì zěnme rènshi de?
Then how did you two meet?
王静
我们是在一次足球比赛中认识的。我们班跟他们班比赛，他一个人踢进两个球，我对他印象很深，后来就慢慢熟悉了。
Wǒmen shì zài yí cì zúqiú bǐsài zhōng rènshi de. Wǒmen bān gēn tāmen bān bǐsài, tā yí ge rén tījìn liǎng ge qiú, wǒ duì tā yìnxiàng hěn shēn, hòulái jiù mànmàn shúxi le.
We met during a football match. My class played his class. He scored two goals on his own — I was deeply impressed, and we slowly got to know each other.
孙月
你为什么喜欢他？
Nǐ wèi shénme xǐhuan tā?
Why do you like him?
王静
他不仅足球踢得好，性格也不错。
Tā bùjǐn zúqiú tī de hǎo, xìnggé yě búcuò.
Not only does he play football well, his personality is great too.
Text 2 · 王静跟李老师聊她要结婚的事情
Wang Jing talks with Teacher Li about getting married
王静
李老师，我下个月5号就要结婚了。
Lǐ lǎoshī, wǒ xià ge yuè wǔ hào jiù yào jié hūn le.
Teacher Li, I'm getting married on the 5th of next month.
李老师
你是在开玩笑吧？你们不是才认识一个月？
Nǐ shì zài kāi wánxiào ba? Nǐmen bú shì cái rènshi yí ge yuè?
You must be kidding! Haven't you only known each other for a month?
王静
虽然我们认识的时间不长，但我从来没这么快乐过。
Suīrán wǒmen rènshi de shíjiān bù cháng, dàn wǒ cónglái méi zhème kuàilèguo.
Although we haven't known each other long, I've never been this happy before.
李老师
两个人在一起，最好能有共同的兴趣和爱好。
Liǎng ge rén zài yìqǐ, zuìhǎo néng yǒu gòngtóng de xìngqù hé àihào.
When two people are together, it's best to share common interests and hobbies.
王静
我们有很多共同的爱好，经常一起打球、唱歌、做菜。
Wǒmen yǒu hěn duō gòngtóng de àihào, jīngcháng yìqǐ dǎqiú, chànggē, zuòcài.
We have many common hobbies — we often play ball, sing, and cook together.
李老师
看来你真的找到适合你的人了。祝你们幸福！
Kànlái nǐ zhēn de zhǎodào shìhé nǐ de rén le. Zhù nǐmen xìngfú!
It really sounds like you've found the right person. Wishing you happiness!
Text 3 · 高老师和李老师聊结婚后的生活
Teachers Gao and Li chat about life after marriage
高老师
听说您跟妻子结婚快二十年了？
Tīngshuō nín gēn qīzi jié hūn kuài èrshí nián le?
I heard you've been married to your wife for almost twenty years?
李老师
到6月9号，我们就结婚二十年了。这么多年，我们的生活一直挺幸福的。
Dào liù yuè jiǔ hào, wǒmen jiù jié hūn èrshí nián le. Zhème duō nián, wǒmen de shēnghuó yìzhí tǐng xìngfú de.
By the 9th of June, we'll have been married twenty years. All these years, our life has always been quite happy.
高老师
我和丈夫刚结婚的时候，每天都觉得很新鲜，在一起有说不完的话。但是现在……
Wǒ hé zhàngfu gāng jié hūn de shíhou, měi tiān dōu juéde hěn xīnxiān, zài yìqǐ yǒu shuō bu wán de huà. Dànshì xiànzài…
When my husband and I had just gotten married, every day felt fresh and we had endless things to say. But now…
李老师
两个人共同生活，只有浪漫和新鲜感是不够的。
Liǎng ge rén gòngtóng shēnghuó, zhǐ yǒu làngmàn hé xīnxiāngǎn shì bú gòu de.
When two people live together, romance and novelty alone are not enough.
高老师
您说的对！我现在每天看到的都是他的缺点。
Nín shuō de duì! Wǒ xiànzài měi tiān kàndào de dōu shì tā de quēdiǎn.
You're right! All I see now every day are his shortcomings.
李老师
两个人在一起时间长了，就会有很多问题。只有接受了他的缺点，你们才能更好地一起生活。
Liǎng ge rén zài yìqǐ shíjiān cháng le, jiù huì yǒu hěn duō wèntí. Zhǐyǒu jiēshòule tā de quēdiǎn, nǐmen cái néng gèng hǎo de yìqǐ shēnghuó.
When two people have been together a long time, many issues arise. Only by accepting his shortcomings can you live together better.
Text 4 · 什么是浪漫？
What is romance?
—
很多女孩子羡慕浪漫的爱情。那什么是浪漫呢？
Hěn duō nǚháizi xiànmù làngmàn de àiqíng. Nà shénme shì làngmàn ne?
Many girls admire romantic love. But what is romance?
—
年轻人说：浪漫是她想要月亮时，你不会给她星星；
Niánqīng rén shuō: Làngmàn shì tā xiǎng yào yuèliang shí, nǐ bú huì gěi tā xīngxing;
Young people say: romance is not giving her stars when she wants the moon;
—
中年人说：浪漫是即使晚上加班到零点，到家时，自己家里也还亮着灯；
Zhōngnián rén shuō: Làngmàn shì jíshǐ wǎnshang jiā bān dào língdiǎn, dào jiā shí, zìjǐ jiā lǐ yě hái liàngzhe dēng;
Middle-aged people say: romance is, even if you work overtime until midnight, coming home to a light still on at home;
—
老年人说：浪漫其实就像歌中唱的那样，"我能想到最浪漫的事，就是和你一起慢慢变老。"
Lǎonián rén shuō: Làngmàn qíshí jiù xiàng gē zhōng chàng de nàyàng, "Wǒ néng xiǎngdào zuì làngmàn de shì, jiù shì hé nǐ yìqǐ mànmàn biàn lǎo."
Elderly people say: romance is really like the song — "The most romantic thing I can think of is growing old slowly with you."
—
其实，让我们感动的，就是生活中简单的爱情。有时候，简单就是最大的幸福。
Qíshí, ràng wǒmen gǎndòng de, jiù shì shēnghuó zhōng jiǎndān de àiqíng. Yǒu shíhou, jiǎndān jiù shì zuì dà de xìngfú.
In fact, what moves us is simple love in everyday life. Sometimes, simplicity is the greatest happiness.
Text 5 · 爱情、结婚与性格
Love, marriage and personality
—
说到结婚，人们就会很自然地想起爱情。
Shuōdào jié hūn, rénmen jiù huì hěn zìrán de xiǎngqǐ àiqíng.
Speaking of marriage, people naturally think of love.
—
爱情是结婚的重要原因，但两个人共同生活，不仅需要浪漫的爱情，更需要性格上互相吸引。
Àiqíng shì jié hūn de zhòngyào yuányīn, dàn liǎng ge rén gòngtóng shēnghuó, bùjǐn xūyào làngmàn de àiqíng, gèng xūyào xìnggé shang hùxiāng xīyǐn.
Love is an important reason for marriage, but living together requires not only romantic love, but also a personality-level mutual attraction.
—
我丈夫是个很幽默的人。即使是很普通的事情，从他嘴里说出来也会变得很有意思。
Wǒ zhàngfu shì ge hěn yōumò de rén. Jíshǐ shì hěn pǔtōng de shìqing, cóng tā zuǐ li shuō chulai yě huì biàn de hěn yǒu yìsi.
My husband is a humorous man. Even very ordinary things, when he says them, become interesting.
—
在我难过的时候，他总是有办法让我高兴起来。
Zài wǒ nánguò de shíhou, tā zǒngshì yǒu bànfǎ ràng wǒ gāoxìng qilai.
When I'm sad, he always finds a way to cheer me up.
—
而且他的脾气也不错，结婚快十年了，我们俩几乎没因为什么事红过脸，很多人都特别羡慕我们。
Érqiě tā de píqi yě búcuò, jié hūn kuài shí nián le, wǒmen liǎ jīhū méi yīnwèi shénme shì hóngguo liǎn, hěn duō rén dōu tèbié xiànmù wǒmen.
And his temper is good too — we've been married almost ten years and have rarely ever quarreled. Many people especially envy us.

1不仅……也/还/而且…… · Not only... but also...

"不仅"用在第一个分句里，第二个分句里常用"也/还/而且"相呼应，表示除第一个分句所说的意思之外，还有更进一层的意思。当两个分句的主语相同时，"不仅"在主语后；当主语不同时，"不仅"在主语前。
"不仅" appears in the first clause; the second clause uses "也/还/而且". When the subject is the same in both clauses, "不仅" follows the subject; when the subjects differ, "不仅" goes before the first subject.

不仅 + V/Adj ， 也/还/而且 + V/Adj
他不仅足球踢得好，性格也不错。
Not only does he play football well, his personality is also great.
长大以后，他不仅身体健康，还成了一名长跑运动员。
As he grew up, not only did he become healthy, he even became a long-distance runner.
听说那边不仅很美，而且人也非常热情。
I heard that not only is it beautiful there, but the people are also very warm.
2从来 · "Always / Never" (from past until now)

"从来"是副词，表示从过去到现在都是这样，多用于否定句中（"从来不"、"从来没"）。
The adverb "从来" indicates something has been a certain way from the past to the present. Most often used in negative sentences with 不 or 没.

Subj. + 从来 + 不/没 + V
虽然我们认识的时间不长，但我从来没这么快乐过。
Although we haven't known each other long, I've never been this happy before.
老张这个人开会从来不迟到。
Lao Zhang has never been late to a meeting.
这家面馆儿从来只卖一种东西：牛肉面。
This noodle shop has always only sold one thing: beef noodles.
3刚 · "Just (a moment ago)"

"刚"是副词，表示行动或情况发生在不久以前，用于主语后、动词前。
The adverb "刚" indicates an action or situation happened a short while ago. Used after the subject and before the verb.

Subj. + 刚 + V ...
我和丈夫刚结婚的时候，每天都觉得很新鲜。
When my husband and I had just gotten married, every day felt fresh.
我上午刚借的那本书，怎么找不到了？
The book I just borrowed this morning — why can't I find it?
我刚从会议室过来，怎么一个人也没有？
I just came from the meeting room — why is nobody there?

对比 · Compare: 刚 vs 刚才
两者都表示动作、情况发生不久。但是：
• "刚"是副词，只能放在主语后、动词前；"刚才"是时间名词，主语前后皆可。
• "刚"前可以有表时间的词语；"刚才"不可以。
• "刚才"后可以有否定词；"刚"不可以。
Both indicate an action that happened not long ago, but "刚" is only adverbial (after subject, before verb), while "刚才" is a temporal noun (can go before or after the subject). "刚" can be preceded by a time word; "刚才" cannot. "刚才" can be followed by a negative; "刚" cannot.

我上个月刚结婚。 ✓ / 上个月我刚结婚。 ✓
I just got married last month.
刚才我去洗手间了，你给我打电话了？
A moment ago I went to the restroom — did you call me?
你为什么刚才不说，现在才说？
Why didn't you say it just now, only now?
4即使……也…… · "Even if... still..."

"即使"用在第一个分句里，第二个分句常用"也"相呼应。一般表示假设的让步关系，也可以表示已经发生或存在的事情。"即使"可以用在主语前，也可以用在主语后。
The conjunction "即使" appears in the first clause and pairs with "也" in the second. It expresses hypothetical concession, or sometimes a concession based on something already happening. "即使" can be placed before or after the subject.

即使 + Clause 1 ， 也 + Clause 2
浪漫是即使晚上加班到零点，到家时，自己家里也还亮着灯。
Romance is — even if you work overtime until midnight — a light still on at home when you arrive.
你应该多回家看看老人，即使只是跟他们吃吃饭、聊聊天，他们也会觉得很幸福。
You should go home to see the elderly more often — even just eating and chatting with them makes them feel happy.
有些人即使只睡5个小时也没问题。
Some people have no problem even if they only sleep 5 hours.

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国的情人节——七夕节Qīxī Jié — Qixi Festival, Chinese Valentine's Day
每年农历七月初七这一天是中国汉族的传统节日"七夕节"。因为当天活动的主要参加者是少女，而节日活动的内容又是以"乞巧（穿针引线、做些小物品、摆上些瓜果）"为主，所以人们称这天为"乞巧节"或"少女节"、"女儿节"。"七夕节"来源于牛郎和织女的爱情故事，是中国传统节日中最具浪漫色彩的节日，也被认为是"中国的情人节"。
The seventh day of the seventh month in the lunar calendar is the annual Qixi Festival (literally Seventh-Eve Festival), a traditional festival of the Chinese Han ethnic group. As the main participants are young girls and the activities centre on "praying for skills" (needlework, making small handicrafts, offering fruit), the day is also called "Pleading-for-Skills Festival", "Maiden Festival", or "Girls' Day". Rooted in the love story of the Cowherd and the Weaver Girl, the Qixi Festival is the most romantic traditional festival in China and is recognized as the "Chinese Valentine's Day".

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
刚 — 刚才gāng vs gāngcái
相同点：两者都表示行动、情况发生在不久以前，都可以做状语。
不同点：
1. "刚"是副词，只能放在主语后、动词前；"刚才"是时间名词，可以用在主语前，也可以用在主语后。
2. "刚"前面可以有表示时间的词语；"刚才"前面不能有。
3. 用"刚"的句子，动词后面可以有表示时量的词语；"刚才"不行。
4. "刚才"后可以用否定词；"刚"不行。
Similarity: Both indicate that an action or situation took place not long ago; both can serve as an adverbial modifier.
Differences:
1. 刚 is an adverb — only used after the subject and before the verb. 刚才 is a temporal noun — can go before or after the subject.
2. 刚 can be preceded by a temporal word; 刚才 cannot.
3. With 刚, the verb can be followed by a duration word; with 刚才, this is not possible.
4. 刚才 can be followed by a negative word; 刚 cannot.

1正好 · "Just right / just in time"

"正好"可以做形容词，表示（时间、位置、体积、数量、程度等）正合适；也可以做副词，表示恰好或遇到某个机会做某事。
"正好" as an adjective means "just right" (a condition is met: not too early, not too big, etc.). As an adverb, it means "it's the right time or opportunity to do something".

(Adj.) 正好 / (Adv.) 正好 + VP
苹果八块五，西瓜十一块五，正好二十块。
Apples 8.5, watermelon 11.5 — exactly 20 yuan.
你来得正好，我的钱包不见了，借我点儿钱吧。
You've come just in time — I lost my wallet, lend me some money.
我们下午要去踢足球，正好一起去吧。
We're going to play football this afternoon — perfect chance to go together.
2差不多 · "Almost / approximately"

"差不多"可以做形容词，表示相近、差别不大；也可做副词，表示程度、范围、时间、距离、数量等方面很接近。
"差不多" as an adjective means "similar, without much difference". As an adverb, it means "close to" (in degree, scope, time, distance, or quantity).

Adj. · 差不多 / Adv. 差不多 + NP
这两个电子词典看上去差不多，左边这个怎么这么贵？
These two e-dictionaries look pretty similar — why is the one on the left so expensive?
他们俩是在国外旅游的时候认识的，性格差不多，所以很快就成了好朋友。
They met while traveling abroad; their personalities are quite similar, so they quickly became good friends.
差不多一半儿吧。
About half.

对比 · Compare: 差不多 vs 几乎
两者都是副词，表示非常接近，相近，都可以做状语，但：
1. "差不多"可直接用在单音节形容词前；"几乎"前后需加别的词语。
2. "差不多"可用在数量词前；"几乎"不行。
3. "差不多"还可做形容词做谓语；"几乎"不行。
4. "几乎"可表示不希望发生的事接近实现但最后没有；"差不多"不行。
Both are adverbs meaning "nearly / almost". Differences: 差不多 attaches directly to a monosyllabic adjective, can precede numerals, and can act as a predicate; 几乎 needs extra words, cannot precede numerals, cannot predicate — but 几乎 can express "something almost happened" (often unwanted).

他们俩差不多高。 / 他们俩几乎一样高。
Those two are about the same height.
李老师差不多六十岁了。
Teacher Li is nearly 60.
这件事我几乎忘了告诉你了。
I almost forgot to tell you about this.
3尽管 · "Although / even though"

"尽管"是连词，一般用在复句的前一分句，提出一个事实，后一分句是一般情况下不会出现的结果，常与"但是、可是、却、还是"搭配。
"尽管" is a conjunction used in the first clause to state a fact, while the second clause introduces an unexpected result — often paired with 但是/可是/却/还是.

尽管 + Fact , 但是/可是/却/还是 + Result
尽管已经毕业这么多年，我们还是经常联系的。
Even though we've graduated so many years ago, we still keep in touch.
男朋友送给我的这份礼物尽管不贵，但却让我非常感动。
Although my boyfriend's gift wasn't expensive, it moved me deeply.
尽管现在工作很忙，可到了周末他还是会跟朋友去打球。
Even though he's very busy at work, he still goes to play ball with friends on weekends.
4却 · "But / yet" (soft transition)

"却"是副词，用于主语后、动词前，表示转折，语气比较轻。
The adverb "却" goes after the subject and before the verb to mark a softer contrast.

Subj. + 却 + V/Adj.
一个脾气不好的人虽然不一定让人讨厌，但是却很难跟人交朋友。
A bad-tempered person isn't necessarily disliked, yet it's hard for them to make friends.
有人觉得有房子和汽车就是幸福，有人却认为找到真正的爱情才是幸福。
Some think having a house and car is happiness; others feel finding true love is happiness.
现在很多人虽然住在同一个楼里，但是却从来没说过话。
Many people live in the same building now, yet have never spoken.
5而 · Connective "and / while / yet"

"而"是连词，用在复句中，连接两个分句，可以表示并列关系，也可以表示转折关系。
The conjunction "而" connects two clauses in a compound sentence, either coordinately (and/while) or adversatively (yet/but).

Clause 1 , 而 + Clause 2
"明"字，左边的"日"意思是太阳，而右边的"月"意思是月亮。
In the character "明", the 日 on the left means sun, while the 月 on the right means moon.
结婚前，我们都要清楚自己想要的是什么，而不要被别人对幸福的看法影响。
Before marriage, we should know what we want, and not be swayed by others' views on happiness.
有些人觉得朋友就是能和自己一起快乐的人，而我的理解是：当你遇到困难的时候，真正的朋友会站出来，及时给你帮助。
Some think a friend is just someone to be happy with; as for me, a true friend stands up and helps when you meet difficulties.

🏮 Culture · 文化
在家靠父母，出门靠朋友Zài jiā kào fùmǔ, chū mén kào péngyou
中国有句俗话叫"在家靠父母，出门靠朋友"。朋友在我们的生活中非常重要。交朋友是一种文化，也是一种学问。中国人的交友观可以用八个字来形容，那就是"有福同享，有难同当"——你有什么高兴或者好的事情，说出来与朋友分享；你有什么难处，说出来与朋友一起想解决的办法。多为他人想想，站在别人的立场上想问题更容易获得一份友谊。
As the Chinese saying goes, "At home one relies on his parents, while away from home one relies on friends." Friends are very important in our lives. Making friends is a type of culture as well as a branch of learning. The Chinese value regarding friendship can be summed up as "share each other's joys and sorrows" — share the good things with your friends, and work out solutions together with them for your problems. It's easier to earn a friendship if you think more about others and put yourself in their shoes.

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
差不多 — 几乎chàbuduō vs jīhū
相同点：两者都是副词，表示非常接近、相近，都可以做状语。
不同点：
1. "差不多"可直接用在单音节形容词前；"几乎"前后需加别的词语。
2. "差不多"可以用在数量词前面，表示接近这个数量；"几乎"没有这个用法。
3. "差不多"还可以是形容词，做谓语，表示相差不远；"几乎"没有这个用法。

Similarity: Both are adverbs meaning "very close / nearly", both can serve as adverbials.

1. 差不多 can attach directly to a monosyllabic adjective; 几乎 needs additional words before/after.
2. 差不多 can precede numeral-classifier compounds; 几乎 cannot.
3. 差不多 can be an adjective acting as predicate; 几乎 cannot.
4. 几乎 can indicate an undesirable event that almost happened but ultimately didn't; 差不多 cannot.

中山装和旗袍Zhōngshānzhuāng hé qípáo — Chinese Tunic Suit and Cheongsam
中山装和旗袍分别是中国男士和女士的传统正式服装。"中山装"是中国民主革命的开拓者孙中山先生综合了西式服装与中式服装的特点设计出来的。它的直翻领代表孙中山严谨的治国理念，四个口袋代表中国四种传统的道德思想，即"礼、义、廉、耻"。旗袍，被当代国际服装界誉为"东方女装"的代表，旗袍的造型与妇女的体态相适合，线条简单，优美大方。在电影、电视、时装展中经常会看到，现在女人穿旗袍已经成了一种时尚。
The Chinese tunic suit (Zhongshan zhuang) and cheongsam (qipao) are respectively the traditional formal attire of Chinese men and women. The former was designed by Mr. Sun Yat-sen — a leading Chinese democratic revolutionary — combining Western and Chinese clothing styles. Its Prussian collar stands for Sun's rigorous governing principles, and the four pockets represent four traditional Chinese moral virtues: propriety, justice, honesty, a sense of shame. In modern international fashion circles, cheongsams are regarded as representative of "Oriental women's dresses". With simple and elegant lines that fit the female figure well, they are often seen in films, TV, and fashion shows — wearing a qipao has become a modern trend.

另外 — 另lìngwài vs lìng
相同点：
1. 两者都可做代词，表示上文所说范围以外的人或事。
2. 两者都可做副词，表示在上文所说范围以外。
不同点：
1. 做代词时，"另外"常用"另外（+的）+数量（+名词）"或"另外+的（+名词）"两种结构，"的"可加可不加；"另"常用"另+数量（+名词）"结构，不加"的"。
2. 做副词时，"另"常修饰单音节动词；"另外"没有这种限制。
3. "另外"可做连词；"另"没有这个用法。
Similarities:
1. Both can be pronouns referring to people/things beyond what was mentioned.
2. Both can be adverbs meaning "beyond the scope mentioned previously".
Differences:
1. As a pronoun, 另外 uses "另外 (+的) + Num-M (+N)" or "另外 + 的 (+N)" (optional 的); 另 uses "另 + Num-M (+N)" without 的.
2. As an adverb, 另 often modifies a monosyllabic verb; 另外 has no such restriction.
3. 另外 can be a conjunction; 另 cannot.

小李
我也明白。但是那份工作的收入太少了！
Wǒ yě míngbai. Dànshì nà fèn gōngzuò de shōurù tài shǎo le!
I know that too. But the pay at that job was too low!
小林
你才到新公司，不要太急着赚钱，多学习才是最重要的。
Nǐ cái dào xīn gōngsī, búyào tài jízhe zhuàn qián, duō xuéxí cái shì zuì zhòngyào de.
You just joined the new company — don't be in a rush to earn money; learning more is what matters most.
小李
好。我一定会努力把现在这份工作做好。
Hǎo. Wǒ yídìng huì nǔlì bǎ xiànzài zhè fèn gōngzuò zuòhǎo.
OK. I'll definitely work hard to do this job well.

1以为 · "To think (wrongly)"

"以为"是动词，常用来表示说话人认为的事情跟事实不符。
The verb "以为" indicates what the speaker thought goes against the fact — i.e. "I thought X, but actually not".

Subj. + 以为 + Clause
她汉语说得那么好，我还以为她是中国人。
She speaks Chinese so well — I thought she was Chinese.
马克以为今天是星期一，到了学校一看，没人来上课，才发现今天是星期天。
Mark thought today was Monday; when he got to school and saw nobody in class, he realised it's Sunday.
我以为新工作比以前的好，没想到还没有以前好呢。
I thought the new job would be better than the old one — unexpectedly it's worse.
2原来 · "Originally / it turns out"

"原来"可以做名词，表示"开始的时候、从前"；做形容词，表示"以前的、没有改变的"；做副词，表示"以前某一时期"（含现在已经不是这样的意思），或表示"发现了以前不知道的情况"。
"原来" as a noun = "the beginning, the past"; as an adjective = "original, unaltered"; as an adverb = "a certain time in the past (implies change)" or "it turns out that (newly discovered)".

(N.) 原来 / (Adj.) 原来的 + N / (Adv.) 原来 + Clause
按原来的计划应该是两周，但是我们可以提前完成。
The original plan was two weeks, but we can finish ahead of time.
她原来是汉语老师，现在已经成了一名律师。
She used to be a Chinese teacher; now she's become a lawyer.
马克一直等李明回电话，可是电话一晚上都没来，原来李明把马克的电话号码记错了。
Mark kept waiting for Li Ming's call that never came — turns out Li Ming had the wrong number.

对比 · Compare: 原来 vs 本来
1. 两者都可做形容词，表示"以前的、没有改变的"。
2. 两者都可做副词，表示"以前的情况跟现在不一样"。
3. "原来"做副词可以表示"发现了以前不知道的情况"；"本来"做副词可以表示"按道理应该这样"。

我还以为是谁帮我打扫房间呢，原来是你啊！
I wondered who was cleaning my room — it turns out to be you!
这本书本来应该昨天还给你，真不好意思。
I really should have returned this book yesterday — sorry.
3并 · "Actually" (emphatic before negatives)

"并"是副词，用在"不、没（有）"等否定词前边，加强否定的语气。常用于表示转折的句子中，有否定某种看法、说明真实情况的意味。
The adverb "并" is used before negatives 不/没(有) to emphasise the negation, often contradicting a prior assumption — "actually not...".

Subj. + 并 + 不/没(有) + V/Adj.

🏮 Culture · 文化
授人以鱼不如授人以渔Shòu rén yǐ yú bùrú shòu rén yǐ yú
中国有句话叫"授人以鱼不如授人以渔"，意思是说传授给人既有知识或财富等，不如传授给人学习知识或获得财富的方法。这句话道理其实很简单，"鱼"是目的，"渔（钓鱼）"是方法，如果想永远有鱼吃，那就要学会钓鱼的方法。在工作中也是这样，如果别人遇到困难，最好的办法不是替他完成工作，而是交给他解决问题、克服困难的方法。
There is a Chinese saying: "It's better to teach a man fishing than to give him fish". Teaching people how to obtain knowledge or wealth is more useful than simply giving them what you have. The idea is simple: "fish" is the aim, and "fishing" is the method. If you want to always have fish to eat, you must learn how to fish. The same applies at work — when someone meets a problem, the best help is not to finish the task for them but to teach them how to solve problems and overcome difficulties.

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
原来 — 本来yuánlái vs běnlái
相同点：
1. 两者都可做形容词，表示"以前的、没有改变的"。
2. 两者都可做副词，表示"以前的情况跟现在不一样"。
不同点：
"原来"做副词可以表示"发现了以前不知道的情况"；"本来"做副词可以表示"按道理应该这样"。
Similarities:
1. Both can be adjectives meaning "original, unaltered".
2. Both can be adverbs meaning "the previous situation differs from now".
Difference:
As an adverb, 原来 can indicate "a formerly unknown situation has been found out"; as an adverb, 本来 can indicate "it should have been this way by reason".

1肯定 · "Certainly / definite / affirmative"

"肯定"可以做副词，表示"一定、没有问题"；也可做形容词，表示"确定的、明确的"或"承认的、正面的"。
"肯定" as an adverb = "certainly, without doubt". As an adjective = "certain, definite" or "affirmative, positive".

Subj. + 肯定 + V/Adj.
您放心，质量肯定不"打折"。
Don't worry, the quality is certainly not "discounted".
喝太多酒肯定对身体不好。
Drinking too much alcohol is definitely bad for your health.
不能肯定的事、没发生过的事，最好不要说。
Things you can't confirm or that haven't happened — best not to say them.
2再说 · "To put off; besides"

"再说"可以做动词，表示（把事情）留到以后办理或考虑；也可以做连词，表示对前面的话补充进一步的情况。
"再说" as a verb means "to put off / handle later"; as a conjunction, it introduces additional information — "besides; moreover".

(V.) ..., 以后 再说 / (Conj.) ..., 再说 , ...
现在的事，做了再说；以后的事，以后再说。
Do the present work first; later things can wait.
今天买的东西太多了。再说，这个月家里已经花了五千多了。
We've bought too much today. Besides, we've already spent over 5000 this month.
晚上八点的，不用急，再说，我就去几天，不用带多少东西。
It's at 8 pm, no hurry. Besides, I'm only going for a few days — no need to pack much.
3实际（上） · "Reality / in fact"

"实际"可以做名词，表示客观存在的事物或情况；也可做形容词，表示真实的、具体的。"实际上"是副词，表示在真实的情况中，多含转折意。
"实际" as a noun = "reality / actual situation"; as an adjective = "actual, concrete". "实际上" is an adverb meaning "in fact / actually", often with adversative tone.

... , 实际上 + Clause
广告只会介绍优点，却不会说它的缺点。实际上，很多东西并不像广告上说的那么好。
Ads only show merits — not flaws. In fact, many things aren't as good as ads claim.
要想别人对你好，你得先对别人好。实际上，我们很多人做不到这一点。
If you want others to be kind to you, be kind first — yet in fact many of us can't do so.
实际工作能让我更理解书本上的知识。
Actual work helps me understand book knowledge better.
4对……来说 · "From the perspective of..."

"对……来说"表示站在某一角度看问题。
"对……来说" indicates the angle from which a matter is seen.

对 + N/Pron. + 来说 , ...
对我来说，衣服的样子流行不流行并不是很重要。
For me, whether the clothes are fashionable isn't very important.
对一个女人来说，漂亮、聪明都很重要，但其实更重要的是快乐。
For a woman, being pretty and smart matter, but happiness matters more.
一杯水，对一条小鱼来说，可能很有帮助，但对一条大鱼来说，是没有什么帮助的。
A cup of water may help a small fish, but not a big fish — it needs a river.
5尤其 · "Especially"

"尤其"是副词，表示更进一步。常常是前一分句提出一个整体情况，后一分句用"尤其"表示整体情况中最突出的方面。
The adverb "尤其" indicates a further degree. The first clause states a general situation; the second uses "尤其" to introduce the most outstanding aspect.

..., 尤其(是) + N/Clause
现在网上购物变得越来越流行了，年轻人尤其喜欢在网上买东西。
Online shopping is getting more popular — young people especially enjoy it.
您女儿真可爱，长得真像您，尤其是眼睛。
Your daughter is so cute — she really looks like you, especially the eyes.
为了健康，我们应该每年都去医院做一次身体检查，尤其是那些四五十岁的人。
For health, we should have an annual check-up — especially those in their 40s–50s.

对比 · Compare: 尤其 vs 特别
1. 两者做副词时都可表示"在全体中或与其他事物比较时特别突出的方面"。
2. "尤其"后可加"是"（可省）；"特别"后一般加"是"。
3. "特别"做副词还可表示"格外、非常"；"尤其"没有这个用法。
4. "特别"可做形容词（与众不同）；"尤其"没有这个用法。

我对音乐很感兴趣，尤其/特别是贝多芬的音乐。
I love music, especially Beethoven's.
那本小说讲了一个爱情故事，很浪漫，让人特别感动。
That novel tells a romantic love story — it's especially moving.

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
尤其 — 特别yóuqí vs tèbié
相同点：两者都可做副词，表示在全体中或与其他事物比较时特别突出的方面，一般用在句子的后半部分。
不同点：
1. 做副词"特别突出"意思时，"尤其"后可加"是"（可省）；"特别"一般加"是"。
2. "特别"做副词还可表示"格外、非常"；"尤其"没有这个用法。
3. "特别"可做形容词（与众不同、不普通）；"尤其"没有这个用法。
Similarity: Both adverbs introduce a very salient aspect in the whole or in comparison; usually in the latter half of the sentence.
Differences:
1. For the "outstanding aspect" meaning, 尤其 may be followed by 是 (optional); 特别 usually takes 是.
2. 特别 as an adverb can also mean "very, exceptionally"; 尤其 cannot.
3. 特别 can be an adjective (different from the rest, uncommon); 尤其 cannot.

1竟然 · "Unexpectedly / surprisingly"

"竟然"是副词，用在动词或形容词前，表示没有想到、出乎意料。
"竟然" is an adverb placed before a verb or adjective to indicate something unexpected or surprising — "actually / unexpectedly".

Subj. + 竟然 + V/Adj.
回家以后，我才发现，竟然忘了去超市买果汁！
After getting home, I realised — unbelievably — I forgot to buy juice at the supermarket!
这本小说最近很流行，你竟然没听过？
This novel has been really popular lately — you've actually never heard of it?
来这家饭馆吃饭的人竟然那么多，排队都排到门口了。
Surprisingly, so many people came to eat here — the queue reached the door.
2倍 · "Times; -fold"

"倍"是量词，表示倍数。常用在数词后边，构成"数词+倍"的结构。
"倍" is a measure word meaning "times / fold". It follows a number to indicate multiplication.

A 是 B 的 + Num + 倍
今天的价格是昨天的两倍。
Today's price is double yesterday's.

Five times one is five.

3值得 · "To be worth"

"值得"是动词，表示做某事有价值、有好的结果。后面可以跟动词或小句。
The verb "值得" means "worth doing / worthwhile". It is followed by a verb or a clause.

Subj. + 值得 + V / Clause
您看看这个，不管从价格方面看，还是从质量上看，都是很值得考虑的。
Look at this one — whether from price or quality, it's well worth considering.
你应该改改自己的脾气，别总是为一点儿小事生气，不值得。
You should change your temper — it's not worth getting angry over small things.
每个人身上的优点都值得我们去发现、去学习。
Everyone's good qualities are worth discovering and learning from.

对比 · Compare: 值得 vs 值
1. 两者都可表示"有意义或者有价值"。
2. "值得"是动词，后面可以带动词或小句；"值"是动词/形容词，一般不带动词，而是表示"事物与钱财相当"。

这个问题值得我们认真研究。
This question is worth our careful study.
这个手机值五千块。
This phone is worth 5000 yuan.

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
值得 — 值zhíde vs zhí
相同点：都可表示做某事有意义或有价值。
不同点：
1. "值得"是动词，后面可以带动词或小句；"值"一般不带动词。
2. "值"表示"事物与钱财相当"，后面加钱数；"值得"表示"有价值、有意义"，后面加行为或状态。
Similarity: Both can express "to be meaningful / valuable".
Differences:
1. 值得 is a verb that can take a verb or clause after it; 值 normally cannot.
2. 值 means "the thing is equal in value to money" — followed by an amount. 值得 means "worth doing" — followed by an action.
Example: 这个手机值五千块 (The phone is worth 5000 yuan). 这个问题值得认真研究 (This problem is worth careful study).

1估计 · "To estimate; to reckon"

"估计"是动词，表示根据某些情况，对事物做大概的推断。可以直接做谓语，后面可以是动词或小句。
The verb "估计" means "to estimate / reckon" — a rough inference based on some evidence. It can serve as the predicate directly, followed by a verb or clause.

Subj. + 估计 + Clause
我还不习惯北方的气候，估计是天气太干。
I'm not used to the northern climate — probably the weather is too dry.
你有没有去问问小王？估计他应该有办法。
Did you ask Xiao Wang? I reckon he should have a way.
机场通知飞机晚点一个小时，估计他下午六点才能到上海。
The airport said the flight is delayed an hour — I estimate he'll arrive in Shanghai at 6 pm.

对比 · Compare: 估计 vs 可能
1. 两者都可表示根据某些情况对事物做大概的推断。
2. "估计"是动词，可以直接做谓语，后面可以是动词或小句；"可能"是能愿动词，只能放在动词前修饰动词。
3. "可能"可做形容词（"能成为事实的"），"估计"没有这个用法。
4. "可能"前可以加"很"（"很可能"）；"估计"没有这个用法。

今天的工作估计完不成了，因为出了点儿问题。
Today's work probably won't be finished — there's been a problem.
他很可能还不知道这件事。
He very likely still doesn't know about this.
2来不及 · "Too late to / not enough time"

"来不及"是动词，表示因为时间短，无法赶上。可以单独使用，也可以后面带动词。
"来不及" means "too late to do sth / not enough time". It may stand alone or be followed by a verb.

... 来不及 + (V) + 了
等你身体出现问题了，后悔就来不及了。
Once your body has problems, it'll be too late to regret it.
今天来不及开会了，明天我们再找时间说这个问题吧。
No time for a meeting today — let's discuss this tomorrow.
你不是和同学约了下午两点见面吗？再不走就来不及了。
Didn't you agree to meet your classmate at 2 pm? If you don't leave, you'll be late.
3离合词重叠式 · Reduplication of separable verbs (AAB)

离合词（如"散步、帮忙、睡觉、游泳"）的重叠形式一般写成 AAB。重叠表示短时、轻松或尝试的意味。
Separable verbs (e.g. 散步, 帮忙, 睡觉, 游泳) reduplicate as AAB, indicating a brief, relaxed or trial action.

A A B — 散散步 / 帮帮忙 / 睡睡觉 / 游游泳
咱们午饭后就去附近的公园散散步吧。

行李怎么这么重啊！你快来帮帮我的忙。

周末马克一般在宿舍看看书、听听音乐、睡睡觉。
On weekends Mark usually reads a bit, listens to some music and takes naps in his dorm.
4要是……就…… · "If... then..."

"要是"是连词，表示"如果、如果是"的意思，常用"要是……（的话），就……"结构。
The conjunction "要是" means "if / supposing" — often used in the structure "要是……(的话), 就……".

要是 + Clause 1 , 就 + Clause 2
要是健康是1，其他都是1后面的0。
If health is the 1, everything else is the 0s behind it.
要是你想来中国的大学留学，那么就需要做好准备。
If you want to study at a Chinese university, then you need to prepare well.
要是质量比别人的好的话，那么价格贵一点儿也是可以接受的。
If the quality is better, a slightly higher price is acceptable.
5既……又/也/还…… · "Both... and..."

"既……又/也/还……"连接动词性结构或形容词，表示并列关系，同时具有两方面的情况。
"既……又/也/还……" connects verb phrases or adjectives, expressing a coordinate relationship — "both...and...".

既 + VP1 , 又/也/还 + VP2

Walking both exercises the body and helps you lose weight.
成功离不开交流。交流会让你认识更多朋友，也容易获得成功。

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
估计 — 可能gūjì vs kěnéng
相同点：两者都可表示根据某些情况对事物做大概的推断。
不同点：
1. "估计"是动词，可以直接做谓语，后面可以是动词或小句；"可能"是能愿动词，只能放在动词前修饰动词。
2. "可能"可做形容词，表示"能成为事实的"；"估计"没有这个用法。
3. "可能"前可以加"很"（"很可能"），表示加强肯定估计；"估计"没有这个用法。
Similarity: Both express an inference about something based on certain information.
Differences:
1. 估计 is a verb that can serve as the predicate directly, followed by a verb or clause; 可能 is a modal verb, placed before a verb only to modify it.
2. 可能 can also be an adjective meaning "possible"; 估计 cannot.
3. 可能 can be preceded by 很 (很可能 — very likely) to strengthen the estimate; 估计 cannot.

1使 · "To make / to cause"

"使"是动词，表示"让、叫、致使"。常用于书面语或比较正式的语体中，表示一种人或事物引起另一种情况的变化。
The verb "使" means "to make / cause". Often used in written or formal styles, it indicates that one thing brings about a change in another.

Subj. + 使 + O + V/Adj.
吃巧克力可以使人的心情变好。
Eating chocolate can make one's mood better.
散步使人身心愉快。
Walking makes body and mind joyful.
这件事使我们都很感动。

2只要……就…… · "As long as... then..."

"只要"提出一个必要条件，"就"引出在这个条件下必然出现的结果。
"只要" introduces a sufficient condition, and "就" introduces the result that follows under that condition.

只要 + Clause 1 , 就 + Clause 2
只要用心观察，就会发现身边有那么多美。
As long as you observe with your heart, you'll find so much beauty around you.

3可不是 · "Exactly! / That's right!"

"可不是"用来表示赞同对方所说的话，相当于"就是""对啊""正是这样"。
"可不是" is used to agree with what the other person has said — equivalent to "exactly", "right", "that's just it".

A: ... ! / B: 可不是 ! , ...
A: 这电影真是让人难忘！ B: 可不是！

4因此 · "Therefore; hence"

"因此"是连词，表示"因为这个""所以"，多用于书面语。后面引出由前面内容推出的结果或结论。
The conjunction "因此" means "therefore, hence" (more written than 所以). It introduces a result or conclusion derived from what preceded.

Clause 1 , 因此 + Clause 2
我们走得太快，没有用心去观察；因此，让我们放慢脚步，多看看周围的世界吧。
We walk too fast without observing — therefore, let's slow down and look more at the world around us.
他对这份工作非常认真，因此得到了大家的好评。
He is very serious about this job; therefore everyone praises him.
天气很冷，因此要多穿一点儿。
It's cold, so wear more clothes.
5往往 · "Often; as a rule"

"往往"是副词，表示某种情况在一定条件下经常出现，常带有归纳总结的意味。
The adverb "往往" indicates that a situation usually occurs under certain conditions — it summarises a general pattern.

Subj. + 往往 + V/Adj.
遇到困难时，积极的人往往能找到办法。
When facing difficulties, positive people often find a way.
周末商场里往往人很多。
On weekends the mall is usually crowded.
他往往晚上工作到很晚。
He often works late into the night.

对比 · Compare: 往往 vs 经常
1. 两者都可表示动作或情况多次重复。
2. "往往"总结在一定条件下经常发生的情况，带有归纳意味；"经常"更强调动作的频率。
3. "往往"一般用于过去或一般情况，不用于将来；"经常"可用于过去、现在、将来。

周末我往往去公园散步。 ✓

我经常给妈妈打电话。 ✓
I often call my mom.
我以后会经常去看你。 ✓（× 往往）
I'll come visit you often in future. (经常 only)

🏮 Culture · 文化
中国人眼中的"红"与"白"Zhōngguó rén yǎn zhōng de "hóng" yǔ "bái" — Red and White in Chinese People's Eyes
在中国文化中，"红色"常常代表幸福、吉祥、热闹。过年、结婚、生孩子等喜庆的场合，人们都喜欢用红色：红包、红灯笼、红春联、红嫁衣。相反，"白色"往往和悲伤、离别、丧事联系在一起——丧礼上的衣服、花圈都是白色的。这和西方文化中"白色象征纯洁、红色常常代表危险"的理解正好相反。所以，了解一个国家的文化，也要了解它对颜色的看法。
In Chinese culture, red often represents happiness, good fortune, and celebration. For New Year, weddings, and childbirth, people love using red — red envelopes (红包), red lanterns, red couplets, and red bridal robes. By contrast, white is often associated with grief, separation, and funerals — mourning clothes and wreaths are all white. This is the opposite of the Western view, where white often symbolises purity and red can mean danger. To understand a culture, it helps to understand its view of colours.

1难道 · "Could it possibly...?" (rhetorical question)

"难道"是副词，用在反问句中，加强反问的语气，表达惊讶、怀疑或质疑。句末常带"吗/不成"。
The adverb "难道" is used in rhetorical questions to strengthen the tone, expressing surprise, doubt or challenge. Often ends with "吗" or "不成".

难道 + Clause + 吗 ?
难道中间没想过放弃吗？
Could it be you never thought of giving up along the way?
难道你还不知道这件事吗？
Do you really not know about this?
他这么努力，难道你不感动吗？
He tries so hard — aren't you moved?
2通过 · "Through / by means of"

"通过"可以做介词，表示"经过、以……为方法"；也可以做动词，表示"穿过、经过、达到标准"。
"通过" as a preposition means "through / by means of"; as a verb it means "to pass through" or "to pass (a test/standard)".

(Prep.) 通过 + N/VP , Clause / (V.) Subj. + 通过 + N
通过努力，我才能有今天的成绩。
Only through effort have I achieved what I have today.
他通过了考试。
He passed the exam.
这条街只能步行通过。
This street can only be walked through.

对比 · Compare: 通过 vs 经过
1. 两者都可表示通过某种途径/过程达到某结果。
2. "通过"更强调"方法、手段"；"经过"可强调"经历了某个过程"或做动词表示"经过某地"。
3. "经过"可做名词（"过程"的意思）；"通过"一般不这么用。

通过朋友介绍，我认识了他。 ✓（方法）
Through a friend's introduction, I met him.
经过两年的努力，终于成功了。 ✓（过程）
After two years of effort, I finally succeeded.
事情的经过很复杂。 ✓（名词）
The course of events is complicated.
3可是 · "But / however"

"可是"是连词，常用于复句的后一分句，表示转折。和"但是"相比，语气稍轻。
The conjunction "可是" marks a transition in a compound sentence — similar to "但是", slightly softer in tone.

Clause 1 , 可是 + Clause 2
大家都觉得她的选择不正确，可是她一直相信自己，没有放弃。
Everyone thought her choice wasn't right — but she always believed in herself and didn't give up.
天气很冷，可是他还是坚持早上跑步。
It's very cold, but he still insists on running in the mornings.
这个问题看起来简单，可是并不容易回答。
The question looks simple, but isn't easy to answer.
4结果 · "Result / as a result"

"结果"可以做名词，表示事情的最后情况；也可以做连词，用在后一分句，表示前面动作或情况造成的结果。
"结果" can be a noun = "result / outcome"; or a conjunction used in the second clause to introduce the consequence.

(N.) ...的结果 / (Conj.) Clause 1 , 结果 + Clause 2
很多人还没开始就担心结果。
Many people worry about the result before starting.
担心自己做不好，担心失败——结果什么都没做。
Worried they won't do well, worried about failure — as a result, they do nothing.
他开玩笑跟老板说要辞职，结果老板真的同意了。
He jokingly told his boss he'd quit — and the boss actually agreed!
5上 · Result complement "to reach / to attain"

"上"作结果补语，用在动词后，可以表示达到某种数量、水平，或者表示"考上、选上"等达到标准。
As a result complement, "上" follows a verb to express reaching a certain quantity/level, or achieving a standard (e.g. passing an entrance exam).

V + 上 + (Num/Goal)
这个城市的地铁已经有十五条了。
This city already has fifteen metro lines.
他终于考上了理想的大学。
He finally got into his dream university.
爱迪生经过多年努力，终于成为了世界上著名的科学家。
After years of effort, Edison became one of the world's most famous scientists.

🏮 Culture · 文化
成功的秘密 — 千里之行，始于足下Chénggōng de mìmì — Qiān lǐ zhī xíng, shǐ yú zú xià
中国有一句古话："千里之行，始于足下"——意思是再远的路程，也是从脚下的第一步开始的。这句话出自《老子》，它告诉我们：所有伟大的成功，都是从小事、从当下、从第一步开始的。
很多人能开始一件事，但真正成功的人往往是那些能够坚持走到最后的人。路上会有困难，也会有风雨，但只要不放弃，阳光总在风雨后。
There is an ancient Chinese saying: "A journey of a thousand li begins beneath one's feet" (千里之行，始于足下) — from the Tao Te Ching. It tells us that every great success begins with small actions, in the present, with the very first step.
Many people can start something; but those who truly succeed are usually those who persevere to the end. There will be difficulties and storms, but if you don't give up — the sun always comes after the storm.

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
通过 — 经过tōngguò vs jīngguò
相同点：两者都可以表示通过某种途径或过程达到某种结果。
不同点：
1. "通过"更强调"方法、手段"，"经过"更强调"过程、时间"。
2. "经过"可以做动词，表示"经过某地"；"通过"也可做动词，但多表示"通过考试、通过标准"。
3. "经过"可以做名词（"过程"的意思）；"通过"一般不这么用。
Similarity: Both can express attaining a result via some means or process.
Differences:
1. 通过 stresses the method/means; 经过 stresses the process/time.
2. 经过 can be a verb "to pass through (a place)"; 通过 as a verb usually means "to pass (an exam / standard)".
3. 经过 can be a noun meaning "the course (of events)"; 通过 normally cannot.

In front of my mother, I can always be a daughter who never grows up; in front of my son, I can be a mother he can rely on. It gives my efforts a direction and a meaning.

1不过 · "But; however" (soft contrast)

"不过"是连词，常用于复句的后一分句，表示轻微的转折，语气比"但是""可是"略轻、略自然。
The conjunction "不过" marks a mild contrast — softer and more conversational than "但是" / "可是".

Clause 1 , 不过 + Clause 2
谢谢你，不过我最近真的很忙。
Thank you — but I really have been very busy lately.
我也这么想。不过现在什么都这么贵。
I think so too — but everything's so expensive now.
这件衣服很漂亮，不过有点儿贵。
This dress is beautiful — but a bit expensive.
2确实 · "Indeed; really"

"确实"是副词，用在动词或形容词前，表示肯定、证实之前提到的事，语气较强。
The adverb "确实" is used before a verb or adjective to affirm something — "indeed / really / truly".

Subj. + 确实 + V/Adj.
您在工作上确实非常优秀。
You really are outstanding at your work.
这家饭馆的菜确实很好吃。
The food at this restaurant really is delicious.
他最近确实很忙。
He really has been very busy lately.
3关键（是） · "The key (is)"

"关键"可以做名词，表示最重要的部分；也可以做形容词修饰名词。常见结构"关键是……"用于引出最重要的一点。
"关键" as a noun = "key / crucial point"; as an adjective = "crucial". The pattern "关键是……" introduces the most important point.

... , 关键是 + Clause
赚多少钱不是关键。关键是你对这份工作有没有兴趣。

4永远 · "Forever; always"

"永远"是副词，表示时间上没有终止，永久，常用在动词或形容词前。
The adverb "永远" means "forever / eternally", used before a verb or adjective.

Subj. + 永远 + V/Adj.

5将来 · "The future" (and comparison with 以后)

"将来"是名词，指"未来的时间"，常指比较长的一段未来，多用于书面语或正式场合。常和"以后"区别使用。
"将来" (a noun) refers to "the future" (usually a longer stretch of time), more formal than "以后". Compare with "以后" which can also mean "after / later".

Subj. + 将来 + V/会 + V

对比 · Compare: 将来 vs 以后
1. "将来"只指未来某个时间，不用于过去。
2. "以后"可指"某件事之后"（可过去、现在、将来）；"将来"不能这样用。
3. "将来"比"以后"更书面、更正式。

毕业以后我想住在上海。 ✓

知足常乐Zhī zú cháng lè — Contentment brings happiness
中国有一句老话——"知足常乐"，意思是一个人如果能对自己现在的生活感到满足，他就常常能体会到快乐。这句话来自《老子》里"知足者富"的思想。幸福的标准每个人都不同：有人觉得有钱是幸福，有人觉得家人平安健康是幸福，也有人觉得能做自己喜欢的事就是幸福。中国文化鼓励人们多看自己拥有的东西，而不是只羡慕别人有的东西。当你羡慕别人的时候，别人也许正在羡慕你。
There is an old Chinese saying: "He who is content is always happy" (知足常乐). From Laozi's idea "he who is content is rich" (知足者富), it means that a person who feels satisfied with their current life is often able to experience happiness. Everyone's standard of happiness is different — some see wealth as happiness, some value family's peace and health, and some simply cherish doing what they love. Chinese culture encourages people to appreciate what they already have rather than only envying what others have. When you envy someone, that someone may be envying you.

⚖️ Compare · 比一比
将来 — 以后jiānglái vs yǐhòu
相同点：都可以指"未来的时间"。
不同点：
1. "将来"只指未来，不能用于过去；"以后"可指"某件事之后"（过去、现在、将来都可以）。
2. "将来"更书面、更正式；"以后"更口语化。
3. 只"以后"可以跟在动词或事件后，表示"……之后"；"将来"不行。
Similarity: Both can refer to "future time".
Differences:
1. 将来 only refers to the future; 以后 can also mean "after (an event)" — past, present or future.
2. 将来 is more written/formal; 以后 is more colloquial.
3. Only 以后 can follow a verb or event (e.g. 毕业以后 "after graduating"); 将来 cannot.
