课文 1 · 马克介绍自己学习汉语的方法
Mark introduces his method of learning Chinese
大卫
你来中国才一年，汉语就说得这么流利，真厉害！
Nǐ lái Zhōngguó cái yī nián, Hànyǔ jiù shuō de zhème liúlì, zhēn lìhai!
You've only been in China for a year and your Chinese is already so fluent — that's impressive!
马克
谢谢！其实我的语法不太好，很多句子说得都不太准确。
Xièxie! Qíshí wǒ de yǔfǎ bú tài hǎo, hěn duō jùzi shuō de dōu bú tài zhǔnquè.
Thanks! Actually my grammar isn't great — I say many sentences inaccurately.
大卫
但是我看你跟中国人交流没什么问题，你是怎么做到的？
Dànshì wǒ kàn nǐ gēn Zhōngguó rén jiāoliú méi shénme wèntí, nǐ shì zěnme zuò dào de?
But I see that you communicate with Chinese people without any problem — how do you do it?
马克
平时多交一些中国朋友，经常和他们聊天儿，听说能力自然就能得到很大的提高。另外，我建议你坚持看中文报纸，这样能学到很多新词语。
Píngshí duō jiāo yìxiē Zhōngguó péngyou, jīngcháng hé tāmen liáo tiānr, tīng shuō nénglì zìrán jiù néng dédào hěn dà de tígāo. Língwài, wǒ jiànyì nǐ jiānchí kàn Zhōngwén bàozhǐ, zhèyàng néng xuédào hěn duō xīn cíyǔ.
Make more Chinese friends and chat with them often — your listening and speaking will naturally improve a lot. Also, I suggest you keep reading Chinese newspapers; that way you can learn many new words.
大卫
你太厉害了！连中文报纸都看得懂。
Nǐ tài lìhai le! Lián Zhōngwén bàozhǐ dōu kàn de dǒng.
You're amazing! You can even understand Chinese newspapers.
马克
刚开始肯定有困难，不过遇到不认识的词语，你可以查词典，然后写在本子上，有空儿就拿出来复习一下，慢慢地就会发现中文报纸也没那么难了。
Gāng kāishǐ kěndìng yǒu kùnnan, búguò yùdào bú rènshi de cíyǔ, nǐ kěyǐ chá cídiǎn, ránhòu xiě zài běnzi shang, yǒu kòngr jiù ná chulai fùxí yíxià, mànmàn de jiù huì fāxiàn Zhōngwén bàozhǐ yě méi nàme nán le.
At first it's definitely difficult, but when you come across words you don't know, look them up in a dictionary, write them in a notebook, and review them whenever you have time — you'll gradually find that Chinese newspapers aren't so hard after all.
课文 2 · 小夏和小雨聊考试的情况
Xiao Xia and Xiao Yu talk about the exam
小夏
考试结束了，你对自己的成绩满意吗？
Kǎoshì jiéshù le, nǐ duì zìjǐ de chéngjì mǎnyì ma?
The exam is over — are you satisfied with your results?
小雨
说真的，我不太满意。这次阅读考试的题太多了，我没做完。
Shuō zhēn de, wǒ bú tài mǎnyì. Zhè cì yuèdú kǎoshì de tí tài duō le, wǒ méi zuò wán.
Honestly, I'm not very satisfied. There were too many reading questions this time — I didn't finish.
小夏
两个小时的时间应该来得及吧？
Liǎng ge xiǎoshí de shíjiān yīnggāi láidejí ba?
Two hours should be enough time, right?
小雨
这次主要是因为我先做了比较难、比较复杂的题，结果花了太多时间，后面简单的题我虽然会，可是时间来不及，最后只好放弃了。
Zhè cì zhǔyào shì yīnwèi wǒ xiān zuòle bǐjiào nán, bǐjiào fùzá de tí, jiéguǒ huāle tài duō shíjiān, hòumiàn jiǎndān de tí wǒ suīrán huì, kěshì shíjiān láibují, zuìhòu zhǐhǎo fàngqì le.
This time it was mainly because I did the harder, more complex questions first — I ended up spending too much time on those. I knew how to do the simple ones at the end, but I ran out of time and had to give up.
小夏
其实我考得也不怎么样。有几个填空题不会做，有几个选择题，实在想不出来该选哪个，就随便猜了一个答案，结果一个都没猜对。
Qíshí wǒ kǎo de yě bù zěnmeyàng. Yǒu jǐ ge tiánkòng tí bú huì zuò, yǒu jǐ ge xuǎnzé tí, shízài xiǎng bu chūlái gāi xuǎn nǎ ge, jiù suíbiàn cāile yí ge dá'àn, jiéguǒ yí ge dōu méi cāiduì.
Actually I didn't do so well either. There were a few fill-in-the-blank questions I couldn't do, and some multiple choice ones where I really couldn't figure out which to pick, so I just guessed randomly — and got none of them right.
小雨
看来要想考好，不但要认真复习，还得注意考试的方法，否则，会做的题也没时间做了。
Kànlái yào xiǎng kǎo hǎo, búdàn yào rènzhēn fùxí, hái děi zhùyì kǎoshì de fāngfǎ, fǒuzé, huì zuò de tí yě méi shíjiān zuò le.
It seems that to do well on exams, you not only need to study seriously, but also pay attention to exam strategy — otherwise even the questions you know how to do will run out of time.
课文 3 · 小李告诉小林阅读的好处
Xiao Li tells Xiao Lin the benefits of reading
小林
你的客厅里怎么到处是书啊？这些书你都喜欢看吗？
Nǐ de kètīng lǐ zěnme dàochù shì shū a? Zhèxiē shū nǐ dōu xǐhuan kàn ma?
Why are there books everywhere in your living room? Do you enjoy reading all of them?
小李
当然，我每天都要看书。无论是普通杂志，还是著名小说，只要打开它们，就会发现，世界上有那么多有意思的事情，有那么多不一样的生活。
Dāngrán, wǒ měi tiān dōu yào kàn shū. Wúlùn shì pǔtōng zázhì, háishi zhùmíng xiǎoshuō, zhǐyào dǎkāi tāmen, jiù huì fāxiàn, shìjiè shàng yǒu nàme duō yǒuyìsi de shìqing, yǒu nàme duō bù yīyàng de shēnghuó.
Of course — I read every day. Whether it's an ordinary magazine or a famous novel, once you open them you discover so many interesting things in the world, so many different ways of life.
小林
想不到你工作那么忙，还能每天坚持阅读。
Xiǎng bu dào nǐ gōngzuò nàme máng, hái néng měi tiān jiānchí yuèdú.
I didn't expect that even with such a busy job, you still manage to read every day.
小李
如果3分钟读一页书，半个小时就可以读10页。每天花半个小时来读书，一个月就可以读300页，差不多就是一本书了。
Rúguǒ sān fēnzhōng dú yī yè shū, bàn ge xiǎoshí jiù kěyǐ dú shí yè. Měi tiān huā bàn ge xiǎoshí lái dú shū, yī ge yuè jiù kěyǐ dú sānbǎi yè, chàbuduō jiù shì yī běn shū le.
If you read one page every three minutes, you can read ten pages in half an hour. Spending half an hour reading every day, you can read 300 pages in a month — that's roughly a whole book.
小林
是啊，一个真正爱看书的人总能找出时间来阅读。
Shì a, yī ge zhēnzhèng ài kàn shū de rén zǒng néng zhǎo chū shíjiān lái yuèdú.
That's true — someone who genuinely loves reading will always find the time.
小李
坚持阅读，除了能增加知识外，还能帮助我减轻压力，人也会变得轻松起来。
Jiānchí yuèdú, chúle néng zēngjiā zhīshi wài, hái néng bāngzhù wǒ jiǎnqīng yālì, rén yě huì biàn de qīngsōng qǐlai.
Keeping up with reading not only increases knowledge, it also helps me relieve stress and feel more relaxed overall.
课文 4 · 阅读文章
Reading passage — on taking reading notes
阅读
根据调查，阅读能力好的人，不但容易找到工作，而且工资也比较高。怎么才能有效提高自己的阅读能力呢？做读书笔记就是其中一种好方法。读书笔记有很多种，最简单的就是把自己喜欢或者觉得有用的词语和句子记下来。另外，在看完一篇文章或一本书之后，还可以把它的主要内容和自己的想法写下来。然而，你不能完全相信书本上的内容，要有自己的看法和判断。坚持做读书笔记，对提高阅读能力有很大帮助。
Gēnjù diàochá, yuèdú nénglì hǎo de rén, búdàn róngyì zhǎodào gōngzuò, érqiě gōngzī yě bǐjiào gāo. Zěnme cái néng yǒuxiào tígāo zìjǐ de yuèdú nénglì ne? Zuò dúshū bǐjì jiù shì qízhōng yī zhǒng hǎo fāngfǎ. Dúshū bǐjì yǒu hěn duō zhǒng, zuì jiǎndān de jiù shì bǎ zìjǐ xǐhuan huòzhě juéde yǒuyòng de cíyǔ hé jùzi jì xialai. Língwài, zài kànwán yī piān wénzhāng huò yī běn shū zhīhòu, hái kěyǐ bǎ tā de zhǔyào nèiróng hé zìjǐ de xiǎngfǎ xiě xialai. Rán'ér, nǐ bù néng wánquán xiāngxìn shūběn shàng de nèiróng, yào yǒu zìjǐ de kànfǎ hé pànduàn. Jiānchí zuò dúshū bǐjì, duì tígāo yuèdú nénglì yǒu hěn dà bāngzhù.
According to research, people with strong reading ability are not only more likely to find jobs, but also tend to earn higher salaries. How can you effectively improve your reading ability? Taking reading notes is one good method. There are many kinds of reading notes — the simplest is to write down words and sentences you like or find useful. In addition, after reading an article or a book, you can also write down the main content and your own thoughts. However, you shouldn't completely trust everything written in books — you need to form your own views and judgments. Consistently taking reading notes is a great help in improving your reading ability.
课文 5 · 读书好，读好书，好读书
Reading passage — it's good to read, read good books, and love reading
阅读
"读书好，读好书，好读书"。虽然这句话只用了三个相同的汉字，但是不同的顺序却表示了不同的意思。首先，"读书好"说的是读书有很多好处；其次，每个人的时间都是有限的，不可能把世界上每一本书都读完，所以要读好的书；最后，"好读书"就是要养成阅读的习惯，使读书真正成为自己的兴趣爱好。阅读有许多好处，它能丰富你的知识，让你找到解决问题的办法；同时，它还会丰富你的情感，使你的生活更精彩。所以，让阅读成为你的习惯吧！
Dú shū hǎo, dú hǎo shū, hào dú shū. Suīrán zhè jù huà zhǐ yòngle sān ge xiāngtóng de Hànzì, dànshì bù tóng de shùnxù què biǎoshìle bù tóng de yìsi. Shǒuxiān, dú shū hǎo shuō de shì dú shū yǒu hěn duō hǎochù; qícì, měi ge rén de shíjiān dōu shì yǒuxiàn de, bù kěnéng bǎ shìjiè shàng měi yī běn shū dōu dúwán, suǒyǐ yào dú hǎo de shū; zuìhòu, hào dú shū jiù shì yào yǎngchéng yuèdú de xíguàn, shǐ dú shū zhēnzhèng chéngwéi zìjǐ de xìngqù àihào. Yuèdú yǒu xǔduō hǎochù, tā néng fēngfù nǐ de zhīshi, ràng nǐ zhǎodào jiějué wèntí de bànfǎ; tóngshí, tā hái huì fēngfù nǐ de qínggǎn, shǐ nǐ de shēnghuó gèng jīngcǎi. Suǒyǐ, ràng yuèdú chéngwéi nǐ de xíguàn ba!
Reading is good, read good books, and love reading. Although this phrase uses only three identical Chinese characters, different orders convey different meanings. First, "reading is good" means that reading has many benefits. Second, everyone's time is limited — you can't possibly read every book in the world, so read good ones. Finally, "love reading" means developing the habit of reading and making it a genuine personal interest. Reading has many benefits: it enriches your knowledge and helps you find solutions to problems; at the same time, it also enriches your emotions and makes your life more wonderful. So, let reading become your habit!

1连……也/都…… · Even…

"连"是介词，表示强调，常用"连……也/都……"结构。说话人通过强调一项极端的例子来说明另一种情况。"连"的后边可以是主语，也可以是前置的宾语。
The preposition "连" is used for emphasis in the structure "连……也/都……". The speaker emphasizes an extreme case to illustrate a broader situation. The noun after "连" can be the subject or a prepositive object.

连 + N + 也/都 + VP
连中文报纸都看得懂。
Can even understand Chinese newspapers.
如果连你自己都不喜欢自己，又怎么能让别人喜欢你呢？
If even you don't like yourself, how can you expect others to like you?
连我住的楼的电梯里都有三个广告。
There are even three ads in the elevator of my building.
2否则 · Otherwise

"否则"是连词，是"如果不是这样"的意思。"否则"后面的句子表示从前面句子推论出的结果，或者提供另一种选择。
The conjunction "否则" means "if not…, then…". The sentence following it indicates a result inferred from the previous sentence or offers another choice.

…… 否则 + 结果
要想考好，还得注意考试的方法，否则，会做的题也没时间做了。
To do well on exams, you must also pay attention to strategy — otherwise, you'll run out of time even for questions you know.
他一定有重要的事找你，否则不会打这么多次电话来。
He must have something important — otherwise he wouldn't have called so many times.
你最好下午四点前去公司找她，否则就明天早上再去。
You'd better go to her office before 4 pm, otherwise go again tomorrow morning.
3无论……都/也…… · No matter…

"无论"是连词，表示在任何条件下结果或结论都不会改变，常用"无论……都/也……"结构。"无论"后面可以是表示选择关系的并列成分，也可以是表示任指的疑问代词。
The conjunction "无论" means the result won't change under any circumstances. It can be followed by coordinate alternatives or an interrogative pronoun referring to anybody or anything.

无论 + (condition) + 都/也 + (result)
无论是普通杂志，还是著名小说，只要打开它们，就会发现世界上有那么多有意思的事情。
Whether ordinary magazines or famous novels, once you open them you discover so many interesting things in the world.
无论做什么事都要注意方法，正确的方法可以让我们做得更好。
No matter what you do, pay attention to method — the right method helps us do better.
无论结果怎么样，我们都应该为他高兴。
Whatever the result, we should all be happy for him.
4然而 · However

"然而"是连词，用在后半句的开头，表示转折，多用于书面语。"然而"后可加逗号，表示停顿。
The conjunction "然而" is used at the beginning of the second clause to indicate a turn in meaning. It is mainly used in written Chinese and can be followed by a comma.

…… 然而，……（转折）
然而，你不能完全相信书本上的内容，要有自己的看法和判断。
However, you can't completely trust what's written in books — you need to form your own views and judgments.
他虽然失败了很多次，然而一直没有放弃。
Although he failed many times, he never gave up.
很多人觉得自己的生活是幸福的，然而每个人对幸福的看法却不完全相同。
Many people feel their lives are happy, however everyone's view of happiness is not exactly the same.
5同时 · At the same time

"同时"作连词，有更进一步的意思，常和"又、也、还"连用。"同时"作名词，表示动作行为在同一个时间发生，常用结构为"在……（的）同时"。
As a conjunction, "同时" introduces a further remark, often used with "又/也/还". As a noun, it indicates two actions at the same time, used in "在……（的）同时".

…… 同时，…… 还/也 ……
阅读有许多好处，它能丰富你的知识，让你找到解决问题的办法；同时，它还会丰富你的情感，使你的生活更精彩。
Reading has many benefits: it enriches your knowledge and helps you find solutions; at the same time, it also enriches your emotions and makes your life more wonderful.
因为小孩子的想法没有那么复杂，所以他们总是很快乐。同时，小孩子也是最诚实的。
Because children's thinking isn't so complex, they're always happy. At the same time, children are also the most honest.
在学习汉语的同时，我还了解了中国文化，认识了很多中国朋友。
While learning Chinese, I also learned about Chinese culture and made many Chinese friends.

📚 Culture · 中国古典文学
中国古典文学名著——《西游记》A Classic Work — Journey to the West
《西游记》是中国古代最著名的小说之一，它成书于明朝（1368–1644）中期。这部小说在中国和世界各地广为流传，被翻译成多种文字。小说以民间传说为基础，通过神话的形式，讲述唐僧带领孙悟空、猪八戒和沙僧三个徒弟去西天取经的故事。在取经的路上，师徒四人遇到了很多困难，战胜了无数妖魔，最终取得真经。以《西游记》故事创作的经典动画片《大闹天宫》、电视连续剧及电影等为人们喜闻乐见。
Journey to the West, completed in the middle of the Ming Dynasty (1368–1644), is among the most famous novels of ancient China. It has been translated into many languages and is widely read around the world. Based on folk legends, the novel tells the myth of Monk Tang's journey with his three disciples — Monkey King, Pigsy, and Sandy — to fetch the Buddhist scriptures. On the way, the four encounter many difficulties and defeat countless demons, ultimately achieving their goal. The classic cartoon Uproar in Heaven, TV series, and films based on the novel are all much loved by the public.
🔤 Language · 汉语特点
汉语的顺序之美The Beauty of Word Order in Chinese
"读书好，读好书，好读书"这句话只用了三个汉字——读、书、好——但三种顺序表达了三种完全不同的意思。这正是汉语语序灵活、表意丰富的一个体现。词序的变化能够改变词性和句子结构，这在汉语学习中非常重要。
"读书好，读好书，好读书" uses just three characters — 读 (read), 书 (book), 好 (good/love) — yet three different orders express three completely different meanings. This is a perfect illustration of Chinese's flexible word order and expressive richness. Changes in word order can alter the part of speech and sentence structure — a key insight for Chinese learners.
⚖️ Compare · 比一比
无论 vs 不管Similarities and Differences
相同点：都可做连词，表示在任何条件下结果都不会改变，常跟"都/也"一起用。两者后面都可加疑问代词或选择关系的并列成分。

不同点：
① "不管"多用于口语，后面不能用"如何、是否"等文言色彩的词；"无论"多用于书面语，可接"如何、是否"。
② "不管"后可用正反形式；"无论"后用正反形式时，一般在正反形式中间加"还是、跟、与"。

例：不管热不热，他总是穿这么多。
     无论/不管热还是不热，他总是穿这么多。
Similarity: Both are conjunctions meaning the result won't change under any circumstances. Both are used with "都/也" and can be followed by interrogative pronouns or coordinate alternatives.

Differences:
① "不管" is more colloquial and cannot take formal words like "如何/是否"; "无论" is more literary and can.
② "不管" can directly follow an affirmative-negative form; when "无论" uses this form, "还是/跟/与" is added between the two parts.

Teacher Gao explains how to study classical Chinese poetry

高老师，您怎么对古诗这么感兴趣？连我们这些中国学生都觉得古诗很难。

When reading old poems, you can't just look at the characters. Carefully imagine the poet's feelings while writing — and you'll roughly understand what they wanted to say.

1并且 — and / moreover
"并且" connects two verbs, adjectives or clauses describing the same subject. The second part adds further information that holds at the same time as the first.
…，并且 + V/Adj …
希望及时发现问题，并且准确地找到解决方法。
Hope to spot the problem in time and find an accurate solution.
他工作认真，并且很有耐心。
He works seriously, and is very patient.
这本书很有意思，并且对学习很有帮助。
This book is interesting, and very helpful for studying.
2再…也… — no matter how … still …
A hypothetical concession: even if the situation is taken to an extreme, the result still holds. "再" + adjective/verb introduces the extreme; "也" gives the result.
再 + Adj / V，也 + …
再普通的语言也能变得温暖、友好。
Even the most ordinary words can become warm and friendly.
再忙也要锻炼身体。
No matter how busy, you must exercise.
再难的题，他也能做出来。
Even the hardest problems, he can solve.
3对于 — with regard to / about
"对于" introduces the topic or object the speaker is concerned about. It usually appears at the beginning of the sentence (before the subject) or right after the subject.
对于 + N，主语 + V…
对于这个问题，我们再商量一下。
As for this question, let's discuss it again.
对于学汉语，他特别有信心。
When it comes to learning Chinese, he's especially confident.
我对于古诗一直很感兴趣。
I've always been interested in classical poetry.
4AA重叠 (名量词) — every X without exception
Some monosyllabic nouns and measure words can be reduplicated to express "every / each one". The reduplicated form usually serves as the subject or attributive of the subject.
N/M + N/M (重叠) + 都 + …
个个学生都很认真。
Every student is very serious.
天天去公园散步。
Walks in the park every day.
件件事都办得很好。
Every matter is handled very well.
5相反 — on the contrary
As an adjective, "相反" means "opposite". As a connective, it introduces a clause that runs against the expectation set up by the previous sentence — placed at the start of the new clause.
…。相反，… / S₁ 与 S₂ 相反。
他没有难过；相反，他笑了。
He wasn't sad — on the contrary, he smiled.
两个人的看法完全相反。
The two people's views are completely opposite.
这次考试他没失败，相反，考得特别好。
He didn't fail the exam — on the contrary, he did especially well.

文化 · Culture
孔子 "因材施教" — Confucius' Individualized Teaching

孔子（公元前 551 — 前 479）是中国古代著名的教育家。他对学生的思想和方法影响深远。"因材施教" 是他最重要的教育思想之一：根据每个学生不同的性格、兴趣和能力，选择不同的教育方法去引导他们，让每个人的优点都能发挥出来，弱点也能得到补足。

Confucius (551 BCE – 479 BCE) was one of ancient China's greatest educators. "Individualized teaching" is one of his most important ideas: choosing different teaching methods according to each student's personality, interests and abilities, so that every learner's strengths shine and their weaknesses are gently corrected. The lesson is timeless — true learning starts with truly seeing the learner.

小故事 · A short story
子路与冉有 — Zilu & Ranyou

有一次，子路问孔子: "听到一个好的道理，是不是马上就要去做？" 孔子说: "你父亲和哥哥还在，你应该先问问他们。" 后来冉有问了同样的问题，孔子却回答: "听到了就马上去做。" 学生不解，孔子解释: 子路性格急，所以让他慢一点；冉有性格慢，所以让他快一点。同样的问题，因人而异地回答 — 这就是 "用心" 的教育。

Reading passage — China's tea culture

茶在中国有大约三千多年的历史，是中国最有名的传统饮料之一。无论是在家里、在饭店，还是在公司的办公室，主人都会用茶来招待客人。中国人喝茶不只是为了解渴，还和身体健康、文化传统、家人朋友的关系联系在一起。喝茶的时候，人们常常会聊天、看戏、听音乐，所以茶馆也成了重要的社交场所。
Chá zài Zhōngguó yǒu dàyuē sān qiān duō nián de lìshǐ, shì Zhōngguó zuì yǒumíng de chuántǒng yǐnliào zhī yī. Tea in China has roughly three thousand years of history and is one of China's most famous traditional drinks. Whether at home, in a restaurant, or in a company office, hosts will use tea to welcome guests. Drinking tea isn't only about quenching thirst — it's tied to health, cultural tradition, and family and friendship. While drinking tea, people often chat, watch operas, or listen to music, so teahouses also became important social places.

1大概 — approximately / probably
"大概" is an adverb expressing speculation about quantity, time, or possibility. It usually appears before the verb or quantifier and indicates the speaker is fairly sure but not 100% certain.
(主语) + 大概 + V / Adj / Quantity
他大概有点紧张。
He's probably a bit nervous.
茶在中国大概有三千多年的历史。
Tea in China has roughly more than 3,000 years of history.
我大概知道你的意思。
I roughly understand what you mean.
2偶尔 — occasionally
"偶尔" emphasises that something happens with very low frequency. It usually appears before the verb. Compare with "经常" (often) and "有时" (sometimes).
(主语) + 偶尔 + V…
我偶尔去茶馆喝茶。
I occasionally go to the teahouse for tea.
他偶尔会迟到一两分钟。
He's occasionally one or two minutes late.
偶尔有学生想换项目。
Occasionally a student wants to switch programmes.
3由 — (carried out) by
The preposition "由" introduces the person or organisation that will take responsibility for an action. It is more formal than "让" or "请" and emphasises a specific assigning of responsibility.
由 + N + V…
这次活动由李老师负责。
This event will be in charge of Teacher Li.
由我来给大家介绍一下。
Let me introduce it to everyone.
这件事由公司决定。
This matter will be decided by the company.
4V着 V着 / 边V边V — while doing X
Both express two actions happening at the same time. "V着V着" implies "while doing X (something happened)" — often used with a sudden development. "边V边V" simply states two parallel actions.
边 + V₁ + 边 + V₂ ／ V着V着 + (新的事情)
他边喝茶边看京剧。
He drinks tea while watching Beijing opera.
走着走着就到了。
Walking and walking, we got there.
说着说着，他笑了起来。
As he was speaking, he started to laugh.

文化 · Culture
中国的筷子文化 — Chopsticks in Chinese Culture

筷子是中国古代汉族人发明的一种重要餐具，已有三千多年的历史。两根简单的小棍子，却能完成挑、夹、拌、扒等许多动作，价廉物美。在中国的餐桌上，筷子不仅是工具，更承载着尊老、和谐、团结的文化观念。

Chopsticks are an everyday tool with a distinct ethnic character, created by the ancient Han Chinese. Their use dates back over 3,000 years. Two simple sticks accomplish a great deal — picking, poking, carrying, mixing — and they are inexpensive yet beautiful.

使用礼仪 · Etiquette
使用筷子需要注意的礼仪
  • 不能用筷子敲饭碗。
  • 给老人或客人夹菜应先于自己。
  • 不能把筷子直立插在饭里（被视为非常不礼貌）。
  • 不能用筷子指着别人或挥来挥去。

Don't tap the bowl with chopsticks; serve elders or guests first; never stand chopsticks vertically in rice (it resembles incense at a funeral); don't point them at others or wave them around.

课文 1 · 李进出差，王静和爸爸聊天儿
Li Jin is on a business trip; Wang Jing chats with her father
爸爸
这次李进去南方出差是要待几天呀？
Zhè cì Lǐ Jìn qù nánfāng chū chāi shì yào dāi jǐ tiān ya?
How long is Li Jin staying in the south for this business trip?
王静
大概一个星期吧。他们公司打算在那边开新办公室。
Dàgài yí ge xīngqī ba. Tāmen gōngsī dǎsuàn zài nàbian kāi xīn bàngōngshì.
About a week. Their company plans to open a new office there.
爸爸
这几年南方的环境怎么样？听说空气没有以前好了。
Zhè jǐ nián nánfāng de huánjìng zěnmeyàng? Tīngshuō kōngqì méiyǒu yǐqián hǎo le.
How's the environment in the south these years? I heard the air isn't as good as before.
王静
是啊，工厂多了，污染也越来越严重。河里的水也没有以前干净了。
Shì a, gōngchǎng duō le, wūrǎn yě yuèláiyuè yánzhòng. Hé lǐ de shuǐ yě méiyǒu yǐqián gānjìng le.
Yes — there are more factories, and pollution is getting more serious. The water in the rivers isn't as clean as before either.
爸爸
地球是我们唯一的家。我们要从自己做起，节约用水、用电、保护环境。
Dìqiú shì wǒmen wéiyī de jiā. Wǒmen yào cóng zìjǐ zuòqǐ, jiéyuē yòng shuǐ, yòng diàn, bǎohù huánjìng.
Earth is our only home. We must start with ourselves: save water, save electricity, and protect the environment.
王静
是的。把垃圾分类，少用塑料袋，这些都是小事，但每个人都做到，世界就会不一样。
Shì de. Bǎ lājī fēnlèi, shǎo yòng sùliào dài, zhèxiē dōu shì xiǎo shì, dàn měi ge rén dōu zuò dào, shìjiè jiù huì bù yīyàng.
Right. Sorting rubbish, using fewer plastic bags — these are small things, but if everyone does them, the world will change.
课文 2 · 经理和服务员在谈工作
A hotel manager and a staff member talk about saving energy
经理
小王，从这个月起，请你每天检查一下空房间的灯和空调，是不是都关好了。
Xiǎo Wáng, cóng zhège yuè qǐ, qǐng nǐ měi tiān jiǎnchá yíxià kōng fángjiān de dēng hé kōngtiáo, shì bu shì dōu guān hǎo le.
Xiao Wang, from this month, please check every day whether the lights and air conditioners in empty rooms are properly turned off.
服务员
好的，经理。我们以前发现客人离开后没关空调和灯的情况比较多。
Hǎo de, jīnglǐ. Wǒmen yǐqián fāxiàn kèrén líkāi hòu méi guān kōngtiáo hé dēng de qíngkuàng bǐjiào duō.
Yes, manager. We've often noticed that guests don't turn off the AC or lights after they leave.
经理
所以我建议在每个房间都贴一张提示，告诉客人节约用电就是保护环境。
Suǒyǐ wǒ jiànyì zài měi ge fángjiān dōu tiē yì zhāng tíshì, gàosu kèrén jiéyuē yòng diàn jiù shì bǎohù huánjìng.
That's why I suggest putting up a small notice in every room — telling guests that saving electricity means protecting the environment.
服务员
这个主意很好。卫生间里也可以提醒客人少用一次性的牙刷、毛巾等。
Zhège zhǔyi hěn hǎo. Wèishēngjiān lǐ yě kěyǐ tíxǐng kèrén shǎo yòng yícìxìng de yáshuā, máojīn děng.
That's a great idea. In the bathroom we can also remind guests to use fewer disposable items like toothbrushes and towels.
经理
没错。这样既能减少污染，也能节省成本。一举两得。
Méi cuò. Zhèyàng jì néng jiǎnshǎo wūrǎn, yě néng jiéshěng chéngběn. Yì jǔ liǎng dé.
Exactly. That way we both reduce pollution and save costs — two birds with one stone.
课文 3 · 朴民和王静在聊不正常的季节
Park-min and Wang Jing chat about strange seasons
朴民
早上看新闻说今天又是"地球一小时"。你知道这个活动吗？
Zǎoshang kàn xīnwén shuō jīntiān yòu shì "Dìqiú Yì Xiǎoshí". Nǐ zhīdào zhège huódòng ma?
I saw on the news this morning that today is "Earth Hour" again. Do you know about this event?
王静
知道。从2007年开始，每年三月最后一个星期六的晚上，人们都会关灯一个小时，提醒大家保护地球。
Zhīdào. Cóng èr líng líng qī nián kāishǐ, měi nián sān yuè zuìhòu yí ge xīngqīliù de wǎnshang, rénmen dōu huì guān dēng yí ge xiǎoshí, tíxǐng dàjiā bǎohù dìqiú.
I do. Since 2007, on the last Saturday evening of March every year, people turn off lights for an hour to remind everyone to protect the Earth.
朴民
我觉得这几年的天气越来越奇怪了，冬天不冷，夏天又特别热。
Wǒ juéde zhè jǐ nián de tiānqì yuèláiyuè qíguài le, dōngtiān bù lěng, xiàtiān yòu tèbié rè.
I think the weather has been getting stranger these years — winters aren't cold, and summers are extra hot.
王静
于是科学家们把它叫作"全球变暖"。我们排放的废气越多，温度就越高。
Yúshì kēxuéjiāmen bǎ tā jiào zuò "quánqiú biàn nuǎn". Wǒmen páifàng de fèiqì yuè duō, wēndù jiù yuè gāo.
So scientists call it "global warming". The more waste gas we emit, the higher the temperature.
朴民
那我们能做什么呢？
Nà wǒmen néng zuò shénme ne?
So what can we do?
王静
少开车，多走路，少用一次性的东西。每个人改变一点点，世界就会变好一些。
Shǎo kāi chē, duō zǒu lù, shǎo yòng yícìxìng de dōngxi. Měi ge rén gǎibiàn yìdiǎndiǎn, shìjiè jiù huì biàn hǎo yìxiē.
Drive less, walk more, use fewer disposable things. If each person changes a little, the world becomes a little better.
课文 4 · 阅读 — 公益广告
Reading — public-service announcements
阅读
在很多公共场所，我们都能看到这样的公益广告："水是生命之源，节约用水从我做起。" "你的一次随手关灯，就是地球的一次呼吸。" "塑料袋使用一次，污染环境上百年。" 这些短短的句子虽然简单，却能让人停下来想一想：我们每天的生活习惯，正在悄悄地改变着地球。世界并不属于我们，是我们属于世界，所以保护环境，其实就是在保护我们自己。
Zài hěn duō gōnggòng chǎngsuǒ, wǒmen dōu néng kàndào zhèyàng de gōngyì guǎnggào: "Shuǐ shì shēngmìng zhī yuán, jiéyuē yòng shuǐ cóng wǒ zuòqǐ." "Nǐ de yí cì suíshǒu guān dēng, jiù shì dìqiú de yí cì hūxī." "Sùliào dài shǐyòng yí cì, wūrǎn huánjìng shàng bǎi nián." Zhèxiē duǎnduǎn de jùzi suīrán jiǎndān, què néng ràng rén tíng xiàlái xiǎng yi xiǎng: wǒmen měi tiān de shēnghuó xíguàn, zhèngzài qiāoqiāo de gǎibiàn zhe dìqiú. Shìjiè bìng bù shǔyú wǒmen, shì wǒmen shǔyú shìjiè, suǒyǐ bǎohù huánjìng, qíshí jiù shì zài bǎohù wǒmen zìjǐ.
In many public places we see ads like: "Water is the source of life — saving water starts with me." "Every time you switch off a light, the Earth takes one more breath." "A single plastic bag pollutes for over a hundred years." These short sentences are simple, yet they make us stop and think: our daily habits are quietly changing the planet. The world does not belong to us — we belong to the world. So protecting the environment is really protecting ourselves.
课文 5 · 阅读 — 一封写给地球母亲的信
Reading — A letter to our Mother Earth
阅读
亲爱的地球母亲：很久以前，你的天空是蓝的，海水是清的，森林里有各种各样的动物。可是因为我们人类的不小心，工厂排出的废气把你的天空染灰了，城市的垃圾堆得越来越高，许多动物失去了它们的家。我们现在才明白，没有你我们什么也没有。所以从今天起，我们会用心去保护你：少开车、少用电、不浪费、把垃圾分类。我们要把蓝天和清水还给你，让我们的孩子也能看到你最美的样子。
Qīn'ài de dìqiú mǔqīn: Hěn jiǔ yǐqián, nǐ de tiānkōng shì lán de, hǎishuǐ shì qīng de, sēnlín lǐ yǒu gè zhǒng gè yàng de dòngwù. Kěshì yīnwèi wǒmen rénlèi de bù xiǎoxīn, gōngchǎng páichū de fèiqì bǎ nǐ de tiānkōng rǎn huī le, chéngshì de lājī duī de yuèláiyuè gāo, xǔduō dòngwù shīqù le tāmen de jiā. Wǒmen xiànzài cái míngbai, méiyǒu nǐ wǒmen shénme yě méiyǒu. Suǒyǐ cóng jīntiān qǐ, wǒmen huì yòng xīn qù bǎohù nǐ: shǎo kāi chē, shǎo yòng diàn, bú làngfèi, bǎ lājī fēnlèi. Wǒmen yào bǎ lántiān hé qīngshuǐ huán gěi nǐ, ràng wǒmen de háizi yě néng kàn dào nǐ zuì měi de yàngzi.
Dear Mother Earth: Long ago, your sky was blue, your sea was clear, and your forests were full of all kinds of animals. But because of our carelessness, the smoke from factories has greyed your sky, the rubbish in cities piles up ever higher, and many animals have lost their homes. Only now do we realise — without you, we have nothing. So from today, we'll protect you with our heart: drive less, use less power, waste nothing, sort our rubbish. We'll give you back your blue skies and clear waters, so that our children too can see you at your most beautiful.

1越来越 — more and more …
"越来越 + adj/v" describes a continuing change. The adjective or stative verb after it cannot be modified by 很 / 非常.
主语 + 越来越 + Adj / V
空气越来越差。
The air is getting worse and worse.
他越来越喜欢汉语。
He likes Chinese more and more.
夏天越来越热。
Summers are getting hotter.
2越 A 越 B — the more A, the more B
"越 + V/Adj₁ + 越 + V/Adj₂" expresses that the change in the second part follows the change in the first. Both parts must usually share the same subject.
主语 + 越 + V/Adj₁ + 越 + V/Adj₂
越紧张越要笑着上台。
The more nervous, the more he must smile on stage.
这本书越看越有意思。
The more I read this book, the more interesting it gets.
他越解释，大家越糊涂。
The more he explains, the more confused everyone gets.
3于是 — so; therefore
"于是" links two clauses where the second states what happened as a direct result. It usually appears at the start of the second clause.
…，于是 + 主语 + V…
天越来越冷，于是我们关上了窗户。
It got colder and colder, so we closed the windows.
忽然下雨了，于是我们决定改天再去。
It suddenly rained, so we decided to go another day.
他听完后觉得很有道理，于是同意了。
After hearing it he found it reasonable, so he agreed.
4从 … 起 — starting from …
"从 + Time/Place + 起" marks the starting point of an action or change. "起" can also be replaced by "开始".
从 + Time/Place + 起，主语 + V…
从这个月起，我开始锻炼身体。
Starting from this month, I'm beginning to exercise.
从明天起，我每天读半小时书。
From tomorrow, I'll read half an hour every day.
从我做起，保护环境。
Starting from me — protect the environment.

文化 · Culture
天人合一 — The Unity of Heaven and Man

"天人合一" 是中国哲学中关于人与自然关系的一种观点，意思是人与大自然要"合一"，要和平共处。它最早是由庄子（约公元前 369—公元前 286）提出的，唐代名学者季羡林先生将其解释为：天，就是大自然；人，就是人类；合，就是互相理解、结成友谊。

"Tiānrén Héyī" — the unity of Heaven and Man — is a classical Chinese philosophy about the relationship between humans and nature. It teaches that humankind should live in harmony with the natural world and coexist with it peacefully. The idea was first proposed by Zhuangzi (369–286 BCE). The contemporary scholar Ji Xianlin explained it as: "Heaven" = nature; "Man" = humankind; "He" (合) = mutual understanding and friendship.

课文 1 · 李老师建议王静让孩子养成好习惯
Teacher Li suggests how Wang Jing can help her child build good habits
王静
李老师，我家孩子这次钢琴考试又得了第一名，这真要谢谢您！
Lǐ lǎoshī, wǒ jiā háizi zhè cì gāngqín kǎoshì yòu dé le dì-yī míng, zhè zhēn yào xièxie nín!
Teacher Li, my child got first place in the piano exam again — I really have to thank you!
李老师
不用谢，孩子自己很认真，进步也快。最重要的是他每天都坚持练习。
Búyòng xiè, háizi zìjǐ hěn rènzhēn, jìnbù yě kuài. Zuì zhòngyào de shì tā měi tiān dōu jiānchí liànxí.
Don't mention it. Your child is very serious and progresses quickly. The most important thing is that he practises every day.
王静
是啊。可是我父亲说，让一个六岁的孩子坚持每天练琴一个小时，太辛苦了。
Shì a. Kěshì wǒ fùqīn shuō, ràng yí ge liù suì de háizi jiānchí měi tiān liàn qín yí ge xiǎoshí, tài xīnkǔ le.
Yes. But my father says making a six-year-old practise the piano for an hour every day is too tiring.
李老师
其实习惯比兴趣更重要。一个孩子如果从小养成好习惯，长大以后做什么事情都更容易成功。
Qíshí xíguàn bǐ xìngqù gèng zhòngyào. Yí ge háizi rúguǒ cóng xiǎo yǎngchéng hǎo xíguàn, zhǎng dà yǐhòu zuò shénme shìqíng dōu gèng róngyì chénggōng.
Actually, habits are even more important than interest. If a child develops good habits when young, then no matter what they do later, they'll find success more easily.
王静
那么，您觉得我应该怎么帮助他呢？
Nàme, nín juéde wǒ yīnggāi zěnme bāngzhù tā ne?
So, how do you think I should help him?
李老师
和孩子一起定一个时间表，每天什么时候练琴、什么时候做作业、什么时候休息，都要清清楚楚。
Hé háizi yìqǐ dìng yí ge shíjiānbiǎo, měi tiān shénme shíhou liàn qín, shénme shíhou zuò zuòyè, shénme shíhou xiūxi, dōu yào qīngqīngchǔchǔ.
Sit down with your child and make a schedule together — clearly setting when to practise piano, when to do homework, when to rest.
课文 2 · 王静建议朴民教孩子学会安排时间
Wang Jing tells Park-min to help his child manage time
朴民
真希望我家孩子能像你家孩子一样，自觉、不偷懒。我家这位每天叫他起床都要叫好几次。
Zhēn xīwàng wǒ jiā háizi néng xiàng nǐ jiā háizi yíyàng, zìjué, bù tōulǎn. Wǒ jiā zhè wèi měi tiān jiào tā qǐchuáng dōu yào jiào hǎo jǐ cì.
I really wish my kid could be like yours — self-disciplined, not lazy. Mine has to be called several times every morning to get out of bed.
王静
我以前也是这样的。后来我给他买了一个小闹钟，让他自己安排早上的时间。慢慢地他就习惯了。
Wǒ yǐqián yě shì zhèyàng de. Hòulái wǒ gěi tā mǎi le yí ge xiǎo nàozhōng, ràng tā zìjǐ ānpái zǎoshang de shíjiān. Mànman de tā jiù xíguàn le.
I used to be the same. Later I bought him a small alarm clock and let him manage his own morning. Bit by bit, he got used to it.
朴民
可是我担心他自己起不来，又怕他迟到。
Kěshì wǒ dānxīn tā zìjǐ qǐ bu lái, yòu pà tā chídào.
But I worry he won't get up by himself, and that he'll be late.
王静
迟到一两次没关系。他迟到了，自己感到不好意思，下次就会自己起来。我们千万不要替他做所有事。
Chídào yì liǎng cì méi guānxi. Tā chídào le, zìjǐ gǎndào bù hǎoyìsi, xià cì jiù huì zìjǐ qǐlái. Wǒmen qiānwàn bú yào tì tā zuò suǒyǒu shì.
Being late once or twice doesn't matter. If he's late and feels embarrassed, next time he'll get up by himself. We absolutely shouldn't do everything for him.
朴民
那批评他可以吗？
Nà pīpíng tā kěyǐ ma?
Is it okay to criticise him then?
王静
少批评，多表扬。一句"你做得真棒"，比一百句"你怎么这么慢"管用。
Shǎo pīpíng, duō biǎoyáng. Yí jù "nǐ zuò de zhēn bàng", bǐ yì bǎi jù "nǐ zěnme zhème màn" guǎnyòng.
Less criticism, more praise. One "you did a great job" is more effective than a hundred "why are you so slow".
课文 3 · 朴民和孙月讨论养孩子的方法
Park-min and Sun Yue discuss how to raise children
朴民
明天我要带儿子去医院打针。他特别害怕打针，每次都哭着不肯进医院。
Míngtiān wǒ yào dài érzi qù yīyuàn dǎ zhēn. Tā tèbié hàipà dǎ zhēn, měi cì dōu kū zhe bù kěn jìn yīyuàn.
Tomorrow I have to take my son to the hospital for a shot. He's especially afraid of injections — every time he cries and refuses to go in.
孙月
我儿子以前也很害羞，怕医生、怕打针。后来我给他讲了一个故事：一个勇敢的小医生帮助了很多生病的小动物。从那以后他就好多了。
Wǒ érzi yǐqián yě hěn hàixiū, pà yīshēng, pà dǎ zhēn. Hòulái wǒ gěi tā jiǎng le yí ge gùshi: yí ge yǒnggǎn de xiǎo yīshēng bāngzhù le hěn duō shēng bìng de xiǎo dòngwù. Cóng nà yǐhòu tā jiù hǎo duō le.
My son also used to be shy and afraid of doctors and injections. Later I told him a story about a brave little doctor helping sick animals. From then on he was much better.
朴民
那真聪明！我有时候真的很怀疑自己的方法是对的还是错的。
Nà zhēn cōngming! Wǒ yǒushíhou zhēn de hěn huáiyí zìjǐ de fāngfǎ shì duì de háishi cuò de.
That was really clever! Sometimes I really doubt whether my methods are right or wrong.
孙月
每个孩子都不一样，所以教育的方法也要根据他们的性格变化。一定不能千篇一律。
Měi ge háizi dōu bù yīyàng, suǒyǐ jiàoyù de fāngfǎ yě yào gēnjù tāmen de xìnggé biànhuà. Yídìng bù néng qiānpiān-yílǜ.
Every child is different, so the way you educate them must change according to their personality. You absolutely can't apply one and the same approach to all.
朴民
是啊，教育孩子真的是一门艺术。光有爱还不够，还要懂他、信任他。
Shì a, jiàoyù háizi zhēn de shì yì mén yìshù. Guāng yǒu ài hái bú gòu, hái yào dǒng tā, xìnrèn tā.
Yes — raising children really is an art. Love alone isn't enough; you also have to understand them and trust them.
课文 4 · 阅读 — 鼓励的力量
Reading — the power of encouragement
阅读
在孩子成长的过程中，鼓励比批评更有力量。心理学家做过一个实验：把一群孩子分成两组，一组的家长经常说"你真聪明"，另一组的家长则常常说"你的努力真值得我表扬"。一年以后，第二组的孩子比第一组进步得更快。这告诉我们，孩子需要的不是被夸"聪明"，而是被认可他们的努力和坚持。每一个温暖的眼神、每一句真诚的话，都会成为孩子成长路上最重要的力量。
Zài háizi chéngzhǎng de guòchéng zhōng, gǔlì bǐ pīpíng gèng yǒu lìliang. Xīnlǐxuéjiā zuò guo yí ge shíyàn: bǎ yì qún háizi fēn chéng liǎng zǔ, yì zǔ de jiāzhǎng jīngcháng shuō "nǐ zhēn cōngming", lìng yì zǔ de jiāzhǎng zé chángcháng shuō "nǐ de nǔlì zhēn zhídé wǒ biǎoyáng". Yì nián yǐhòu, dì-èr zǔ de háizi bǐ dì-yī zǔ jìnbù de gèng kuài. Zhè gàosu wǒmen, háizi xūyào de bú shì bèi kuā "cōngming", ér shì bèi rènkě tāmen de nǔlì hé jiānchí. Měi yí ge wēnnuǎn de yǎnshén, měi yí jù zhēnchéng de huà, dōu huì chéngwéi háizi chéngzhǎng lùshang zuì zhòngyào de lìliang.
As children grow up, encouragement is more powerful than criticism. Psychologists once ran an experiment: a group of children were split into two — parents in one group often said "you're so smart", while parents in the other group often said "your effort really deserves praise". A year later, the second group progressed faster than the first. This tells us that children need not to be praised as "smart", but to be acknowledged for their effort and persistence. Every warm look, every sincere word will become the most important strength on a child's path of growth.
课文 5 · 阅读 — 父母是孩子最好的老师
Reading — parents are children's best teachers
阅读
人们常说，父母是孩子最好的老师。这句话非常有道理。孩子年纪小，模仿能力很强。父母怎么做事、怎么说话、怎么对待别人，孩子都会一点一点地学。如果父母诚实、热爱阅读、善于学习，孩子也会自然地养成这些好习惯；如果父母经常吵架，对工作不认真，那孩子长大以后也很难有好的态度。所以，最好的教育，其实是父母本身的样子。
Rénmen cháng shuō, fùmǔ shì háizi zuì hǎo de lǎoshī. Zhè jù huà fēicháng yǒu dàoli. Háizi niánjì xiǎo, mófǎng nénglì hěn qiáng. Fùmǔ zěnme zuò shì, zěnme shuō huà, zěnme duìdài biérén, háizi dōu huì yìdiǎn yìdiǎn de xué. Rúguǒ fùmǔ chéngshí, rè'ài yuèdú, shànyú xuéxí, háizi yě huì zìrán de yǎngchéng zhèxiē hǎo xíguàn; rúguǒ fùmǔ jīngcháng chǎojià, duì gōngzuò bú rènzhēn, nà háizi zhǎng dà yǐhòu yě hěn nán yǒu hǎo de tàidu. Suǒyǐ, zuì hǎo de jiàoyù, qíshí shì fùmǔ běnshēn de yàngzi.
People often say that parents are their child's best teacher. There's deep truth in this. Children are young and excellent imitators. How parents do things, how they speak, how they treat others — children will learn it bit by bit. If parents are honest, love reading and are good at learning, children will naturally pick up those habits; if parents often quarrel and don't take work seriously, then it'll be hard for the children to have a good attitude when they grow up. So, the best education is in fact the very example parents set themselves.

1起来 — directional / inceptive complement
"起来" follows a verb to express upward motion (站起来 stand up, 拿起来 pick up) or to mark the beginning / continuation of an action or state (说起来 to start saying / talking about, 想起来 to recall).
V + 起来 (+ Object)

说起来容易，做起来难。
Easier said than done.
想起来了，那是十年前的事。
I just remembered — that was ten years ago.
2替 — on behalf of
"替" means to do something on someone else's behalf, or in place of someone. It typically precedes the person being represented.
替 + 人 + V…
不要替孩子做所有事。
Don't do everything for the child.
请替我向他问好。
Please say hello to him for me.
他生病了，我替他上课。
He's sick, so I'm teaching the class for him.
3千万 — by all means / on no account
"千万" is a strong reminder. It often appears in negative sentences (千万不要 / 千万别) and indicates that the speaker really emphasises the warning.
千万 + 不要/别 + V…
千万不要替他做所有事。

4A 比 B 更 + Adj — A is even more Adj than B
"更" is added to a 比-comparison to emphasise that on top of B already being Adj, A is to a greater degree. Without 更, the difference is simply stated.

文化故事 · Cultural story
孟母三迁 — Mencius' Mother Moved Thrice

孟子（约公元前 372 — 公元前 289）是中国古代著名的思想家、教育家。他和孔子一样，是儒家学派的主要代表人物之一。孟子小时候，他的母亲为了让他成长得更好，曾搬过三次家：第一次因为家附近是坟墓，孟子常常模仿别人办丧事；第二次因为家旁边是市场，孟子又开始模仿做买卖；最后母亲把家搬到学堂附近，孟子从此开始勤奋读书。这个故事告诉我们：环境能够改变一个人的好坏与习惯，给孩子一个好的成长环境非常重要。

Mencius (c. 372 BC – 289 BC) was a famous thinker and educator of ancient China. Like Confucius, he was a major representative of Confucianism. When he was a child, his mother moved their home three times to find a good environment for him. At first, they lived near a graveyard, where Mencius mimicked funerals. They moved next to a market, where he imitated trade. Finally his mother moved the family near a school — and from then on Mencius studied hard and grew up to be a sage. The story tells us how strongly the environment shapes a child's habits and character.

中国古代经典童蒙读物 — Classical primers for children

《三字经》、《百家姓》、《千字文》是中国传统教育中最有名的三本童蒙读物，简称 "三百千"。它们用简短押韵的句子，把识字、礼仪、历史和做人之理都融在一起，让孩子在朗朗上口的诵读中学习。今天，仍然有许多家长选择给孩子读这些经典，用古人的智慧帮助孩子从小养成好习惯。

课文 4 · 阅读 — 明天和后天
Reading — Tomorrow and the day after
阅读
有人说："明天很美好，后天也很美好，可是大部分人都倒在今天晚上。" 这句话听起来像一个笑话，其实非常真实。我们常常对未来充满希望，却很少认真过好今天。每一个想要让生活更美好的人，都应当从今天的一件小事做起：给家人一句温暖的话，给同事一次真诚的微笑，给自己一个小小的进步。明天会变成今天，今天又会变成昨天。把每一个"今天"过好，明天才会真正美好。
Yǒu rén shuō: "Míngtiān hěn měihǎo, hòutiān yě hěn měihǎo, kěshì dà bùfen rén dōu dǎo zài jīntiān wǎnshang." Zhè jù huà tīng qǐlái xiàng yí ge xiàohuà, qíshí fēicháng zhēnshí. Wǒmen chángcháng duì wèilái chōngmǎn xīwàng, què hěn shǎo rènzhēn guò hǎo jīntiān. Měi yí ge xiǎng yào ràng shēnghuó gèng měihǎo de rén, dōu yīngdāng cóng jīntiān de yí jiàn xiǎo shì zuòqǐ: gěi jiārén yí jù wēnnuǎn de huà, gěi tóngshì yí cì zhēnchéng de wēixiào, gěi zìjǐ yí ge xiǎoxiǎo de jìnbù. Míngtiān huì biànchéng jīntiān, jīntiān yòu huì biànchéng zuótiān. Bǎ měi yí ge "jīntiān" guò hǎo, míngtiān cái huì zhēnzhèng měihǎo.
Someone once said: "Tomorrow is wonderful and the day after is wonderful too — but most people fall down tonight." It sounds like a joke, but it's deeply true. We often fill the future with hope while rarely truly living today. Anyone who wants their life to be better should begin with one small thing today: a warm word to family, a sincere smile to a colleague, a tiny step forward for themselves. Tomorrow will become today; today will become yesterday. Live every "today" well — only then will tomorrow truly be wonderful.

"到底" can intensify a question (showing impatience or strong curiosity) or affirm a final outcome. In a question it generally appears between subject and verb.

文化故事 · Cultural story
只要功夫深，铁杵也能磨成针 — As Long as You Work Hard Enough

李白（701–762），是中国唐朝时期著名的诗人。他小时候对学习提不起兴趣，经常跑到外面玩儿。一天，李白在河边发现一位老婆婆正在磨一根铁棒子。李白觉得很奇怪，就上前问这位老婆婆在干什么。原来老婆婆的小女儿要绣花，她想给小女儿一根绣花针。他所到这里，惊了：像大部分孩子一样想成大，因为他认为打铁棒子磨成绣花针太浪费时间了。等老婆婆磨好了，她终于可以做出来给孙女了。可是，老婆婆自信地告诉他："只要功夫深，铁杵磨成针。" 听完老婆婆的回答，只要坚持就可以成功。从此，李白就开始勤奋学习，积累知识，最后成了一名伟大的诗人。

Li Bai (701–762) is one of the Tang dynasty's most famous poets. He wasn't interested in studying as a child and often went out to play. One day Li Bai saw an old woman by a river grinding an iron pestle. He asked what she was doing. She said she was making a needle for her granddaughter to embroider with. Astonished — surely an iron pestle could not become a needle! — Li Bai was convinced she was wasting her time. But the old woman calmly replied: "As long as you work hard enough, an iron pestle can be ground down to a needle." From that day, Li Bai studied diligently, accumulated knowledge and became a great poet. The lesson endures: perseverance, more than talent or luck, is what truly carries us through.

中国国宝大熊猫 — Giant Pandas, China's National Treasure

大熊猫是中国国家一级保护动物。它的身体只有黑色和白色两种颜色，圆圆的脸、大大的黑眼圈、胖胖的身体，非常可爱。大熊猫已经在地球上生活了至少 800 万年，人们叫它 "活化石"。野外大熊猫现在主要生活在中国四川、陕西和甘肃的山区，一般能活到 18 — 20 岁；圈养的大熊猫可以活到 30 岁以上。它们每天有一半的时间用来吃东西，剩下的一半时间多数都在睡觉。在野外，大熊猫每两次吃东西的中间睡 2 — 4 个小时，即使在睡觉的时候，大熊猫看起来也很可爱。

The giant panda is a first-class nationally protected and endangered animal in China. With its black-and-white coat, round face, dark eye-patches and chubby body, it is one of the world's most beloved animals. Pandas have existed for at least eight million years and are known as "living fossils". Wild pandas mainly live in the mountains of Sichuan, Shaanxi and Gansu, where they live to about 18–20 years; captive pandas can live more than 30. They spend half the day eating and most of the rest sleeping. Even napping, they remain irresistibly cute.

1"又"和"再"的比较 · Comparison of "又" and "再"

副词"又"和"再"都可以放在动词前边,表示动作或情况的重复出现。"又 + V"通常表示已发生了的重复动作或情况,"再 + V"表示还没发生的重复动作或情况。
Both "又" and "再" can precede verbs to show repetition. "又 + V" usually means the recurrence has already happened; "再 + V" means the recurrence has not yet happened.

又 + V (已发生)　｜　再 + V (还没发生)

"又"的例句:

上个星期我买了一条裤子,昨天又买了一条。
I bought a pair of pants last week, and bought another yesterday.
你上午已经喝了一杯咖啡,下午怎么又喝了一杯？
You already had a coffee this morning — why another this afternoon?
小刚,你前天迟到,昨天迟到,今天怎么又迟到了？
Xiaogang, you were late the day before yesterday, yesterday, how come you're late again today?

"再"的例句:

你只吃了一点儿饭,再吃一点儿吧。
You only ate a little — have some more.
家里只有一个面包了,我们再买一些吧。
There's only one loaf left — let's buy some more.
那个饭馆我昨天去了一次,明天还想再去一次。
I went to that restaurant yesterday; I want to go again tomorrow.
昨天去看了看,今天又去看了看,明天还要再去看看。
Went yesterday, went again today, will go again tomorrow.
2疑问代词活用 1 · Flexible Use of Interrogative Pronouns

汉语中,用"就"连接两个相同的疑问代词,表达不确指的人、事物或方式。疑问代词在句中出现两次,前后所指的对象相同。当前后两个分句的主语相同时,后一个分句中的主语可以省略;如果两个分句的主语不同,第二个主语要放在"就"的前边。
"就" connects two same interrogative pronouns to indicate an uncertain person, thing or manner. Same subject: the 2nd can be left out. Different subjects: 2nd subject goes right before 就.

WH + (S₂) + 就 + WH　(WH = 什么/哪儿/谁/哪天…)
什么东西便宜我就买什么。
Whatever is cheap, I'll buy.
你哪天有时间就哪天来我家吧。
Come to my place whenever you have time.
谁喜欢他他就喜欢谁。
He likes whoever likes him.
你坐哪儿我就坐哪儿。
I'll sit wherever you sit.
你去哪儿我就去哪儿。
I'll go wherever you go.
我想吃什么就做什么,想吃多少就做多少。
I cook whatever I want, however much I want.

1"越 A 越 B" 结构 · The Structure "越A越B"

"越 A 越 B"表示 B 随着 A 的变化而变化。A 和 B 可以是动词或形容词。
"越 A 越 B" (the more A, the more B) indicates B changes along with A.

越 + A + 越 + B
雨越下越大。
The rain is falling harder and harder.
你的中文越说越好。
Your Chinese is getting better and better.
越往南,天气越热。
The further south, the hotter the weather.
山越高,路越难走。
The higher the mountain, the harder the road.
休息得越多,好得越快。
The more you rest, the faster you recover.
你总是吃甜的东西,会越吃越胖。
If you keep eating sweets, you'll get fatter.

注意"越 A 越 B" 和"越来越 + Adj" 的区别:"越来越"表示程度随时间变化,"越A越B"表示两件事的程度同步变化。
Note: "越来越 + Adj" shows change over time; "越A越B" shows two things changing in sync.

🏯 Common Saying · 俗语
不可同日而语Bù kě tóng rì ér yǔ
"不可同日而语"的意思是不能放在同一个时间谈论,用来比喻差别很大,不能放在一起比较。例如:通信技术进步太快了,过去跟现在不可同日而语。
"不可同日而语", literally "(two things) cannot be mentioned in the same breath", is used metaphorically to mean that there is no comparison between two people or two things because of the significant difference. For instance, in terms of science and technology, there is no comparison between the past and the present.
