What's your plan for the weekend

Text 1 · 谈周末的打算
Talking about the plan for the weekend
小丽
周末你有什么打算？
Zhōumò nǐ yǒu shénme dǎsuàn?
What's your plan for the weekend?
小刚
我早就想好了，请你吃饭、看电影、喝咖啡。
Wǒ zǎo jiù xiǎnghǎo le, qǐng nǐ chīfàn, kàn diànyǐng, hē kāfēi.
I've already planned it — I'll treat you to dinner, a movie, and coffee.
小丽
请我？
Qǐng wǒ?
Treat me?
小刚
是啊，我已经找好饭馆了，电影票也买好了。
Shì a, wǒ yǐjīng zhǎohǎo fànguǎnr le, diànyǐngpiào yě mǎihǎo le.
Yes! I've already found a restaurant and bought the movie tickets.
小丽
我还没想好要不要跟你去呢。
Wǒ hái méi xiǎnghǎo yào bu yào gēn nǐ qù ne.
I haven't decided yet whether I'll go with you.

At home
妈妈

Text 3 · 聊旅游计划
Talking about the travel plan
小丽
下个月我去旅游，你能跟我一起去吗？
Xià ge yuè wǒ qù lǚyóu, nǐ néng gēn wǒ yìqǐ qù ma?
I'm going travelling next month — can you come with me?
小刚
我还没想好呢。你觉得哪儿最好玩儿？
Wǒ hái méi xiǎnghǎo ne. Nǐ juéde nǎr zuì hǎowánr?
I haven't decided yet. Where do you think is the most fun?
小丽
南方啊，我们去年就是这个时候去的。
Nánfāng a, wǒmen qùnián jiù shì zhège shíhou qù de.
The south! We went there at this time last year.
小刚
南方太热了，北方好一些，不冷也不热。
Nánfāng tài rè le, běifāng hǎo yìxiē, bù lěng yě bú rè.
The south is too hot. The north is better — not cold, not hot.
Text 4 · 准备去旅游
Preparing for their trip
小刚
水果、面包、茶都准备好了，我们还带什么？
Shuǐguǒ, miànbāo, chá dōu zhǔnbèihǎo le, wǒmen hái dài shénme?
Fruit, bread and tea are all ready — what else should we bring?
小丽
手机、电脑、地图，一个也不能少。
Shǒujī, diànnǎo, dìtú, yí ge yě bù néng shǎo.
Phone, laptop, and map — not one can be left behind.
小刚
这些我昨天下午就准备好了。
Zhèxiē wǒ zuótiān xiàwǔ jiù zhǔnbèihǎo le.
I prepared all of those yesterday afternoon.
小丽
再多带几件衣服吧。
Zài duō dài jǐ jiàn yīfu ba.
Bring a few more clothes then.
小刚
我们是去旅游，不是搬家，还是少带一些吧。
Wǒmen shì qù lǚyóu, bú shì bān jiā, háishi shǎo dài yìxiē ba.
We're going on a trip, not moving house — better to pack light.

1结果补语"好" · The Complement of Result "好"

结果补语"好"用在动词的后边，如"吃好""准备好"，表示动作完成，并让人满意。
The complement of result "好" follows a verb to indicate that the action is satisfactorily completed.

动词 + 好 + 了
我早就想好了，请你吃饭、看电影、喝咖啡。
I've already planned it out — dinner, a movie, and coffee.
饭还没做好，请你等一会儿。
The meal isn't ready yet — please wait a moment.
去旅游的东西准备好了吗？
Have you prepared everything for the trip?
2"一……也/都+不/没……" · The Negative Structure

用"一＋量词＋名词＋也/都＋不/没＋V"表示完全否定。
"一 + Measure Word + Noun + 也/都 + 不/没 + V" indicates complete negation (not...at all).

一 + 量词 + 名词 + 也/都 + 不/没 + V
手机、电脑、地图，一个也不能少。
Phone, laptop, map — not one can be left out.
你怎么一点儿也不着急？
Why aren't you worried at all?
我一个苹果也不想吃。
I don't want to eat even one apple.
3连词"那" · The Conjunction "那"

"那"放在句首，表示依据上文的意思得出的结果。
Used at the beginning of a sentence, "那" indicates the result obtained from what was said previously.

那 + （结果）
（明天的考试）我早就复习好了。那也不能一直玩儿啊。
I finished reviewing ages ago. — Even so, you can't just play all day.
A: 我不想去看电影。B: 那我也不去了。
A: I don't want to see a movie. B: Then I won't go either.

🏯 Common Saying · 俗语
不到长城非好汉Bú dào Chángchéng fēi hǎohàn
"不到长城非好汉",这句话里"非"的意思是"不是","好汉"的意思是"很厉害的人""英雄"。这句话的意思是没有去过长城的人就不是英雄,常常用来比喻一种积极的精神,遇到困难,要努力想办法克服,解决了问题,那就是"好汉"。
In the saying "不到长城非好汉", "非" means "not", and "好汉" means "awesome person" or "hero". The saying means that a person who has never been to the Great Wall is not a hero. As a metaphor for positive spirit, it defines a hero as one who tries hard and works out the problems he faces.

🗺️ Geography · 地理文化
中国的南方与北方South vs North China
中国的南方和北方在气候、饮食、文化上差别很大。北方比较干燥、寒冷,有饺子、面条等以小麦为主的食物。南方比较温暖、潮湿,主食是米饭。中国人在国内旅行时,常常会讨论哪个地方"更好"!
China's north and south differ greatly in climate, cuisine, and culture. 北方 (běifāng) — the north — tends to be drier and colder, known for wheat-based foods like dumplings and noodles. 南方 (nánfāng) — the south — is warmer and more humid, with rice as the staple food. When Chinese people travel within China, they often debate which region is "better"!

1简单趋向补语 · Simple Complements of Direction

"V + 来 / 去"表示动作的方向。"来"表示朝着说话人的方向，"去"表示背离说话人的方向。最常用的动词有"上、下、进、出、回、过、起"。
"V + 来/去" indicates the direction of an action, with "来" indicating the direction towards the speaker and "去" the direction away.

V + 来 / 去
我们在楼上等呢，你上来吧。
We're waiting upstairs — come up.
那边树多，我们过去坐一下吧。
There are lots of trees over there — let's go over and sit.
我上楼去给你拿把伞。
I'll go upstairs to get you an umbrella.

如果宾语是地点名词，要放在"来/去"的前边；如果是事物名词，可以放在前边，也可以放在后边。
Place-noun objects go before 来/去. Thing-noun objects may go before or after.

小狗下楼来吃东西。
The puppy comes downstairs to eat.
明天要带作业来。
Bring the homework tomorrow.
这次旅游，我想带去这本书。
I want to bring this book on the trip.
2"V₁了……就V₂……" · Two Successive Actions

表示两个动作连续发生，后边的动作紧接着前边的动作。
Used to indicate two successive actions, with the second immediately following the first.

V₁ + 了 + …… + 就 + V₂ + ……
我下了课就吃饭。
I eat right after class ends.
你每天晚上吃了饭就睡觉。
Every night you go to sleep right after eating.
小刚拿了伞就下来。
Xiaogang came down as soon as he got the umbrella.

如果句子中有两个主语，第二个主语要放在"就"前边。
If the sentence has two subjects, the second subject goes right before 就.

他到了办公室我就告诉他。
As soon as he arrives at the office, I'll tell him.
爸爸回来了我们就吃饭。
When Dad's back, we'll eat.
3反问的表达"能……吗？" · The Rhetorical Question

"能……吗"表示反问。肯定形式表达否定意思，否定形式表达肯定意思。
"能……吗?" is used in rhetorical questions — the positive form means a negative, the negative form means a positive.

能 + …… + 吗？
你不做作业，也不练习，能学好吗？（= 不能学好）
You don't do homework or practice — how can you learn well? (= you can't)
你每天晚上吃了饭就睡觉，也不出去走走，能不胖吗？（= 会胖）
You sleep right after eating and never walk around — how could you not gain weight? (= you will)

👥 Pair Work · 双人活动
两人一组,其中一个人用简单趋向补语说句子,另一个做出相应的动作,然后交替进行。

例如:出去、进来 / 出门去、进教室来 / 下楼去、上楼来
👨‍👩‍👧 Group Work · 小组活动
3~4 人一组,说说你家谁最忙。每组请一位同学报告。

例:A: 你们家谁最忙? B: 我们家我最忙。A: 你每天做什么事? B: 我每天早上起了床就吃饭，吃了饭就去上课……
Pair Work: Work in pairs. One says a sentence using a simple complement of direction, and the other acts accordingly. Then switch roles.
Example — 出去 (go out) / 进来 (come in) / 下楼去 (go downstairs).

Group Work: Work in groups of 3–4. Talk about who is the busiest in your family. Ask one member to report the results to the class.
🏯 Common Saying · 俗语
饭后百步走,活到九十九Fàn hòu bǎi bù zǒu, huódào jiǔshíjiǔ
"饭后百步走,活到九十九"——这句话字面的意思是吃完饭以后要走一百步,这样可以活到九十九岁。现在人们常用这句话说明,吃饭以后要运动运动,身体才能健康。
The literal meaning of the saying "饭后百步走,活到九十九" is that a walk of 100 steps after each meal every day makes one live to 99 years old. Now it means it is good for health to do some exercise after a meal.

桌子上放着很多饮料

There are plenty of drinks on the table

Text 1 · 在小丽家
At Xiaoli's home
小刚
明天是晴天还是阴天？
Míngtiān shì qíngtiān háishi yīntiān?
Will it be sunny or cloudy tomorrow?
小丽
阴天，电视上说多云。怎么了？有事？
Yīntiān, diànshì shang shuō duōyún. Zěnme le? Yǒu shì?
Cloudy — the TV said it'll be overcast. Why? Something up?
小刚
没事，我们明天要去爬山。
Méi shì, wǒmen míngtiān yào qù pá shān.
Nothing — we're going to climb the mountain tomorrow.
小丽
爬山的时候要小心点儿。
Pá shān de shíhou yào xiǎoxīn diǎnr.
Be careful when you're climbing.
小刚
好，你也去吗？
Hǎo, nǐ yě qù ma?
OK. Are you going too?
小丽
我不去，我有事。
Wǒ bú qù, wǒ yǒu shì.
I'm not going — I have something to do.

Text 3 · 在水果店
In a fruit store
周太太
这些水果真新鲜，我们买西瓜还是苹果？
Zhèxiē shuǐguǒ zhēn xīnxiān, wǒmen mǎi xīguā háishi píngguǒ?

Xīguā ba. Nǐ kàn, zhè shàngmian xiězhe "xīguā bù tián bú yào qián".

花茶、绿茶、红茶，我都喜欢。

Flower tea, green tea, black tea — I like them all.

1"还是"和"或者" · Comparison of "还是" and "或者"

"还是"和"或者"都可以表示选择。一般来说，"还是"用在疑问句中，"或者"用在陈述句中。
Both "还是" and "或者" connect choices. "还是" is used in interrogative sentences; "或者" is used in declarative sentences.

A 还是 B ?　｜　A 或者 B
你要喝咖啡还是喝茶？
Do you want coffee or tea?
明天是晴天还是阴天？
Will it be sunny or cloudy tomorrow?
今天晚上吃米饭或者面条都可以。
For dinner, rice or noodles — either is fine.
天冷了或者工作累了的时候，喝杯热茶会很舒服。
When it's cold or when tired from work, a cup of hot tea feels great.

有的句子包含疑问形式的小句，小句中只能用"还是"。
In clauses with an interrogative meaning, only "还是" can be used.

小丽还没想好周末去爬山还是去看电影。
Xiaoli hasn't decided whether to go climbing or to a movie this weekend.
他的生日是10月还是11月，我不记得了。
I don't remember whether his birthday is in October or November.
2存在的表达 · Expressing Existence

用"Location Word + V着 + NP"表示什么地方有什么东西。V常常是"放、写、坐、住"等。NP一般是不确定的事物（如"一本书""几个人"），不能是"这本书""周经理"等定指的名词。
This structure means "there is something somewhere". Common verbs: 放/写/坐/住. The NP must be indefinite.

Location + V着 + 数词 + 量词 + N
桌子上放着一杯咖啡。
There's a cup of coffee on the table.
我家楼上住着一个老师。
A teacher lives on the floor above us.
上面写着320元。
It says 320 yuan on it.
桌子上放着很多饮料。
There are lots of drinks on the table.

否定形式是"Location Word + 没V着 + NP"，NP前不再有量词。
Negative form: "Location + 没V着 + NP", no measure word before the noun phrase.

桌子上没放着咖啡。
There's no coffee on the table.
我的电脑旁边没放着书。
There are no books next to my computer.
3"会"表示可能 · "会" Indicating Possibility

助动词"会"用在句中，表示可能，通常用于未发生的事件中。
The auxiliary verb "会" indicates possibility, usually for something that hasn't happened yet.

会 + V + ……（的）
你穿得那么少，会感冒的。
You're wearing so little — you'll catch a cold.
别担心，我会照顾好自己。
Don't worry, I'll take care of myself.
你不给他打电话吗？他会不高兴的。
You're not going to call him? He'll be unhappy.
喝杯热茶会很舒服。
A cup of hot tea will feel great.

她总是笑着跟客人说话

Text 1 · 在教室
In the classroom
小明
这是你们比赛的照片吗？

小明

At the entrance of a supermarket

Drinking coffee while eating cake — perfect!

汉语中用"又 Adj₁ + 又 Adj₂"描述人或事物同时具有两个特点。比如"又高又漂亮"表示很高，也很漂亮。
"又 Adj₁ + 又 Adj₂" describes two qualities existing in the same person or thing (e.g., "又高又漂亮" = both tall and pretty).

2动作的伴随 · The Accompanying Action

用"V₁着(O₁) + V₂(O₂)"表示两个动作同时进行，V₁表示伴随，是 V₂ 的状态或者进行的方式。比如"他们站着聊天儿"表示"他们在聊天儿"，方式是"站着"，不是"坐着"。
"V₁着(O₁) + V₂(O₂)" indicates two simultaneous actions, with V₁ being the accompanying state or manner of V₂.

🏯 Common Saying · 俗语
五十步笑百步Wǔshí bù xiào bǎi bù
"五十步笑百步"这句话来源于中国古代的一个故事。从前有两个士兵,打仗失败往回跑,一个人跑了五十步,另一个跑了一百步,跑了五十步的就笑话跑了一百步的,说他胆小。其实两人都是逃跑,只是跑得远近不同。这句话用来批评那些跟别人有一样的缺点,却因程度轻而笑话别人的人。
"五十步笑百步" originates from a story of ancient China. There were two soldiers who fled from a lost battle. One had run 50 steps backward, and the other 100 steps. The one who had retreated 50 steps laughed at the one who had retreated 100 steps, saying he was a coward. The truth is both of them were running away — the only difference lying in how far they'd run. Now it's used metaphorically to criticize people who have the same problems as those they laugh at, only to a lesser degree.

1可能补语 · Complements of Possibility (V 得/不)

汉语中用"V 得 + Complements"或"V 不 + Complements"表示能否实现某种结果或达到某种目的。"V 得 + C"是肯定形式,"V 不 + C"是否定形式。"得"后的补语常常是结果补语、趋向补语、一些形容词或动词。
"V 得 + C" (positive) / "V 不 + C" (negative) indicate whether a result can be obtained. Complements are usually result/direction complements, or certain adjectives or verbs.

3"刚" vs "刚才" · Comparison

"刚"是副词,"刚才"是名词。都表示动作发生的时间不长,但是"刚才"常常表示几分钟以前,"刚"表示的时间短,是对说话人来说的,可以是几分钟、几天甚至几个月。比如:"我刚来中国两个月",表示对说话人来说,两个月时间很短。"刚"只能用在动词前,"刚才"可以用在动词前,也可以放在句子前边。
"刚" is an adverb; "刚才" is a noun. Both indicate recent action. "刚才" usually means a few minutes ago; "刚" can mean minutes, days, or even months from the speaker's perspective. "刚" only goes before the verb; "刚才" can go before the verb or at the start of a sentence.

刚 + V　｜　刚才（+ S）+ V
爸爸刚出去。 / 爸爸刚才出去了。
Dad just went out. / Dad went out just a moment ago.
我刚喝完一杯咖啡。 / 刚才我喝了一杯咖啡。
I just finished a cup of coffee. / I had a cup of coffee just now.
我刚放在桌子上,现在就没了。
I just put it on the table, and now it's gone.
眼镜周明刚才放在桌子上了。
Zhou Ming just put the glasses on the table a moment ago.
儿子刚做完作业。 / 刚才儿子在做作业。
My son just finished homework. / My son was doing homework just now.

1时段的表达 · To Express a Period of Time

时段是指动作或状态持续的时间,一般由时量补语充当,如"一刻钟、半个小时、两天、三个月"等。
A period of time refers to the length of time an action or state lasts, usually indicated by a complement of duration (e.g., 一刻钟 "a quarter", 半个小时 "half an hour", 两天 "two days").

S + V + 了 + Duration + O　(动作持续的时间)
她工作了三年。
She has worked for three years.
我们坐了一个小时公共汽车。
We took the bus for an hour.
你怎么晚到了一刻钟？
How come you arrived 15 minutes late?
我们唱了两个小时歌。
We sang for two hours.

"S + V + 了 + Duration + O + 了"表示仍在进行的动作所持续的时间。比如"看了一个小时书了"表示看书的时间是一个小时,并且现在还在看书。
"S + V + 了 + Duration + O + 了" indicates the duration of an action that is still going on.

"一步走错步步错" means that in a chess game one wrong move will make all the moves after it wrong. In the metaphorical sense it indicates if we've made a wrong decision, then every decision following it would be wrong. This saying tells us to be prudent when doing something so as to avoid mistakes.

1"又"和"再"的比较 · Comparison of "又" and "再"

副词"又"和"再"都可以放在动词前边,表示动作或情况的重复出现。"又 + V"通常表示已发生了的重复动作或情况,"再 + V"表示还没发生的重复动作或情况。
Both "又" and "再" can precede verbs to show repetition. "又 + V" usually means the recurrence has already happened; "再 + V" means the recurrence has not yet happened.

又 + V (已发生)　｜　再 + V (还没发生)

"又"的例句:

上个星期我买了一条裤子,昨天又买了一条。
I bought a pair of pants last week, and bought another yesterday.
你上午已经喝了一杯咖啡,下午怎么又喝了一杯？
You already had a coffee this morning — why another this afternoon?
小刚,你前天迟到,昨天迟到,今天怎么又迟到了？
Xiaogang, you were late the day before yesterday, yesterday, how come you're late again today?

"再"的例句:

你只吃了一点儿饭,再吃一点儿吧。
You only ate a little — have some more.
家里只有一个面包了,我们再买一些吧。
There's only one loaf left — let's buy some more.
那个饭馆我昨天去了一次,明天还想再去一次。
I went to that restaurant yesterday; I want to go again tomorrow.
昨天去看了看,今天又去看了看,明天还要再去看看。
Went yesterday, went again today, will go again tomorrow.
2疑问代词活用 1 · Flexible Use of Interrogative Pronouns

汉语中,用"就"连接两个相同的疑问代词,表达不确指的人、事物或方式。疑问代词在句中出现两次,前后所指的对象相同。当前后两个分句的主语相同时,后一个分句中的主语可以省略;如果两个分句的主语不同,第二个主语要放在"就"的前边。
"就" connects two same interrogative pronouns to indicate an uncertain person, thing or manner. Same subject: the 2nd can be left out. Different subjects: 2nd subject goes right before 就.

WH + (S₂) + 就 + WH　(WH = 什么/哪儿/谁/哪天…)
什么东西便宜我就买什么。
Whatever is cheap, I'll buy.
你哪天有时间就哪天来我家吧。
Come to my place whenever you have time.
谁喜欢他他就喜欢谁。
He likes whoever likes him.
你坐哪儿我就坐哪儿。
I'll sit wherever you sit.
你去哪儿我就去哪儿。
I'll go wherever you go.
我想吃什么就做什么,想吃多少就做多少。
I cook whatever I want, however much I want.

1"越 A 越 B" 结构 · The Structure "越A越B"

"越 A 越 B"表示 B 随着 A 的变化而变化。A 和 B 可以是动词或形容词。
"越 A 越 B" (the more A, the more B) indicates B changes along with A.

越 + A + 越 + B
雨越下越大。
The rain is falling harder and harder.
你的中文越说越好。
Your Chinese is getting better and better.
越往南,天气越热。
The further south, the hotter the weather.
山越高,路越难走。
The higher the mountain, the harder the road.
休息得越多,好得越快。
The more you rest, the faster you recover.
你总是吃甜的东西,会越吃越胖。
If you keep eating sweets, you'll get fatter.

注意"越 A 越 B" 和"越来越 + Adj" 的区别:"越来越"表示程度随时间变化,"越A越B"表示两件事的程度同步变化。
Note: "越来越 + Adj" shows change over time; "越A越B" shows two things changing in sync.

2比较句 1:A 跟 B 一样 (+ Adj) · Comparative Sentences

"A 跟 B 一样"表示 A 和 B 两者相比较,结果相同。后边可以用形容词表示比较的某一方面,如"一样大"表示大小相同。否定式是在"一样"的前边加"不"。
"A 跟 B 一样" means A and B are the same after comparison. An adjective can follow to specify the aspect (e.g., 一样大 = "the same in size"). Negative: add 不 before 一样.

A 跟 B (不) 一样 (+ Adj)
这本书跟那本书一样。
This book is the same as that book.
儿子跟爸爸一样高。
The son is as tall as his dad.
这辆车的颜色跟那辆车不一样。
This car's color isn't the same as that car's.
她的汉语说得跟中国人一样好。
She speaks Chinese as well as a native.
你的眼睛怎么跟大熊猫一样了？

🏯 Common Saying · 俗语
三人行,必有我师Sān rén xíng, bì yǒu wǒ shī
"三人行,必有我师"的意思是三个人一起走路,里边一定有一个人可以做我的老师。这句话用来比喻别人身上可能有我们没有的优点,我们要学习别人身上的优点。
"三人行必有我师" means "When I walk with two others, there must be one whom I can learn from". In the metaphorical sense, it means everybody may have some merits that we lack, so we should learn from others.

✍️ Character Note · 汉字知识
形声字Pictophonetic Characters
现代汉语中,70% 左右的汉字都是形声字。形声字由形旁和声旁两部分组成。形旁提示汉字的意思,声旁提示汉字的发音。左右结构的形声字有两种:"左形右声"和"右形左声"。
Around 70% of modern Chinese characters are pictophonetic (combining a semantic component that hints at meaning and a phonetic component that hints at pronunciation). Left-right pictophonetic characters come in two types: semantic-left / phonetic-right, and semantic-right / phonetic-left.

1比较句 2:A 比 B + Adj + 一点儿/一些/得多/多了 · Comparative Sentences

"一点儿""一些""得多""多了"等词可以用于"比"字句中形容词的后边,表示事物之间的差别程度。"一点儿"和"一些"表示差别的程度不大,"得多"和"多了"表示差别的程度很大。
These words can follow an adjective in a 比-comparison, indicating degree of difference. "一点儿/一些" = slight difference; "得多/多了" = big difference.

A 比 B + Adj + 一点儿 / 一些 / 得多 / 多了

我今天起得比昨天晚一些。
I got up a bit later today than yesterday.
今天的作业比昨天多得多。
Today's homework is much more than yesterday's.
数学比历史难多了。
Maths is much harder than history.

🏯 Common Saying · 俗语
不可同日而语Bù kě tóng rì ér yǔ
"不可同日而语"的意思是不能放在同一个时间谈论,用来比喻差别很大,不能放在一起比较。例如:通信技术进步太快了,过去跟现在不可同日而语。
"不可同日而语", literally "(two things) cannot be mentioned in the same breath", is used metaphorically to mean that there is no comparison between two people or two things because of the significant difference. For instance, in terms of science and technology, there is no comparison between the past and the present.
