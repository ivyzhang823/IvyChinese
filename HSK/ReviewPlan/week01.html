Lesson 9 复习
1
中文 zhōng wén n.
Chinese Language
中 = 中国  ·  文 = 文字 wordage
拓展 Extension
也可以说 汉语 hàn yǔ（Chinese Language）
汉 = 汉族 ethnic Han  ·  语 = 语言 language
2
班 bān n.
class
拓展 Extension
班 also means work
上班 shàng bān — to go to the office
下班 xià bān — get off work
加班 jiā bān — work overtime（加 = plus）
3
一样 yí yàng adj.
same; as…as…
4
最后 zuì hòu n.
the last (one); finally
时间顺序词 Sequence Words
先 xiān — firstly  →  然后 rán hòu — then  →  最后 — the last
5
放心 fàng xīn v.
to ease one's mind; to rest assured
拓展
放心 = 别担心 don't worry
6
担心 dān xīn v.
to worry
7
比较 bǐ jiào adv. / v.
① fairly, rather, relatively    ② to compare
8
一定 yí dìng adv.
definitely; certainly; must
定 = stand still, no changing
拓展
一定 = only one & no changing → must
确定 què dìng — 确 = check；check & no changing → make sure
9
了解 liǎo jiě v.
to know; to understand
相关词 Related
理解 lǐ jiě  ·  明白 míng bai  ·  清楚 qīng chǔ
10
中间 zhōng jiān n.
middle; in between
中 = center  ·  间 = between / space / measure word for rooms
11
参加 cān jiā v.
to participate; to join
用于 events / matches / activities
12
影响 yǐng xiǎng n.
influence; effect; impact
A 跟 B 一样（+ adj.）
A and B are the same (in some way)
跟 = 和。加形容词说明哪方面一样。
我的蛋糕和Anna的蛋糕一样甜。
My cake and Anna's cake are equally sweet.
这件衬衫跟那件一样贵。
This shirt is as expensive as that one.
越 A 越 B
The more A, the more B — B changes with A
雨越下越大。
The more it rains, the heavier it gets.
你的中文越说越好。
The more you speak Chinese, the better it gets.
越往南，天气越热。
The further south you go, the hotter it gets.
山越高，路越难走。
The higher the mountain, the harder the road.
越来越……
more and more…
天气越来越冷了。
The weather is getting colder and colder.
我越来越喜欢中文。
I like Chinese more and more.
